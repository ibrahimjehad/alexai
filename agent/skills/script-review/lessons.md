# Script lessons (all projects)

Every note the user gives on a script becomes a lesson here, so the writer never repeats the mistake. The writer reads this file before drafting and before every rewrite; the reviewers fail any line that breaks a lesson.

**How to add one:** next free id, the rule in one sentence, why, then the bad line and the better line exactly as the user gave or approved them. Business-specific lessons (offer, refund, prices) go in `projects/<id>/scripts/lessons.md` instead.

## LES-001 · White Arabic, not fusha, not Gulf
Write light spoken Levantine (white Arabic) that a presenter from Syria, Jordan, Palestine or Lebanon says naturally and Gulf + Levant viewers understand at once.
- Why: the user rejected a Gulf-dialect version, then a fusha version ("better, but this is fusha"). (2026-11-20)

| Bad | Why | Better |
|---|---|---|
| المقاطع المسجّلة والمجموعات لا ترى صفقتك. | Fusha, translated from English | (see LES-002 for the full idea) |
| لدينا وعدان مكتوبان. | Fusha | (see LES-004) |
| شنو يصير إذا ما عجبني؟ | Gulf dialect | شو بيصير إذا ما ناسبني؟ |
| حطّ مصاري حقيقية | "مصاري" is Levant-only | حطّ فلوس حقيقية |

## LES-002 · Say the whole idea, with its reason, for a complete beginner
The viewer has never traded. Every line says its full point plainly: the reason behind a claim, the fact instead of a metaphor, the named danger behind a warning, the need a problem creates. Never hint.
- Why: four lines were rejected as unclear for beginners. (2026-11-20)

| Bad | What was missing | Better (user approved) |
|---|---|---|
| ما حدا بيضمن ربح بالسوق. | Why nobody can promise profit | لأنه الأسعار بتطلع وبتنزل، وما حدا بيقدر يضمنلك إنك رح تربح. |
| الفيديو المسجّل ما بيعرف شو عم تعمل. | Personification; the fact isn't said | إذا الكورس كله فيديوهات مسجّلة، ما في حدا عم يشوف صفقاتك ولا يقلّك إذا عم تتداول صح أو لأ. |
| إذا أول شي طلبوا منك تحوّل فلوس، وقّف واسأل ليش. | The warning sign isn't named (pushed to open a real account and deposit before practising) | إذا أول شي طلبوه منك إنك تفتح حساب حقيقي وتحط فلوس، قبل ما تتمرّن ولا مرة، وقّف واسألهم ليش مستعجلين. |
| لأنه متل ما شفنا مع طلابنا، أصعب مرحلة هي لما تتداول لحالك. | The need it creates (a mentor with you) | لأنه أصعب مرحلة هي لما تكمّل وتتداول لحالك، وهون بتحتاج مدرّب يكون معك ويقلّك إذا كل شي ماشي صح أو لأ. |

## LES-003 · The Point must carry the message we want to land, not just the question
Each line's Point states what the viewer should conclude, including our advantage when the line sets it up. A question that only asks ("what happens to my money?") without landing our answer ("we give it back; most don't") fails, even if every word is clear.
- Why: the user said the last part "is not clear at all": the idea to deliver was "if the course doesn't fit you, you get your money back; others won't do this, we will", and it never came through. (2026-11-20)

## LES-004 · Say the benefit in plain words, not as a formal label
Name what we do for the viewer ("بنضمنلك… بنرجعلك فلوسك"), not a label for it ("وعدين مكتوبين", "ضمانين").
- Why: user note: "we don't use وعدين مكتوبين". (2026-11-20)

| Bad | Better |
|---|---|
| وعالسؤال الخامس عنّا وعدين مكتوبين، بنشرحهم بأول درس. | إحنا بنضمنلك فلوسك: … بنرجعلك فلوسك كاملة. (with the project's refund condition, see the project lessons) |

## LES-005 · Length: a complete idea beats a short one
About 110 seconds (about 280 words) for a 5-point Reel was accepted. Don't cut a reason or explanation to save time; only cut filler and repetition.
- Why: user: "I am fine with the length of the current video." (2026-11-20)
