## Definitions

- **Engaged lead: a lead who shows interest in what you sell** (`leads-engaged-lead`) [leads, metrics] An engaged lead is a contactable person who has shown interest in your stuff; engaged leads are the true output of advertising.
- **Grand Slam Offer** (`offers-grand-slam-offer`) [offers, value, pricing] An offer so differentiated it cannot be compared to anything else on the market, so the prospect decides between you and nothing.
- **Gross profit** (`offers-gross-profit`) [metrics, pricing] Gross profit is revenue minus the direct cost of servicing one additional customer.
- **Lead magnet: a complete solution to a narrow problem that reveals the next one** (`leads-lead-magnet`) [lead-magnets, leads] A lead magnet is a low-cost or free offer that fully solves one narrow problem and, once solved, reveals another problem your core offer sol
- **Lead: a person you can contact** (`leads-lead-definition`) [leads] A lead is simply a person you can contact, nothing more.
- **Lifetime value as lifetime gross profit (LTGP)** (`offers-lifetime-gross-profit`) [metrics, pricing] Hormozi defines lifetime value as the gross profit a customer generates over their whole relationship with you (LTGP), not their total reven
- **Money model: a deliberate sequence of offers** (`models-money-model-definition`) [money-models, offers] A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers (what you offer, when, and how) designed to increase how many customers you get, how much t
- **Preference objection: "I want your outcome, my way"** (`sales-preference-objection`) [sales, objections] Any objection about features (no one-on-one calls, no email support, no PDF) is a preference objection: they want your outcome delivered the
- **Rough price bands: impulse, high ticket, and B2B** (`sales-high-ticket-price-benchmarks`) [pricing, sales] Hormozi's rules of thumb for services: consumer impulse ~$500-600; consumer high ticket ~$3,000-10,000; small-business cheap ~$400-800/month
- **The five types of sales obstacles** (`sales-five-objection-categories`) [sales, objections, closing] Anything other than a yes after you state the price is an obstacle, and almost all of them fall into five types.
- **Three-term contingency: before, during, after as a framework maker** (`sales-three-term-contingency`) [sales, scripts] Almost any process breaks into something before, something during and something after, which gives you an instant three-step framework.
- **Warm vs. cold audiences** (`leads-warm-and-cold-audiences`) [leads, warm-outreach, cold-outreach] A warm audience has given you permission to contact them; a cold audience has not.
- **What an offer is (and the offer-quality ladder)** (`offers-offer-definition`) [offers] An offer is the goods and services you agree to provide, how you accept payment, and the terms of the agreement.
