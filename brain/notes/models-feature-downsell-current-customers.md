When a customer is not using a feature they pay for, offer a lower price for only what they use, before they cancel.

**Why.** Customers who use everything they pay for stay longer. Proactive outreach either reactivates the feature ("I want to keep it") or earns gratitude for a better deal. It takes work but beats a cancellation.

**Evidence.** Hormozi says customers downsold into a package built just for them have the second-highest lifetime value of all his customers: a product they like at a price they find fair keeps them paying.

**When to use.** Recurring/membership businesses; monthly usage reviews.
