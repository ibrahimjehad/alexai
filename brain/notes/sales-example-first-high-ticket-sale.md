Hormozi broke his own price ceiling by quoting a number he expected to be refused.

He was used to selling $500, 16-week training packages and had eight gyms to call to cancel done-for-you launches. The first owner, a referral, insisted. Hoping he'd say no, Hormozi offered to show him the system without flying out, and said $6,000 (12x his usual price). The owner said "Done." The next call he said $8,000, then $10,000; by day's end he had collected about $60,000, before the product even existed. He calls it the moment his career "elevated" and the seed of licensing his gym system. Lesson: the ceiling was his belief, not the buyer's wallet ([sales-stop-selling-from-your-wallet]).
