Warm outreach means personally contacting people who already know you; it's the cheapest, easiest and most reliable way to find your first customers, and most businesses skip it.

## The idea
- Channels: calls, texts, emails, DMs, voicemails, physical mail.
- You promote either a lead magnet or your core offer.
- Everyone has a warm audience: phone contacts, email contacts and social connections.
- It yields few engaged leads per hour because every message is personal, but it is reliable. It works with 100 contacts or 1 million; as you grow, automation and employees scale it (advanced use: re-engaging past customers, launching new product lines).
- Minimum setup to start a business: a tax ID, a bank account, a way to take payments, and a way to communicate with people. The last one is the most important because it's how you get leads.

## Two limitations
1. **Time:** early on, getting customers should take most of your day (4+ hours, first thing each morning).
2. **People who know you:** you eventually run out. That's when you add free content (grows the warm audience) and then cold outreach.

## When to use
The first lead method for anyone with zero to few customers, any new product, or a business under roughly $500K/year. Use the ten-step process.
