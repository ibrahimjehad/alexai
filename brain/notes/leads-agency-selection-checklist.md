Shortlist agencies using ten traits the good ones shared, and talk to 5–10 of them before deciding.

## Ten signs of a good agency
1. Someone you know got good results with them (word-of-mouth agencies beat ones found via their own ads/outreach — the best rely on referrals).
2. Prominent companies you recognize got good results.
3. A waiting list (demand exceeds supply).
4. A clear sales process that sets realistic expectations.
5. No short-term hacks; they talk long-term strategy with clear timelines for setup, scaling and results.
6. They say exactly what they need from you, when, and how they'll use it.
7. They propose a regular meeting schedule and several ways to update you.
8. Simple-language updates with clear tracking of cost vs results.
9. A good offer by the value equation: the dream outcome promised; how many people like you they've gotten there; how long it takes; what effort/sacrifice they require and whether you can sustain it.
10. They're expensive. All good agencies are expensive; not all expensive agencies are good.

## Process tips
- Talk to 5–10 agencies first; you'll learn a lot and the gap between good and bad becomes obvious.
- If you like the people but they don't fit, ask them to refer you to another agency — good specialists refer to other good specialists.
- Even if one agrees to your terms, compare a few more against the checklist before choosing.

## When to use
Vetting any outside marketing/lead-generation provider.
