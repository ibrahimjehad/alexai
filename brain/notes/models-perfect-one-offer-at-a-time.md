Do not implement a whole money model at once; get one offer working reliably, then automatic, before adding the next.

**Rules.**
- Stick to your stage. Pick one offer, run it until it works reliably, repeat it until it is automatic, then move on.
- Measure progress in quarters, not weeks. "Patience is still the fastest way to get to your goal."
- Build it right or build it again (and again); rebuilding, however fast, takes longer than building right the first time.
- Trying the full model at once will break the business.

**When to use.** When a client wants to launch many offers simultaneously.
