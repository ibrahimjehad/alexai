Bucket the questions prospects already ask, then answer them live at the end of the pitch in order of how often they come up, for as long as people stay on.

**Steps.**
1. Categorise past DM, email and call questions into buckets (usually six to nine; "there are no new questions").
2. Rank by share: e.g., the top question covers ~40% of people, the next ~25%, then the long tail.
3. After the offer, put each question on screen and answer it.
4. Keep going while people stay; answer the most common first.

**Why.** Unanswered FAQs are why buyers DM for one-on-one calls, which eats the founder's time. Handle them up front and more people buy straight from checkout.

**When to use.** Any webinar, launch or group call; also as raw material for objection content ([sales-objection-content-library]).
