Excluding ad spend, the cost of employee-driven lead getting is mostly payroll, so cost per lead = payroll ÷ engaged leads, and CAC follows from your close rate.

## Formulas
- Cost per engaged lead = total payroll ÷ total engaged leads.
- CAC = cost per lead × engaged leads needed per customer.
- Compare to LTGP: e.g. $100k payroll ÷ 1,000 leads = $100/lead; 1 in 10 closes → CAC $1,000; LTGP $4,000 → 4:1.
- Real example: acquisition.com got ~30,000 leads/month from content alone with a ~$100k/month content team → ~$3.33 per engaged lead.

## Which employees to focus on (diagnostic)
- CAC within 3x industry average → fine; work on raising LTGP.
- CAC above 3x → sales or advertising problem. Ask: "Do my engaged leads have the problem I solve and the money to spend?"
  - No → unqualified leads = advertising problem.
  - Yes and buying, but too few → advertising (volume) problem.
  - Yes but not buying → sales problem.
- Don't fire salespeople for an advertising problem, or advertisers for a sales problem.
- Rule of thumb: total cost to get a customer should be at most one-third of lifetime gross profit (≥3:1).

## When to use
Evaluating any in-house lead-getting team or content operation.
