Advertise to potential affiliates exactly like customers — call out, value, CTA — but the product you're selling them is a new way to make money.

## Call-out options
- The affiliate business owners themselves ("attention spot owners").
- The affiliate's customers ("Do you work with busy professionals who spend all day in meetings?").
- The results the affiliate promises ("To the heroes who heal the stress of others").
- The products/services they deliver ("If you sell lotions or scented oils, this is for you").
- Your own customers ("Do you know anyone who owns a spa?").

## Value template (fill in the blanks)
- **Dream outcome:** make more money from your current customers and get more leads than with your current offer.
- **Likelihood:** high chance of working because your customers already want this product.
- **Effort/sacrifice:** no need to build, deliver or support the product.
- **Time delay:** you can start selling tomorrow.

## When to use
Recruiting affiliates (step two of the six-step affiliate process).
