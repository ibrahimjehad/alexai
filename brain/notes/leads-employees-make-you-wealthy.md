A business that depends on you is a high-paying job worth little to anyone else; the same profits produced without you become a sellable asset worth many times profit.

## Two scenarios
1. $5M revenue / $2M profit, owner working around the clock: a high-paying job; nearly unsellable because it only makes money with you in it.
2. Same numbers, runs without you: you get your time back (to grow, acquire, or vacation) and the business becomes a good investment for others. $2M of profit (especially if growing) could easily be worth ~$10M.
Learning to get other people to do the work can make a ~$10M difference in net worth.

## Maxim
"You get rich from what you make; you become wealthy from what you own."

## When to use
Motivating an owner to hire and delegate lead getting; framing exit value.
