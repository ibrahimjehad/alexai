The whole book as a build sequence for a high-margin, decommoditized Grand Slam Offer.

1. Do not be a commodity: differentiate into a category of one.
2. Pick a normal or growing market, and niche down ("riches are in the niches").
3. Charge a lot of money (premium pricing).
4. Justify it with the four value drivers (value equation).
5. Create the value offer in five steps (dream outcome, problems, solutions, delivery vehicles, trim and stack).
6. Stack the value, deliver it profitably.
7. Shift the demand curve with scarcity.
8. Lower the action threshold with urgency.
9. Raise demand with strategically presented bonuses.
10. Reverse risk with a creative guarantee.
11. Name it so it resonates with the avatar (MAGIC).

The result is the first building block of an acquisition machine; lead generation (the next book) comes after.

**When to use.** As the audit checklist and agenda for any offer-building session: walk a client's offer through all 11 and fix the weakest link first.
