Effort and sacrifice are the tangible and intangible costs the buyer bears beyond the price; reducing them (or how they feel) raises value sharply.

Fitness vs liposuction for the same outcome:
- Fitness: waking 1-2 hours earlier, 5-10 hours a week, giving up favourite foods, hunger, soreness, embarrassment, injury risk, meal prep, new and pricier groceries, new clothes, fear of regaining weight.
- Liposuction: fall asleep, wake up thin, sore for 2-4 weeks.
That gap is why a trainer struggles to sell at a tenth or hundredth of the surgery price. Surgeons' ads attack exactly these sacrifices.

**Done-for-you.** It costs more than DIY because the buyer skips the effort, and because people believe an expert doing it is more likely to succeed (a likelihood gain too).

**When to use.** List every effort and sacrifice the customer faces, then remove, automate or do as many as you can for them.
