Making each customer more valuable, faster, compounds: you can afford more customers and recycle cash sooner.

**The argument.** More offers that people buy means more money per customer. More money per customer lets you buy more customers. Faster payment lets you buy them sooner while staying profitable. So doubling customer value can roughly mean twice the money per customer, twice as many customers, at twice the speed: about 2 x 2 x 2 = 8x growth. Tripling gives about 3 x 3 x 3 = 27x.

**Caveat.** This is an illustrative multiplier, not a law; it assumes the extra cash is reinvested into acquisition and that more customers are available.

**When to use.** To motivate owners to work on monetization (upsells, downsells, continuity) instead of only chasing cheaper leads.
