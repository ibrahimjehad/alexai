Hire an agency with an explicit purpose and deadline — teach you and your team how they do it — then drop to cheaper consulting once your team beats them.

## When to hire an agency
- Good agencies cost money; with no money, learn by trial and error.
- With money, use agencies for two things: learning **new methods** (new ways to do content, outreach or ads) and learning **new platforms**. They've already made the big mistakes, so you skip to making money, and you invest in skills you couldn't otherwise learn without costly trial and error.
- Exception: owners who never want to learn can just use agencies to do the work.

## Opening agreement (paraphrased template)
"I want to do what you do in my business but don't know how. I'd like to work with you for [six months] to learn how you do it, and I'll pay extra for you to explain why you make each decision and the steps behind it. Once I understand it, I'll train my team; when they can do it well enough, I'd like to switch to a lower-cost consulting arrangement so you can still help if we hit problems. Are you opposed to this?"
Most agencies agree; if not, move on — but be willing to negotiate; at some price it works for both.

## Two-agency play for a new platform
Hire a "good enough" agency to learn the ropes, then an elite one (Hormozi paid ~4x more for YouTube) to learn to maximize it. When your videos beat theirs, drop to consulting.

## Steps
1. Decide whether an agency makes sense now.
2. Talk to many agencies to learn the market. Don't be cheap.
3. Use the agreement framework above.
4. Set a clear deadline to force you and your team to learn.
5. Run both teams in parallel until yours regularly beats theirs (budget for paying both for a while).
6. Switch to discounted consulting until you feel you're teaching them, then end it and reinvest the money into scaling.

## Why it works
Better short-term results (they know more) plus better long-term results (you own the skill), and you get the agency's best reps while the arrangement lasts. Hormozi's time to get his team as good as an agency fell from ~1 year to 6 months or less.

## When to use
Entering a new platform or lead method with budget but no in-house expertise.
