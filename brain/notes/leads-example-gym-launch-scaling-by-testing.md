After hearing the "test budget" advice in 2017, Hormozi tripled ad spend and grew Gym Launch roughly tenfold through a chain of tests, each doubled down on until it broke.

## Sequence
- Tripled the ad budget the next week → $400k (June) to $780k (July).
- CAC rose too high → tested new audiences; most failed, one hit → $1.2M then $1.5M/month.
- Realized engaged leads weren't followed up: email failed, phone calls failed, text blasts worked → $1.8M.
- Tested far more paid ads with higher production value → $2.5M+/month.
- Launched the affiliate program → +$1.5M/month, past $4M/month. (The portfolio later did $16M+/month.)

## Lesson
Test until something works, take massive action and double down until it breaks, then test for the next constraint (More-Better-New in practice).
