A business can only grow by getting more customers, raising their average purchase value, or getting them to buy more times.

**Grow or die.** Hormozi treats maintenance as a myth: markets grow (he cites roughly 9% per year for the stock market), so standing still is falling behind; in a fast-growing market you may need 20-30% a year just to keep pace.

**The levers.**
1. Get more customers.
2. Increase average purchase value (profit per purchase).
3. Increase number of purchases (frequency/retention).
Levers 2 and 3 are both "increase each customer's value", so at the simplest level there are two ways: more customers, more value per customer.

**Ceiling math.** Max monthly revenue = new customers per month x lifetime value. 10 new clients/month x $1,000 lifetime value caps at $10,000/month. To grow you must raise one factor while holding margins. A Grand Slam Offer helps all three levers at once.

**When to use.** When diagnosing growth: compute the ceiling, then decide which lever is cheapest to move.
