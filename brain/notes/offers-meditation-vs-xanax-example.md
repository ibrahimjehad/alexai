Two vehicles with the same dream outcome (relaxation, less anxiety) score very differently on the other value drivers, which explains their market sizes.

Binary scoring (1 = driver delivered):
- Meditation: dream outcome 1; likelihood 0 (most doubt they will stick with it); time delay 0.5 (some immediate benefit, long-term results slow); effort 0 (discomfort, feeling like you fail, daily time). Total 1.5/4.
- Xanax: outcome 1; likelihood 1; time delay 1 (about 15 minutes); effort 1 (swallow a pill). Total 4/4.
Hence a multi-billion-dollar drug and few large meditation businesses. Similarly, Hormozi cites supplements (about $123B) at roughly twice health clubs (about $62B): people will pay $200 for pills and shakes but resist a $29/month gym.

**Lesson.** Perceived value is not the same as what is good for people. Monetize what people value in order to deliver what they need: "you can either be right or you can be rich".

**When to use.** As a template: score your offer and a competitor 0/1 on each driver to see where to win.
