Make self-checkout the cheaper, bonus-included path and charge a premium if the buyer needs a personal call, so the default is to buy without taking your time.

**Example.** Checkout price $2,500 with a bonus for buying during the live session; $3,000 and no bonus if they want to talk to the founder first, framed honestly: "I can't physically talk to everyone; if I consulted hourly it'd be $500, so that's what the call adds." On a call the founder can still say "I'm not supposed to, but I'll give you the bonus."

**Logic.** The goal is that most people don't pay the extra; if lots do, raise prices. Either way you stop doing unpaid one-on-one selling.

**Related pricing rule.** One-on-one tiers must be priced as a real step up: a $150/month team one-on-one add-on at roughly twice the group price "doesn't math"; it should be about five times (or be removed), per [sales-price-tier-ladder].

**When to use.** Group programs, courses and launches where buyers habitually DM or call before buying.
