Deposit goodwill with rewarding content before withdrawing it with asks; to grow, over-give, and ideally keep giving publicly until people ask privately to buy.

## Give-to-ask benchmarks
- Gary Vaynerchuk's "jab, jab, jab, right hook" popularised giving many times before asking.
- **TV:** ~13 minutes of ads per 60 minutes, so ~3.5:1 give-to-ask. **Facebook:** ~4 posts per ad, ~4:1.
- These mature platforms maximise monetisation over growth, so ~3-4:1 is roughly the *minimum* ratio you can sustain without shrinking. Growing platforms show lots of content and almost no ads. To grow, model them: give far more than you ask.

## Give until they ask (Hormozi's version)
- If you give enough, people feel uncomfortable receiving without giving back, so they visit your site, DM and email asking to buy.
- **Give in public, ask in private:** the audience self-selects when ready. These buyers are the best customers (natural givers who feel they get the better deal), and growth never slows.
- The moment you start asking is the moment you choose to slow growth; the more patient you are, the more you get when you finally ask. Most people fail because they won't wait 12 months.

## When to use
Setting a content monetisation policy. Default to give-until-they-ask; if cash is needed now, use integrated or intermittent asks while keeping the ratio high and watching growth.
