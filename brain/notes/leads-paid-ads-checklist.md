A filled-in planning card for a paid-ads program, answering who/what/where/when/why/how/how much/how many/how long.

## Checklist
- **Who:** yourself.
- **What:** your offer.
- **Where:** any platform or audience you can buy access to.
- **To whom:** targeted audience or lookalike audience.
- **When:** every day, seven days a week.
- **Why:** get engaged leads to sell.
- **How:** call-out + what/who/when value + CTA.
- **How much:** a learning budget first, then reverse the budget from your sales goal.
- **How many:** 30+ call-outs x 10 main ads.
- **How long:** as long as it takes.

## When to use
Turning the paid-ads method into an action plan for a specific business.
