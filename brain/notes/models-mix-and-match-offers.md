Offers can be used anywhere in the sequence: upsell tactics in attraction offers, a downsell process behind every offer, continuity to attract.

**Advice.** Start the way the book suggests; as you get better, experiment. Hormozi learned many of these offers from people who used them differently than he does.

**When to use.** When a client's situation does not fit the default sequence.
