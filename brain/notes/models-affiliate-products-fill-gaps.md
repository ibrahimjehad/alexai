Sell someone else's product for a commission to add offers without building or delivering anything.

**Who it suits.** A new business with nothing to sell; a business with a single offer; even a $100M business wanting more profit without operational headache.

**Examples.**
- Service: a dental marketing agency refers its dentist clients to a braces manufacturer and earns a commission per dentist.
- Local business: a massage therapist sells home massage tools, bands and medicine balls; the customer pays the therapist and the supplier ships directly.
- Digital: an educator recommends a customer-service software and earns a commission per sign-up.

**When to use.** When a money model lacks an upsell or continuity offer and you cannot (or should not yet) build one.
