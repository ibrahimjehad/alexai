Write down every obstacle the customer will face, in the order they meet it, in insane detail; each one is an opportunity to add value.

**How.**
1. List each core thing the customer must do to reach the outcome (weight loss: buy healthy food, cook it, eat it, exercise...).
2. Think about what happens immediately before and after they use your product: what is the next thing they need help with?
3. For each action, write the reasons they could fail to do it or keep doing it, prompted by the four value drivers:
   - Dream outcome: "this won't be financially worth it."
   - Likelihood: "it won't work for me, I won't stick with it, outside factors will get in the way" (the most service-specific bucket).
   - Effort and sacrifice: "too hard, confusing, I won't like it, I'll be bad at it."
   - Time: "takes too long, I'm too busy, not convenient."
Four actions x four drivers x 2-4 sub-problems quickly gives 32-64 problems. That is good news: more problems means more to solve and more value to stack. Do not let the buckets constrain you.

**When to use.** Step 2 of offer creation, and to prepare objection handling.
