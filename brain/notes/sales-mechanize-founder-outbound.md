If one person's outbound already works, document it in fine detail and hire many people to do it, before trying something new like brand awareness.

**The case.** A B2B frozen cocktail-mix company (~$3.5M/year, selling to clubs, resorts, bars) grew on the founder's father's 20 years of relationships and word of mouth, and wondered whether to "grow awareness". Hormozi: mechanise the existing outbound motion and get ten people doing it.

**Break it into ~50 pieces.** Not "how do you do outbound?" but: initial contact, first message, second message, the script, the opener, the offer that entices them, how to widen the gap between where they are and where they want to be. Then get the decision maker to taste the product (trade shows and conferences suit a product that sells once tasted).

**Rule.** Between more of a proven thing and a brand-new thing you've never done, choose more of the proven thing.

**When to use.** Founder- or relationship-led sales that have stalled at the founder's capacity.
