The dream outcome is the experience the prospect pictures at their destination; you channel existing desire rather than create it.

It is the gap between their current reality and their dreams. Your job is to reflect it back accurately so they feel understood and explain how your vehicle gets them there.

**Status underneath.** Surface desires (to look beautiful, be respected, be loved) usually rest on a deeper desire for status, meaning relative standing in the eyes of others. Many vehicles serve the same desire (makeup, serums, surgery and fitness all serve "look beautiful"). Prospects value whole categories more when that category raises their status more (Hormozi argues many men value money-making offers over appearance offers for this reason). Russell Brunson's wife preferred a minivan over a Lamborghini because among her mom friends the minivan signalled "good mom", a status gain.

**Comparison rule.** Between two offers that deliver the same dream outcome, that driver cancels out and the other three drivers decide the value.

**When to use.** When naming the promise of an offer and writing its headline: state the destination, framed around the status it brings.
