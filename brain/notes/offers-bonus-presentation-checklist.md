Rules for building and presenting each bonus so it adds maximum perceived value.

1. Always offer bonuses (use the bundle from trim and stack).
2. Give each a special name with a benefit in the title.
3. For each, explain: (a) how it relates to their problem, (b) what it is, (c) how you discovered or built it, (d) how it makes their result faster or easier, with less effort or sacrifice (the value equation).
4. Provide proof it works: a stat, a past client or a personal experience.
5. Paint a vivid picture of their life after using it.
6. Always attach a price tag and justify it.
7. Tools and checklists beat extra trainings: less time and effort means more value.
8. Each bonus should address a specific concern about why they cannot or will not succeed, and prove that belief wrong.
9. A bonus can also solve the next problem they will logically face, before they meet it.
10. Total bonus value should exceed the core offer's value. This widens the gap and implies the core must be worth even more.
11. Add scarcity or urgency to the bonuses themselves:
    - Scarcity: bonuses available only through this program; or "three tickets left to my $5,000 event, one is yours if you buy".
    - Urgency: "buy today and I add this $1,000 bonus to reward action-takers". Scarcity bonuses are not time-bound; urgency bonuses are lost if they do not buy today.

**When to use.** When writing sales pages, decks or scripts that present an offer stack.
