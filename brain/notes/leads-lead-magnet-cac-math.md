Worked numbers showing why a lead magnet that costs money to deliver still lowers acquisition cost.

## Without a lead magnet (advertising the core offer)
- $10,000 profit per sale.
- $1,000 in ads to get one person on a call.
- Close 1 in 3, so **$3,000 to acquire a customer**.

## With a lead magnet
- Lead magnet costs $25 to deliver; free to them, so more engage.
- Ads now cost $75 to get someone on a call, so $100 per call all-in (10x more engaged leads for the same spend).
- 1 in 10 lead magnet takers buys, so 10 x $100 = **$1,000 to acquire a customer**: CAC down 3x, a 10:1 return on $10K profit.
- Same ad budget, triple the business.

## Lesson
Experienced operators spend on the lead magnet (e.g. $25 per person) to give far more value than competitors with a $0 budget. That wins more engaged leads and more sales. The goal is to "print money", not just earn a fair share.

## When to use
When a client resists spending on a lead magnet or free service. Rebuild this math with their own numbers (profit per sale, cost per call, close rates).
