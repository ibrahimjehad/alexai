A high-ticket seller offered a cheaper price without the money-back guarantee; many then chose full price to keep the guarantee.

**Script shape.** On a price objection: "If you don't want the option to get your money back, you can pay less, or keep the money-back guarantee. Which would you prefer?" Once they see what they give up, many say they would rather keep the guarantee.

**Numbers.** Before: 100 calls, 25 bought (25%). After: 35 bought the full-price offer and 40 took the no-guarantee downsell, 75% in total. Full-price buyers, total close rate and up-front cash all rose.

**Lesson.** People see a feature's value most clearly when it is taken away. If you have a guarantee, make removing it a step in your feature downsell. Complements [leads-prepay-guarantee] (guarantee only for paid-in-full buyers).
