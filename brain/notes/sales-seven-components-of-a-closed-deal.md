A deal closes only when seven things are true: four the seller needs from the buyer (BANT) and three the buyer needs from the seller (trust in product, salesperson and company).

**From the buyer (BANT, from enterprise software).**
1. **Budget** - they have the money.
2. **Authority** - they can make the decision for themselves or their company.
3. **Need** - they have a problem this solves (and the desire and ability to implement it).
4. **Timing** - they're willing to start now.
In enterprise SaaS a prospect isn't passed to an account executive until all four are met.

**From the seller ("three 10s", which Hormozi heard from Jordan Belfort and Grant Cardone).**
5. **Product** - 10/10 trust it will solve their problem.
6. **Salesperson** - 10/10 trust this person is honest, sharp and knows what they're talking about.
7. **Company** - 10/10 trust this is a business they feel good dealing with.

**Use.** The thresholds differ (a free trial vs a $1M/year contract) but the components don't. Screen the four buyer requirements before the call ([sales-bant-application-filter]) so the rep only has to earn the three 10s. Ep 305's stall questions (does it meet your needs, do you like us, do you have the funds) check the same ground at the close ([sales-think-about-it-three-questions]).

**When to use.** Designing qualification, lead hand-off rules and diagnosing lost deals by which component was missing.
