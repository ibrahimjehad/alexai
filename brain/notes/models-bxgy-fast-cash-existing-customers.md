Offer current recurring customers a prepay deal ("buy 10, get 2 free" at their current rate), capped at about 10% of the base, and keep making offers to people who prepay.

**Steps.**
1. Offer a bulk prepay with free units to existing recurring customers at their current price.
2. Cap takers at about 10% of the customer base: you get a cash pop while monthly recurring cash flow stays healthy.
3. Keep upselling prepaid customers. Owners often stop selling to people who prepaid; this is a mistake. In Hormozi's experience prepaid customers spend the most, and their wallets have refilled since they paid.

**When to use.** A recurring business that needs cash quickly without new acquisition.
