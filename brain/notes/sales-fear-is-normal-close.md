When a prospect says the amount scares them, agree that it should: fear is what normal people feel before a big decision, and not having the money is often the very problem you solve.

**Script.** "Man, this is a lot of money." -> "Of course you're supposed to be afraid. If you weren't afraid to spend this right now, I'd send you out the door. That just makes you like everybody else." B2B add-on (gym owners): "Virtually none of our clients had the money to pay us when they started, and the reason is they didn't have the systems we give them." Follow with "How else do you think we'd fulfil the promise I just made you?" when the price funds the delivery.

**Why.** It reframes fear from a warning sign into a sign they're taking it seriously, and links the shortage of money to the problem itself (see [sales-reason-is-the-reason-close]).

**When to use.** High-ticket closes where the prospect hesitates out of nerves rather than doubt about the product.
