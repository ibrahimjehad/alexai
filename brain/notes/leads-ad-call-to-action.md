Interested viewers have huge motivation for a tiny window, so tell them exactly what to do next and make it effortless.

## Rules
- Spell it out: click this button, call this number, reply "yes", go to this website, scan this QR code. Many ads still don't.
- Make it quick and easy: easy phone numbers, obvious buttons, short memorable URLs (acquisition.com/training, not a long string). Hormozi paid $370k for a one-word domain because he thinks most people undervalue easy domains.
- Assume the audience doesn't know you, what you do or how it works, is in a rush, and has a third-grade education.
- Layer urgency, scarcity and bonuses (from the engaging-leads material) to strengthen any CTA.

## When to use
Ending every ad or piece of content that should produce an engaged lead.
