Your best-performing free content usually makes your best paid ads, and customer user-generated content is a free, steady supply of ad creative.

## The idea
- If a free content piece generates sales or performs very well, nine times out of ten it makes a great paid ad. Promote proven organic winners.
- User-generated content (customer testimonials, reviews, posts using your product): if it performs as free content, it often makes killer ads.
- Build a system that encourages customers to post publicly — Hormozi's favorite way to get a steady stream of potential ads with no extra work.

## When to use
Choosing creative to test; reducing creative production cost.
