A good CTA has two parts: a clear instruction of what to do, and reasons to do it right now.

## 1. What to do
Call the number, click the button, book the call. Use clear, simple, direct language ("Call now", not "Don't delay"). Don't be clever, be clear.

## 2. Reasons to act now
Good reasons beat bad reasons, but any reason beats no reason, so stack as many as you honestly can.
- **Scarcity** (limited supply). The best scarcity is real: if you can't handle 1,000x customers tomorrow, you have a real limit (service capacity, onboarding, inventory, weekly slots). Advertise it.
  - "The best class times fill up fast. Call now to get yours."
  - "I can only take five people a week."
  - "One print run of this shirt, never reprinted."
- **Urgency** (limited time). Fewer hours to act means faster action. Use deadlines on promotions, discounts or bonuses ("ends Monday at midnight").
  - Hormozi's go-to: put time limits on **bonuses**, not prices. Build a handful of valuable bonuses and rotate them weekly; miss the week, miss the bonus. Urgency without changing pricing or capping sales.
- **"Fraternity party planner": make up a reason.** Fraternities invent reasons to party (Thirsty Thursday). The Harvard line-cutting study found any reason increases compliance and good reasons increase it more. Fill in the "because...": because it's my birthday, because mom knows best.

## When to use
Step 7 of the lead magnet process and at the end of every ad, post, email or outreach message. Repeat CTAs often.
