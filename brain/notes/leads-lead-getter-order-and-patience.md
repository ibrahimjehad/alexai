Each lead getter has a distinct strength and they tend to arrive in a natural order; whichever you pick, expect three to six months to crack a new lead source and don't switch after early losses.

## Strengths
- **Customer referrals:** biggest potential for low-cost exponential growth.
- **Employees:** under your direct influence; run the business on your behalf.
- **Agencies:** teach skills you keep forever and transfer to your team.
- **Affiliates:** once going, operate entirely on their own.

## Natural order
Solo owners usually get referrals first → build a team (employees) → seek professional help (agencies) → only after managing people inside the business, manage people outside it (affiliates).

## Patience rule
Getting-rich-quick obsession ensures it never happens; people try shortcuts for a decade when they should have picked one strategy and stuck with it for a decade. Losing early is normal. Hormozi expects 3–6 months to crack a new lead source himself — faster expectations are unreasonable.

## When to use
Prioritizing which lead getter to build next; setting expectations for a new channel.
