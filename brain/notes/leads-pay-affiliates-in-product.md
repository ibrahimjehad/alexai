Reward affiliate performance with your product: they value it at retail, it costs you only your cost.

## The idea
- Set sales tiers and bonus affiliates with product or retail credit; at low tiers you can pay exclusively in free stuff.
- Example: an affiliate sending massage clients may value free massages more than a $30 check (your cost). As volume grows, they'll usually want cash (nobody redeems 100 massages).
- Prestige Labs: anyone selling 3+ packages/month got a free $200 bundle of their choice — every affiliate became a "sponsored athlete" with free product for life as long as three clients kept buying.

## When to use
Product businesses with good margins; low-volume affiliates; keeping affiliates using (and believing in) the product.
