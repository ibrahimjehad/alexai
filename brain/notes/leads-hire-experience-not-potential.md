For people-heavy, operationally complex lead channels, put an experienced leader in charge — ideally one who knows more than you.

## Story
Hormozi tried cold outreach three times: external hire with experience — failed; internal hire without experience — failed; internal hire *with* experience — worked. The manager made the difference.

## Rule
Pick experienced leaders for these roles; if you're not learning from them in the interview, you've got the wrong person. (Contrast: for ground-level lead-getting jobs, hire for will and train.)

## When to use
Hiring heads of outreach, paid media, content or affiliates (roadmap level 6).
