Hormozi put 300 flyers on cars for his first gym and got one call — from a man whose Mercedes he'd scratched; his mentor tested with 5,000.

## Story
A tanning-salon-chain mentor suggested flyers when Hormozi's ad costs were rising. Zero leads from 300. The mentor's response: 300 is too small to know anything; he tests with 5,000, and when he finds a winner puts out 5,000 a day, every day, for a month. Response benchmarks: ~0.5% is decent, 1% is a winner — on 300 flyers, 0.5% is 1.5 people, so no conclusion is possible.

## Lesson
He did the right thing the wrong number of times — ~1/1,500th of the effort a flyer campaign needs. He vowed never to let effort be the reason something didn't work: it could be the offer, copy, image, targeting, media or platform, but not effort.
