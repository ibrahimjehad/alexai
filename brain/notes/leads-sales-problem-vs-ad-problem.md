If engaged leads have the problem you solve and the money to pay but aren't buying, your ads work — you have a sales problem.

## Story
A portfolio company spent 12 weeks and $150k on paid ads. The right leads were getting on the phone but not buying. The owner declared ads didn't work and quit "six inches from gold." Hormozi estimates the misdiagnosis cost ~$30M in enterprise value.

## Rule
CAC comes mostly, but not only, from advertising; closing rate is part of it. Check lead quality (problem + money) before blaming ads; if leads qualify, fix sales.

## When to use
Whenever an owner says "ads don't work" — inspect lead quality and close rate first.
