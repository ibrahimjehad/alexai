Local marketing converts more easily because people trust the familiar, but offers fatigue fast because the reachable market is small.

**Easier:** in-person selling at higher prices in a local market converts a much higher share of leads, so marketing usually works.
**Harder:** total addressable market for a brick-and-mortar business is usually its immediate radius; the smaller the radius, the faster offers fatigue.

**Response.** Keep the value stack the same and frequently vary how it looks: creative, headlines, seasonal wrappers, dated promotions. Hormozi says mastering rapid variation in his local businesses made scaling to national advertising much easier.

**When to use.** Advising gyms, clinics, dentists, salons, home services and other radius-bound businesses.
