Testimonials are the strongest way to convince, so choreograph the buyer's path from first contact to payment around them, starting with a must-watch page of results sent after booking.

**The ideal scene.** A prospect whose ten friends all used you and multiplied their results needs no screenshots: "You signed up 10 of my friends, I'm in." Your job is to manufacture that feeling for everyone else.

**Steps.**
1. **After booking**, send a link: "Please watch this before you come in" (call it mandatory). One sizzle video or a page of clips.
2. **Vary the testimonials** so every prospect finds someone like them: men and women; toning and big losses; fast results and long-term results; someone who kept it off for two years.
3. **Community proof**: a customer group where people post wins, open for prospects to see.
4. **The sales room**: floor-to-ceiling testimonials so that when someone says "I don't know if I can hit that goal", you just look around the room ([sales-engineer-the-sales-environment]).

**Why.** Prospects arrive pre-framed: you're a real business with real customers who got the result they want. You can't credibly boost yourself, but choreographed third-party proof can. Most people are sold by this point; whatever remains is usually a limiting belief ([sales-limiting-belief-customer-stories]).

**When to use.** Any appointment-based sale (gyms, clinics, coaching, agencies).
