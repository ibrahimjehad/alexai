A portfolio company's B2B close rate went from 14% to 35% (2.4x) within four months; the biggest single change was simplifying the script.

Reps had been feature-stacking and talking technically to business owners who didn't care. The fix: rewrite the script below third-grade reading level, switch to a three-pillar pitch with analogies, and lean on questions. See [sales-simplify-the-script].
