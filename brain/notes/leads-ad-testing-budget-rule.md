Let a new ad spend up to twice the cash (profit) a new customer brings in within 30 days before killing it, or 1x if it produces no leads at all.

## Decision rule
- Test budget per ad = 2 x the cash collected (gross profit) from a new customer in the first 30 days — not LTGP.
- If the ad produces leads, let it run up to 2x before shutting it off.
- If the ad produces zero leads by 1x, shut it off.
- Example: $100 profit per customer in 30 days → stop at $200 spend; stop at $100 if no leads yet.

## Why
Hormozi wasted money both by letting losers run too long and, even more, by killing ads before giving them a chance; this is the sweet spot he found.

## When to use
Every new ad test in the lose-money phase.
