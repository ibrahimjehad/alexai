Worked numbers showing how an upsell turns a profitable-but-slow membership into free customer acquisition within 30 days.

## Numbers
- Membership $15/month, delivery $5 → $10 gross profit/month. Average stay 10 months → LTGP $100.
- CAC $30 → LTGP:CAC = 3.3:1. Profitable, but you spend $30 and collect only $10 in month one; break-even takes until month three. Still take the trade, but it's a cash-flow problem.
- Fix: add a $100 upsell at ~100% margin taken by 1 in 5 new customers → $20 average extra gross profit per customer.
- First 30 days: $10 + $20 = $30 gross profit = CAC → customer acquired for free; the remaining $10/month for nine months is pure gain, and the cash recycles into the next customer.

## Lesson
You don't need cheaper ads to scale — you need more cash collected in the first 30 days.
