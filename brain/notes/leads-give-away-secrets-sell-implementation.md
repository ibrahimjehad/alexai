Make free stuff as good as your paid stuff: give so much value up front that people feel obligated to pay you for implementation.

## The idea
- The market judges everything you offer, free or not. You can't give too much value, only too little.
- Aim to give more value than the price of your core offer before they buy.
- Weak free content ("sucky fluff") makes would-be customers conclude you suck. They buy elsewhere and warn others.
- People buy based on how much value they expect after buying, and the easiest way to make them expect a lot is to deliver value before they buy.
- 99% of people won't buy, but they will make or break your reputation based on your free stuff.
- Example: a company that grows from $1M to $10M on Hormozi's free content is very likely to partner with Acquisition.com, because he "paid for his share" up front.

## When to use
Whenever a client is afraid to give away "too much" in a lead magnet, content or a free consult. Push them to give the real thing.
