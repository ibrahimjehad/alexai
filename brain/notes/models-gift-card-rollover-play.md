Advertise gift cards at 90% off, usable only by someone else; when the recipient comes in, roll the card's value into a bigger offer.

**Steps.**
1. Sell, e.g., a $200 gift card for $20. Limit two per customer; they can only be used by other people (gifts), which makes it a strong holiday offer.
2. At purchase, ask who it is for and whether they will make an introduction.
3. When the recipient comes in, roll the card over into your next offer.
4. Size the card at about 20% of the price of what you sell next: a $200 card applied to an offer of at least $1,000.

**Why.** Customers pay you to refer their friends, and you keep the pocket change from card sales.

**When to use.** Holiday promotions, referral pushes, local and service businesses.
