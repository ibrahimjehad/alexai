A checklist of proof elements that make prospects believe they will get the result, so more say yes.

## The eleven
1. Proof you have done it yourself (your own story).
2. Proof people like them got it (testimonials).
3. Volume of happy reviews (or, if none yet, how many people you've helped).
4. Legitimate certifications, degrees, third-party accreditation.
5. Numbers, stats and research supporting the outcome.
6. Experts vouching for you.
7. A new, unique mechanism they haven't failed with before, so they think it might work this time.
8. Celebrity endorsements (they trust them, so they trust you).
9. A guarantee (your own skin in the game).
10. How precisely you describe them and their current pain ("they really get me, so they must know how to help").
11. Demonstrate the outcome live or with a recording. Example: an ad agency plays a gym's sales call with a lead and asks, "Could you handle calls like this if we got them for you?" People don't want leads, they want customers.

## When to use
When building any offer, script, landing page or sales call. Include one or more; perfect-fit testimonials plus a guarantee are the defaults.
