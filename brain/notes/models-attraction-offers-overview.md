Attraction offers generate leads and convert them into customers by offering something free or discounted, then make money on what comes with or after it.

**Why discounts attract.** Everyone wants a great deal (far more value than price). Strangers can only take your word on value, but they understand price perfectly, so a discount makes anything look like a great deal, and the biggest discount is free.

**Free, discount and $1 are interchangeable.** All are discounts of some size (free is 100%). Wherever the book says free you can test a discount or $1, and vice versa.

**How free makes money.** People routinely come for one thing and buy another; attraction offers make that deliberate. Better than one free thing is two, and to get them they may have to buy one.

**Goal.** Turn strangers into customers while collecting enough cash up front to cover the cost of acquiring and serving them, ideally several times over, so you can pay yourself back and buy the next customer. Hormozi credits these offers with turning $1,000 into $10M in 10 months by repeatedly reinvesting returns.

**The five he teaches.**
1. Win your money back.
2. Giveaways.
3. Decoy offer.
4. Buy X, get Y free.
5. Pay less now or pay more later.

**When to use.** Stage one of any money model, and whenever a business spends too much to get customers and makes too little from them up front.

**Pitfall.** Rules on advertising "free" are strict and changing; check local law.
