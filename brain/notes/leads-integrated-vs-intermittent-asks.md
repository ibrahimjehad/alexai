If you must monetise content, treat asks as commercials inside your own program, either woven into each piece (integrated) or as occasional ask-only pieces (intermittent).

## Two strategies
- **Integrated:** an ask inside each piece, keeping the ratio high. A 1-hour podcast with three 30-second ads is 58.5 minutes give to 1.5 minutes ask. Place the first ask after a valuable moment or at the end; add more only if growth doesn't slow. A friend's fast-growing podcast shrank after he over-asked.
- **Intermittent:** many pure-give pieces, then one ask piece (e.g. 10 gives, ask on the 11th).
- **Platform rule:** short-form platforms favour intermittent; long-form platforms favour integrated.
- **PS statements** are among the most-read parts of any written piece, a great place for an ask.
- **One CTA at a time:** "a confused mind doesn't buy". Don't ask people to like, share, subscribe and comment at once.

## What to promote: lead magnet or core offer
- **Lead magnet ask (default if unsure, lower risk):** after giving a tip, "I have 11 more that helped me; grab the visual on my site." The opt-in thank-you page then shows the paid offer with an explainer video. Bonus if the magnet relates to the content.
- **Core offer ask (paraphrased):** "I'm looking for five [avatar] to help get [dream outcome] in [time] without [effort/sacrifice]. If you don't get it, I'll refund you and keep working with you until you do. If that sounds fair, DM me / book a call / comment / reply." Then go back to giving.

## When to use
When you decide to monetise an audience directly. Pick integrated or intermittent, then lead magnet or core offer.
