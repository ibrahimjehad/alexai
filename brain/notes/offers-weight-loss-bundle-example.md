Hormozi's worked Grand Slam Offer for his gym, which could not sell a $99/month boot camp against a $29/month big-box gym.

**Step 1:** dream outcome "lose 20 pounds in 6 weeks" (big outcome plus short time delay); he stopped selling memberships and sold the result.

**Final stack** (each problem became a named component with a stated value):
- Foolproof bargain grocery system (saves money and time), $1,000: nutrition orientation, recorded store tour, grocery calculator, weekly lists, bargain shopping training, grocery buddy, pre-made Instacart carts, weekly text check-in.
- Ready-in-5-minutes busy-parent cooking guide, $600 (about 200 hours a year back): prep instructions, calculator, buddy system, 5-minute snacks, weekly feedback post.
- Personalized meal plan easier to follow than cheating, $500: 5-minute shakes, budget lunches and dinners, family-size meals, daily photo check-in (one-on-one adjustment meeting sold as an upsell).
- Fat-burning workouts adjusted to you, $699.
- Tone-up-while-you-travel blueprint, $199.
- Never-fall-off accountability system, $1,000.
- Eating-out system so you can still socialize, $349.

**Total stated value $4,351 for $599.** His facilities later sold longer versions for $2,400-$5,200. The bundle solves every perceived problem, gives the seller conviction and cannot be compared with a gym membership. (Presentation differs between one-on-one and one-to-many selling; see the bonuses chapter.)
