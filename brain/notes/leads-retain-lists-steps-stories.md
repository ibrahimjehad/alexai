Curiosity drives retention; embed unanswered questions (explicit or implied) using lists, steps and stories.

## The three devices
1. **Lists:** themed items in any order ("top 10 mistakes", "28 ways to stay poor"). Saying the number up front, in the headline or first seconds, sets expectations and holds attention longer. Flexible, but less explicit reward.
2. **Steps:** actions in a set order that achieve a goal ("3 steps to a great hook"). If early steps are clear and valuable, people want them all. Less flexible, more explicit reward.
3. **Stories:** events, real or imagined, with a takeaway. People want to know what happens next ("my journey from sleeping on a gym floor to a five-star hotel").

Combine them: lists inside steps, stories reinforcing a step, a list of stories, ongoing storylines.

Curiosity can hold people for years; Hormozi got daily messages about when his sales book would be out.

## When to use
Structuring the body of any post, video, podcast or email after the hook.
