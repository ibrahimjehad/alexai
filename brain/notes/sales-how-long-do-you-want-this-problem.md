For a business buyer who says they don't have the money, ask permission to talk frankly, then ask how long they want "not having $X" to stay on their list of problems.

**Script** (Hormozi credits Myron Golden). "Can I talk to you like you're my little brother?" "Sure." "Our program is $10,000. How long do you want not having $10,000 (or $1,000 today) to be among the things you struggle with in life? What else is going to change unless you change something?"

**Why.** It turns lack of money from a reason to wait into the problem itself, and makes waiting the choice that keeps it.

**When to use.** B2B or business-opportunity offers, where the purchase is meant to produce income; less suited to purely emotional consumer sales.
