Turn the four buyer requirements into the only four application questions, so reps start every call with budget, authority, need and timing already settled.

**The four questions (Gym Launch's pruned application).**
1. Do you need this, and do you have the desire and ability to implement it?
2. Can you make this financial decision on your own?
3. Do you have the budget required to work with us?
4. When do you want to start: immediately or later?

**The data.**
- A biz-op friend nearly deleted the timing question; reviewing six months of applications, he found about 95% of sales came from people who said they'd start immediately or the next day, and only about 5% from "later". He stopped calling the "later" group.
- When Gym Launch added the questions, cost per booked call rose about 2.5x (fewer bookings), yet gross sales went up during the same period: more money from far fewer calls.

**Why.** With BANT handled on the form, the rep only needs to earn trust in product, salesperson and company ([sales-seven-components-of-a-closed-deal]) instead of fighting money, decision-maker and "think about it" objections.

**When to use.** Any business where closers take booked calls from ads or content. Judge the change on sales, not cost per booking.
