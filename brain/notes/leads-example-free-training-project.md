Hormozi got his first clients by messaging people he knew with a free 12-week training offer, then converted them to paying and got referrals.

## The story
- In 2013 he skipped business school (~$150K debt, two years without income) to start a business with ~$51K savings. Setup: an LLC, a bank account and a merchant account.
- He called, texted and Facebook-messaged people he knew: "Do you know anyone trying to get in shape? I'm training people free for 12 weeks plus a custom nutrition and grocery plan. They just donate to a charity of their choice and let me use their testimonial."
- Six said yes: two high-school friends, one college friend and three of their referrals.
- After 12 weeks he asked them to pay him instead of the charity; nobody minded. Once they had results he asked for referrals: another 5-6 clients, who paid directly.
- The simple business (emailed plans, text check-ins) made ~$4,000/month and replaced his job income.

## Lessons
Warm outreach plus a free offer plus a testimonial ask plus a referral ask works even with zero marketing knowledge. Start free, switch to paid once results and referrals appear.

## When to use
As a model plan for a solo service provider with no audience or money.
