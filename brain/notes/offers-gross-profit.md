Gross profit is revenue minus the direct cost of servicing one additional customer.

Examples: lotion sold at $10 that costs $2 gives $8 gross profit (80%). Agency services at $1,000/month with $100/month of labor to run the client's ads give $900 (90%).

It is not net profit: net profit is what remains after all expenses (rent, admin, software, etc.), not only fulfillment costs.

**When to use.** Whenever comparing offers or pricing: judge offers on gross margin per customer, the input to lifetime gross profit and to how much you can afford to spend acquiring a customer.
