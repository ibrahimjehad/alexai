The only thing that beats free is fast; many people will pay to get the result sooner.

Examples: private licence renewal vs waiting at the DMV, FedEx overnight vs the regular postal service, Spotify vs slow free music, Uber vs walking.

**When to use.** If your market competes with a free option (DIY, government service, free content), make speed the core of the offer and price for it.
