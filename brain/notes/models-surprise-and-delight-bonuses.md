If you hold bonuses to tip fence-sitters, add them one at a time; if someone says yes before you reach them all, still give them every bonus.

**Why.** It surprises and delights the buyer, and everyone ends up with the same package, so no one feels cheated later when customers compare notes.

**When to use.** Any sale where you add bonuses progressively to close.
