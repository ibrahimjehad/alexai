Take whatever reason they give for not buying and reframe it as the very reason they need to buy, then ask again.

**Script.** "I don't have time." -> "I think the fact that you don't have time is probably the reason you need this more than anyone." "I don't have the money right now." -> "That's probably why you need to do this more than anyone."

**Why it holds up.**
- Consumer: they'll care more than anyone because they're so invested.
- B2B: the problem they're using as the reason not to buy is the problem you solve ("No money because not enough customers? That's what our SEO fixes. Thin margins? That's what our pricing work fixes").

**Where it fits.** One of seven universal closes Hormozi says anyone can memorise for non-logistical objections (decision maker, time, money). The aim is not to win the argument but to reframe and ask again.

**When to use.** Time and money objections where the product genuinely addresses that constraint; not for real logistical mismatches.
