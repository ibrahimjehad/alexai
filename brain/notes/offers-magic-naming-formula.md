Name an offer by combining three to five of five components: Magnet, Avatar, Goal, Interval, Container.

1. **M: Magnet (make a magic reason why).** Why are you making this offer, or what is in it for them? Anything works if you believe it (like a frat party thrown because someone had their wisdom teeth out). Examples: free, 88% off, giveaway, spring, back to school, grand opening, new management, anniversary, Halloween, New Year.
2. **A: Announce your avatar.** Call out who it is for (and implicitly who it is not). Be as specific as possible but no more. Locally, go hyper-local: a suburb or neighbourhood, not the big city. Examples: salon owners, retired athletes, local moms, busy executives in a specific neighbourhood.
3. **G: Give them a goal.** The dream outcome in a word or phrase, specific and tangible: pain-free, celebrity smile, double your profit, first client, little black dress, seven-figure.
4. **I: Indicate a time interval.** How long it takes: 21-day, 6-week, 3-month. Compliance: most ad platforms reject a quantified claim (income, pounds) paired with a time frame because it implies a guarantee; use durations with non-claim goals ("make your first sale in 10 days" rather than "$10,000 in 10 days").
5. **C: Complete with a container word.** Signals a bundled system that cannot be compared with a commodity: challenge, blueprint, boot camp, intensive, accelerator, masterclass, sprint, fast track, reset, workshop, mastermind, detox.

Roughly: attention, discrimination, purpose, timeline, method.

**Tips.** Shorter and punchier beats complete; order is flexible. Rhymes stick ("six-pack fast track", "12-month no-debt reset"); alliteration is easier ("debt detox", "real estate reset", "life coach liftoff"). Do not force either. Name every sub-item and bonus in the stack with the same formula.

**Testing.** Write many names, run your best two or three in ads, keep the winner as the control and test new names against it. Some names take off for no clear reason; do not be emotional about it.

**When to use.** Naming any offer, promotion, bonus or program.
