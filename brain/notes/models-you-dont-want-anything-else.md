People are trained to answer "You don't want anything else, do you?" with "no", so phrase the close so that "no" confirms the purchase you just offered.

**How.** After making the upsell, ask "You don't want anything else (besides what I just offered), do you?" The customer says "no", meaning they keep the upsell and decline anything further.

**Variant in downsells.** After rejected packages: "So nothing more than the minimum package then?" A "no" is a yes to the minimum.

**When to use.** Closing any upsell or package choice, in person or on the phone.
