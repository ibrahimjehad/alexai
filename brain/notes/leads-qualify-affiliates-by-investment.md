Make potential affiliates invest — by becoming customers, paying for expert certification, or both — because those who pay pay attention, win faster and make more.

## Way 1 — Make them a customer
Require them to buy (and ideally use) the product to keep affiliate status. Lowest-barrier investment that works; if they won't buy it, they shouldn't sell it. The more an affiliate invests, the more they make.
- **Bulk purchases** (physical products/product lines): require a big upfront package. Huge for Prestige Labs: affiliates followed through and won more. Phrase: "Do you want anything extra, or just the minimum order?" — they'll at least buy the minimum and often more.

## Way 2 — Make them an expert
Charge for onboarding and training that certifies them as a product expert (product purchase can be credited toward it). Covers your cost of acquiring them and funds proper onboarding.
- **Price:** 10–20% of what the average affiliate earns in their first 12 months (avg earns $40k/year → charge $4k–$8k). Too low → not invested; too high → too few affiliates. 10–20% maximizes active affiliates.
- Just starting? Use bulk purchases, or raise the minimum investment every five sign-ups until you find the sweet spot.

## Decision rule
Not enough people starting → lower the commitment. Not enough following through → raise it. Hormozi's favorite: both customer and expert.

## When to use
Step three of the six-step affiliate process.
