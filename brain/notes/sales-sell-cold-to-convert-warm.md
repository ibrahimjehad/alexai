Sell as if nobody knows you and you convert both cold and warm prospects; sell as if everyone already knows you and only the warm ones buy.

**Why.** A coach converting ~9% was mostly selling to people who already followed her; her ads were reaching existing fans. To scale you must sell to cold audiences, so the pitch has to establish who you are, why to listen and proof from scratch ([sales-structured-webinar-pitch]).

**When to use.** When growth has stalled after selling out a warm audience, or before scaling paid traffic.
