The shorter the gap between purchase and benefit, the more valuable the offer; engineer early emotional wins even when the main outcome takes months.

**Two layers.** Customers buy the long-term outcome (the dream) but stay because of short-term experiences: milestones that prove they are on the right path. "They buy the dream, but they stay for the benefits they discover along the way."

**Fast-win tactics.**
- Gym Launch: get the client's ads live and their first $2,000 sale closed within 7 days, reinforcing the decision and building trust for the rest of the system.
- Weight loss: introduce new clients to another member for instant social benefit, and start with a more aggressive diet for a big early result.
Early victories make people more likely to persist.

Time delay also explains price: $25,000 for liposuction (done in an afternoon) vs reluctance to pay $100/month for a boot camp (12-24 months).

**When to use.** In onboarding design: map the first 7-30 days and plant at least one visible win as close to purchase as possible.
