When the prospect names a future date for the result, reframe it as a deadline that already requires starting now, then ask for the first day this week.

**Script.** "You want him ready by summer, right? That's like saying 'I want to be 40 pounds lighter by summer' - we have to start now. Honestly, the best time to start would have been last month. It's February; you should have started in December. So what's the first day this week you can bring him in?" Then straight to payment ([sales-take-payment-on-the-call]).

**Why.** Prospects anchor on when they want the outcome, not when they need to start. Moving the anchor creates urgency from their own goal rather than from fake scarcity.

**When to use.** Any offer where results take time (fitness, skills, seasonal services) and prospects try to defer.
