A single unsolved obstacle is often the one reason a prospect does not buy, so the offer should answer every problem they raise.

Story: Hormozi insisted weight-loss clients cook at home and lost many sales to people who ate out. Needing rent money, he told an executive who ate lunch out daily that he would build her an eating-out guide so she could eat out 100% of the time and still hit her goal. She bought. From then on "what about eating out?" had an answer, and over time he added templates and trainings until no single objection could block a sale.

**Rule.** Do not get romantic about how you want to solve the problem. Leaving an obstacle unsolved does not stop all sales, but it caps how many people convert. The goal is the most buyers at the highest price and margin.

**When to use.** Whenever recurring objections appear in sales calls: turn each one into a deliverable (guide, template, training, support channel).
