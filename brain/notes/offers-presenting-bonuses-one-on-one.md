In a one-on-one sale, ask for the sale before mentioning bonuses, then use bonuses to answer specific objections.

**Steps.**
1. Present the core offer and ask for the sale.
2. If they say yes: after they sign, reveal the extra bonuses they are getting. It creates a "wow" moment and reinforces the decision.
3. If they say no: agree with them, present a bonus that directly solves the obstacle they named, and ask again whether that makes it fair.
4. Repeat with another bonus if needed. Reciprocity makes it hard to keep refusing; people start to feel obligated.

You were going to provide all of it anyway; releasing it one piece at a time raises perceived value. (Group selling works differently and is outside the book's scope.)

**When to use.** One-on-one sales calls and in-person closes.
