Get permission to present the premium first, contrast it with the decoy, let the prospect choose the premium themselves, then assume the close.

**Script structure.**
1. Ideally present the premium first; keep the decoy in your back pocket.
2. If they arrive asking for the decoy, get permission: "Are you here for free stuff or lasting results?" If "results" (most say this), go to the premium. If "free stuff", present the decoy and immediately contrast it with the premium.
3. After presenting both: "Which do you think will get you to your goal faster?" or "Would you prefer [lesser thing] or [premium], which gives you [benefit 1, 2, 3]?" They will name the premium; proceed as mutually agreed.
4. Be genuinely excited about the premium and explain why it fits them better.
5. **Assumed close.** Act as if this is what everyone does and the rest is a formality ("let me get your ID and card so you can get started"): friendly, almost bored by how normal buying is. No hype.
6. **Optional surprise benefit.** If they take the decoy, throw in one or two low- or zero-cost premium features "because I want you to get great results". Builds goodwill and makes later upsells likelier; they are still leads.

**Legal note.** If a prospect asks to hear about the advertised decoy, you must present it.

**When to use.** Every sales conversation that starts from a decoy ad.
