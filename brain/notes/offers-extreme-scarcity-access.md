Create a very exclusive tier of direct access to you, cap it at a tiny number, price it very high, and tell people.

Access can use any delivery-cube medium (DMs, email, phone, voice memos, Zoom). Pick a channel you do not hate (Hormozi dislikes email and messages but tolerates Zoom). The top "1% of the 1%" will adjust to your terms, and these tend to be the best clients.

**"Once you're out, you can never come back."** Capping a tier and stating that anyone who leaves can never rejoin makes members think hard before leaving. Hormozi used it in his gyms and at his top Gym Lords tier. It works best with small groups and loses force as groups grow.

**When to use.** When an owner wants fast revenue and has a reputation or expertise people want direct access to; and to cut churn in a small premium group.
