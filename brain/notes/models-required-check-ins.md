Make attendance at check-in meetings part of the qualifying criteria; each meeting helps the customer succeed and is a natural moment to make the next offer.

**How.**
1. Make every meeting required to win the money back (or avoid the trial fee).
2. At each one, check progress, then offer what makes sense based on their feedback.

**Hormozi's gym version (three appointments).**
1. Nutrition orientation (before photos): supplement offer.
2. Progress check-in: membership offer.
3. Transformation feedback (after photos): membership offer again; if they buy, offer a discount to prepay the year.

**When to use.** Any program with a defined start and end (challenges, onboarding, trials, coaching blocks).
