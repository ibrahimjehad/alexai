Launch anything — and activate affiliates — in three phases that mirror an ad: whisper (curiosity/call-out), tease (value), shout (call to action), with escalating frequency as launch day nears.

## Why launches for affiliates
How much value affiliates get determines how much they advertise; nothing gives them a fast win like a big launch with lots of cash. Affiliates advertise your lead magnet or core offer (posts, warm outreach, paid ads, even cold outreach) before it's available, then sell to all the engaged leads they gathered on launch day. Do all the work ahead of time so they can plug and play.

## Phase 1 — Whisper (call-out / curiosity)
Keep the product mysterious, hint at how big a deal it is, keep whispers short, show behind-the-scenes. Start as early as possible (even years): the longer something appears to take, the more people value it (10 years > 10 days), so show your work. Embed questions, then say "not yet."
Cadence: every 4–6 weeks until 60 days out, then every 2–3 weeks until 30 days out.
Book example: posting drafts, printing pages, framework sketches, early-morning/late-night editing videos; updates to friends, email list and potential affiliates.

## Phase 2 — Tease (value)
Reveal the product, announce the launch date, show the value elements using what/who/when; get more specific with hard information and examples.
Cadence: once a week until 14 days out, then twice a week until 3 days out.

## Phase 3 — Shout (CTA)
Give specific actions; pound bonuses, scarcity, urgency and guarantees for early buyers; maximize exposure.
Cadence: at least twice a day from 3 days out; on launch day every few hours until 2 hours out, then every 30 minutes until launch.

## Model
Movie releases: 5-second trailers a year out, 30-second trailers ~90 days out, longer trailers as release nears — curiosity → interest → action.

## When to use
Product launches, affiliate activation, book/event launches.
