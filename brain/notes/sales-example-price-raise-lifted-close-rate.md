A health company Hormozi worked with, run by a doctor, was priced far below its promise. He wanted to double the price; the owner resisted and they settled on a 50% increase. Close rates went up: the old price was so low against what it promised that prospects didn't believe it worked. See [sales-stop-selling-from-your-wallet].
