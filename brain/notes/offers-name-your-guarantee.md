Replace bland wording like "30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee" with a memorable, vivid name and image.

Examples from the book: "in 30 days, if you wouldn't jump into shark-infested waters to get our product back, we return every dollar"; or a deliberately outrageous named guarantee about what a customer would do to stay on after 30 days. The terms can be identical; the striking image conveys confidence and sticks.

**When to use.** Whenever you state a guarantee in copy or on a call.
