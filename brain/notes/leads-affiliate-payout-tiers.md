Decide exactly what behavior you're paying for, cap payouts at your maximum allowable CAC, and tier payouts so only proven affiliates get the full amount.

## What to pay for
Ask "what exactly do I want the affiliate to do?" and pay for that. Basics: new customers and repeat customers. With better tracking, pay for earlier steps that reliably become sales (lead magnet downloads, appointments set).

## How much — maximum allowable CAC
Gross profit per sale ÷ (target LTGP:CAC + 1) parts. Example: $200 single-use product, $40 to fulfill → $160 gross profit. Target 3:1 → 3 parts ($120) to the business, 1 part ($40) to the affiliate. Max payout: $40 per new customer.

## Three-tier structure (example with $40 max)
1. **Tier 1 — 25% of CAC ($10):** anyone who accepts the initial terms (signs up, buys product/certification).
2. **Tier 2 — 50% ($20):** once they activate (finish certification, hit a set number of posts/outreach, run a launch).
3. **Tier 3 — 100% ($40):** once they sustain performance (e.g. keep five subscription customers per month).

## Hidden benefit
Blended payout falls below max CAC. E.g. sales 20% tier 1, 20% tier 2, 60% tier 3 → blended $30 instead of $40, moving LTGP:CAC from 3:1 to 4:1. Cutting marketing cost ~33% can mean a 10–20% jump in annual net profit. Use the leftover for contests, recruiting more affiliates, rewarding rising stars — or keep it.

## Duration
Hormozi prefers paying a share forever (never capping payouts) so affiliates stay motivated to keep the customer forever.
