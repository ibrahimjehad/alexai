Replace "if you want it done right, do it yourself" with beliefs that let others outperform you — owners who compete with their team stay small.

## Old (false) beliefs
"If you want it done right, do it yourself." "No one can do it like I can." "Nobody can replace me." Hormozi held these for years, compared every hire to himself, and alternated between gloating and complaining — ignoring that he had hired and trained them (and was too poor, then too cheap, to hire better). You may do *anything* better than any one employee, but not *everything* better than all of them.

## New beliefs
- If you want it done right, get someone to spend all their time doing it.
- If I can do it, someone else can do it better.
- Everyone is replaceable, especially me — so replace yourself as soon as you can and be useful elsewhere.

## Why it pays
Trusting employees makes the owner's time far more valuable: if someone else can do or train it, the owner should learn and do what grows the business.

## When to use
When an owner resists delegation or blames staff quality.
