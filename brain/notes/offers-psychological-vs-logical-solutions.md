Logical solutions have usually already been tried; the remaining problems tend to have psychological fixes.

Drawing on Rory Sutherland (Ogilvy): any fool can sell by discounting, it takes great marketing to sell at a premium.
- Logical: faster trains. Psychological: a countdown map, or more pleasant onboard experience, so people mind the trip less.
- Logical: faster elevators. Psychological: mirrors so people do not notice the wait.
- Logical: make it cheaper. Psychological: make fewer and raise the price, so more people want it.

**When to use.** When a customer complaint seems to require an expensive operational fix: first ask how to change how the experience is perceived.
