Advertise a lifetime lower rate that customers earn by staying past the month when most people cancel.

**Steps.**
1. Find average customer drop-off (e.g. month 4).
2. Tell everyone up front they unlock a lifetime discount after that month.
3. As the date approaches, remind them their lower rate is around the corner.

**Example.** A rice company offered one-time purchase, 5% off subscription, or 15% off for life after five straight months, likely set just beyond typical cancellation.

**When to use.** Subscriptions with a predictable churn cliff.
