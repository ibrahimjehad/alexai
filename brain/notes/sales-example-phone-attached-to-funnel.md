When a Facebook change raised Hormozi's lead cost about 5x overnight, his gym marketing went from profitable to losing money daily; the fix was in sales, not ads.

**What changed.** A gym-owner friend told him they had "attached a phone to the funnel": instead of selling cheap trials, they called leads and sold expensive packages on the front end. Hormozi copied it and went from losing about 2:1 to about 10:1 return on the front end. Break-even lead cost went from about $10 to about $100, so he could keep buying leads at the new prices.

**Later results.** The approach (with CLOSER) became the Gym Launch system: one launch did 191 sign-ups in 19 days (~$100k) for a single gym, while charging about 3x the closest competitor. He claims a 36:1 lifetime return on ad spend (just under $3M in ads).

**Where the skill came from.** About 4,000 one-on-one, in-person sales over roughly four years: managers stacked 20 to 25 consults a day at one of his six gyms, he sold all day, then moved to the next gym. Volume of reps taught him to read people and tonality.

**The lesson.** If lead costs rise, raising what you make per customer through a sales conversation and a higher front-end price can absorb a 10x higher cost per lead.
