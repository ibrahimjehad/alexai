Good refund criteria are easy to track, likely to produce the customer's result, and advertise the business; they make or break the offer.

**Three characteristics.**
1. **Easy to track.** Train customers on exactly what to do or they will get it wrong. Bonus if they already track it (phones count steps, word processors count words, cameras date photos).
2. **Gets customers results.** Copy what your best customers do to get the best results and require everyone to do it (attend meetings and workouts, watch videos). Realistic criteria are enough; if they look too easy, you are probably close to realistic. Expect a few iterations.
3. **Advertises the business.** Include posting about their participation, tagging you on social media, referring, reviews and testimonials.

**Plus.** Make check-in meetings required (see [models-required-check-ins]); they help customers succeed and are your best upsell moments.

**Reuse.** The same criteria become the terms of a trial with penalty and the conditions on a pay-later guarantee.

**When to use.** Whenever you design a conditional refund, trial or guarantee.
