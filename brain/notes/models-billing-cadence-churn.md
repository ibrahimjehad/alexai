Subscription data from about 14,000 businesses (reported by ProfitWell) showed that less frequent billing goes with much lower monthly cancellation.

**Monthly cancellation rate by billing cadence.**
- Billed monthly (12x/year): 10.7%.
- Billed quarterly (4x/year): 5%.
- Billed annually (1x/year): 2%.

**Implication.** Presenting prices from most cash up front to least (annual, then quarterly, then monthly) both raises 30-day cash and makes customers stay longer. Changing how customers pay changes how long they stay.

**Caveat.** Correlation across businesses; people choosing annual plans may already be more committed.

**When to use.** Choosing billing options and the order to present them; arguing for prepay and bulk offers.
