Performance-based pay is an implied guarantee: if you do not deliver, they do not pay; if you deliver big, you earn big.

**Structures.**
- Performance: pay per sale, per show, per pound lost.
- Revenue share: e.g. 10% of top line, or 25% of growth above a baseline.
- Profit share: a percentage of profit or gross profit.
- Ratchets: 10% above X, 20% above Y, 30% above Z.
- Bonuses and triggers: you get X when Y happens.
- With a minimum: the greater of $1,000 or 10% of revenue; or a fixed fee for the first three months, then 100% performance (good when results take time to start).

**Why Hormozi favours it.** Aligns you with client results, weeds out low performers, builds long relationships. Agencies using his software moved from retainers to performance offers wrapped in a Grand Slam Offer, and he has seen many go from about $20K to $200K/month within months.

**Requirements and drawbacks.** Needs quantifiable outcomes, transparent measurement, and trust or control over collection. Tracking and collecting are the hard parts.

**When to use.** Services with measurable outcomes (agencies, consultants, lead gen) where the client fears paying for no results.
