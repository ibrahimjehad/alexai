Tell entrants that if someone they refer wins the grand prize, they win one too; referrals multiply and stay high quality.

**How.** Offer two grand prizes: one to the winner and one to whoever referred them. Entrants get effectively unlimited entries by referring friends, so they refer more and collaborate.

**Why quality stays high.** Referrers are invested in their referral's success, so they bring people likely to do well.

**Example.** On Skool (a community platform Hormozi co-owns), a competition announced that if someone you referred finished top 10, you came along too, alongside the standing 40% lifetime referral commission.

**When to use.** Any giveaway or competition where you want referral volume.
