Case story: in late 2016 Hormozi was effectively broke, and a single Grand Slam Offer funded the comeback.

**Setup.** He had sold five of six gyms and put the money in an account with a new business partner, who withdrew it all. A payment processor then froze $120K of collected sales for six months; he had already paid the $22K commission to his salesman, leaving about $1K. His only assets were a business credit card with a $100K limit and an offer he had perfected in his own gyms (the gym "challenge" offer, cash collected upfront, with his fee taken from the turnaround revenue).

**Bet.** The day after Christmas he launched six gyms at once, spending about $3,300 per day on travel and ads with money he did not have.

**Result.** January 2017 brought in about $100K, enough to cover the burn. Within 12 months the business had about $3M in profit and was doing $1.5M per month; later his portfolio crossed $120M in sales.

**Lesson.** A strong offer is an asset that works in any town and with any team; with it, even borrowed ad spend returns more than it costs. Illustrates [offers-grand-slam-offer].
