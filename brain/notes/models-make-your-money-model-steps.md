Add one offer type at a time, in order: attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity, each with a specific goal.

1. **Start with an attraction offer.** Goal: turn strangers into customers and cover costs. Decide what to sell and the best way to present it (win your money back, giveaway, decoy, buy X get Y free, pay less now or later), then advertise it. Finding what works may take up to a year (see *$100M Leads* for advertising).
2. **Pick an upsell offer.** Goal: push 30-day profit well above CAC plus delivery cost. Solve the problem your attraction offer creates (classic, menu, anchor or rollover upsell), at the customer's time of greatest need.
3. **Pick a downsell offer.** Goal: turn people who said no into buyers, so the same leads produce more cash. Keep the price but change how they pay (payment plan, trial with penalty), or charge less by changing what they get (feature downsell); you can alternate in one sale. The more flexible your offers, the more people buy.
4. **Pick a continuity offer.** Goal: one last sale inside the 30-day window plus stacked recurring cash (continuity bonus, continuity discount, waived fee). Sometimes the right moment is after 30 days; better at the right time than forced.

**Bootstrapper note.** Without investors, a fortune or endless free customers, you must acquire customers at a profit; a working money model is the only way to scale profitably.

**When to use.** As the build plan for any client's monetization; pair with [models-perfect-one-offer-at-a-time].
