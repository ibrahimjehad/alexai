A rental-car counter turned a $19/day booking into about $100/day (5x) with five well-timed offers, each solving a problem the customer had or would have.

**The offers.**
1. Vehicle upgrade: a roomier pickup for a big customer (solves "big man in small car").
2. Late return: drop off any time that day without late fees (solves the evening-flight problem).
3. Premium insurance covering any damage (declined).
4. Downsell to minimum insurance (accepted).
5. Prepaid fuel at $3.75/gallon vs about $3.50 locally: return it empty (solves the risk of missing the flight while refuelling).

**Lessons.**
- The company anticipated every problem and offered solutions to future fees and hassles for a smaller total price now.
- The customer paid more and faster, and was happy to, because more problems were solved.
- Even a declined offer is followed by a downsell.

**When to use.** To explain money models to an owner in one story; ask them to list their own "upgrade, late return, insurance, fuel" equivalents.
