How Gym Launch went from one decoy offer to $4.4M/month in 20 months by adding one offer layer at a time.

**Layer 1: decoy attraction offer (to about $476K/month in three months).** Free books, courses and trainings for gym owners, each with a free call. On the call: do it yourself for free, or the premium done-with-you implementation, $16,000 over 16 weeks.

**Layer 2: classic upsell plus payment-plan downsell plus continuity (to about $1.5M/month).** "Gym Lords" for advanced owners at $42,000/year (advanced playbooks, services, a community as a continuity bonus). $6,000 off for prepaying ($36K). If not: $10,000 down and the rest over time; then $800/week for 52 weeks; then start free, with a continuity discount front-loading free time until their first program ended, so payments rolled straight on.

**Layer 3: menu upsell plus feature downsells (to about $2.3M/month within 14 months).** A personalized menu: done-for-you advertising, done-for-you sales training, turnkey quick-cash campaigns, and a minimum package (ongoing access to the original materials plus tech support at a discounted monthly rate). Feature downsells found the best fit; almost everyone stayed for something.

**Stage three menu as summarized:** full package $800/week; à la carte done-for-you ads $300/week, daily sales training $200/week, monthly new releases $500/week, original licensed materials with tech support $100/week; minimum package $100/week. (The à la carte items add to $1,100/week, so the full package is effectively a bundle discount.)

**Layer 4: an integrated second business** (Prestige Labs supplements) took the total to $4.4M/month by month 20.

**Lesson.** It only looks clean in hindsight; each layer was added when the previous one plateaued.
