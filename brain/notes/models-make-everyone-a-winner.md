Sell the program as if only qualifiers get their money back, then privately credit everyone toward the long-term offer, whether or not they hit the goal.

**Mid-program (about halfway).** Make the next offer as if they have already won. Script shape: "I know you're working on [short-term goal], but what's your long-term goal? ... Great, so this is about your long-term results. To show how much I want you to get there, I'll credit this program toward the next one whether you hit the short-term goal or not. How does that sound?" Then sign them up for the long-term offer before the program ends. This lowers fear of failing and keeps them longer.

**End of program: let the losers win.** If someone failed the challenge and refused the first upsell, act as if they won: "You started, which is the biggest win. You didn't hit your short-term goal, but you hit ours: finishing what you started. To show we're in this for the long haul, we'll credit your whole deposit toward staying with us, so you get your money back and can still reach your goal."

**Do it in private** so everyone stays surprised and grateful when you make the offer.

**Principle.** "We don't get customers to make a sale. We make sales to get customers."

**When to use.** Any challenge, deposit or win-your-money-back program with a long-term follow-on offer.
