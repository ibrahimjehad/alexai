Assume the sale ("Visa or Mastercard?"), send a payment or booking link while they're on the phone, and don't hang up until you see it complete.

**Steps.**
1. After they agree to a start day: "Great. What card do you want to use, Visa or Mastercard?"
2. If they start reading the number: "Actually, don't read it out, let's keep it safe. I'm sending you a link now and I'll stay on the line to make sure it works."
3. Walk them through it: "Got it? Click through, pick the 1:00 slot..." and confirm on your side ("I see you through").
4. Close warmly with next steps: reminders by text, what to bring.

**Why.** Every hang-up before payment is a chance to cool off. Staying on the line converts a verbal yes into a paid customer.

**When to use.** Phone sales of any ticket size, especially inbound leads from ads.
