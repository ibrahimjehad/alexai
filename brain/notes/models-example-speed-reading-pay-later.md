A speed-reading trainer offered a free training with two payment options, earning $297 from Hormozi without pitching during the session.

**Offer.** Option 1: card down for $0, billed $297 tomorrow; email before then if your speed did not double and the charge is cancelled, but only if you attended. Option 2: pay $97 now and get the recordings as a bonus (not sold elsewhere).

**Outcome.** Hormozi chose pay-later, attended, doubled his speed in about two hours, and paid the $297. He declined the eight-week upsell pitched at the end.

**Lesson.** Clear promise plus conditional cancel equals easy yes and earned payment. Missed opportunity: hinting at the upsell early (see [models-hint-next-offer-early]).
