Get non-competing businesses to supply free products or services as bonuses in exchange for exposure to your customers, and negotiate a commission on top.

**Why partners agree.** It is free marketing to high-quality prospects (your paying customers), and earns referral goodwill.

**Example (pain clinic, $400 offer):** free massages from a massage therapist, two chiropractor adjustments (about $100), discounts from a low-inflammation food company (about $50), orthotics discounts (about $150), a free personal-training session plus a month of pool access at a local club (about $100), pharmacy discounts (about $100/month). Repeat with several providers (e.g. 10 chiropractors = 10 adjustments). The bonuses alone exceed the price.

**Level up.** Negotiate a group discount for your customers and a referral commission for you. Hormozi's supplement company Prestige Labs gives gym owners' clients 30% off and pays the gym 40% of net sales. In the clinic example, referral fees (e.g. $100 per chiropractor visit, $100 per orthotics referral, $50/month per club signup, $100 per pharmacy customer) could add about $350 of profit on a $400 sale. Bonuses then both lift close rates and become revenue streams. You can even help partners build Grand Slam Offers so their bonuses are more valuable.

**When to use.** When an offer needs more value without more fulfillment cost; ask what the customer will need next and approach those businesses.
