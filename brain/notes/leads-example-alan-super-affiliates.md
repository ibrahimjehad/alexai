Hormozi's software company Alan scaled to $1.7M/month within six months using super affiliates who recruited agencies who recruited local businesses.

## Chain
Agency "super affiliates" → brought agencies → agencies brought local businesses → local businesses brought end-consumer leads.
One super affiliate added ~10 agencies/month → ~50 local businesses/month → ~2,500 leads/month, which Alan worked at ~$5 each = $12,500/month. Because each layer kept recruiting, each super affiliate added $12.5k the first month, $25k the second, $37.5k the third, and so on.

## Lesson
A lead getter who gets lead getters (scenario four of lead-getter leverage) compounds revenue month after month from a single recruitment effort; with only a few super affiliates the company hit $1.7M/month in six months.
