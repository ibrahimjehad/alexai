Copy becomes more powerful when it describes how other people will react to the buyer's result.

Example: not just "add 40 yards to your drive", but your golf buddies' jaws dropping as your ball lands 40 yards past theirs and they ask what changed. Connect the dots from the result to the social reward.

**When to use.** Ad copy, sales scripts and testimonials: after stating the outcome, add the social reaction it produces.
