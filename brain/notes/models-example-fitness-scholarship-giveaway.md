A fitness-certification company got tons of engaged leads by advertising one full-ride scholarship and then offering partial scholarships to everyone else.

**How it ran.** Applicants submitted contact details and answered questions like "Why should we pick you?". The best answer got the full ride, which was celebrated publicly. Every other applicant got a call saying they had won a partial scholarship; most joined on the spot. They did not know the program's actual price, but they knew the full ride's value, so the discounted price felt like a big saving. Demand was high enough that they had to cap sign-ups to keep service quality. They taught the same play to the trainers they certified, who used it to get fitness clients.
