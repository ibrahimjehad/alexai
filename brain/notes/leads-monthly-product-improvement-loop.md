Every month, find the most common customer problem, fix it with input from customers who succeeded anyway, test on struggling customers, then roll out — forever.

## Steps
1. Use customer service data, surveys and reviews to find the most common product problem.
2. Design a fix — get a head start from customers who made the product work despite the problem.
3. Improve the product with that feedback.
4. Give the new version to a small group of struggling customers.
5. Get feedback: if solved, roll out to everyone; if not, return to step 2.
6. Move to the next most common problem and repeat.

## Why
Less effort and sacrifice for the customer = better product = more goodwill = more referrals. There's no perfect product.

## When to use
As a standing monthly process in every business.
