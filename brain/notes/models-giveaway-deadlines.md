Limit a giveaway by time and number of entries, and put a deadline on each step: entering, claiming and using.

**Scarcity.** Cap by time (e.g. 7 days), by entries (e.g. 5,000), or both (Hormozi prefers both). Size the cap to the number of people your team can actually call within 7 days; more is waste.

**Urgency in three places.**
1. *To enter*: make the entry deadline clear in the ads.
2. *To claim*: after announcing winners, state how long they have to claim; book their call the same or next day.
3. *To use*: once they claim, state how long they have to use it (Hormozi prefers hours, up to 5 days).

**Rule.** Advertise for 7 days or until leads exceed what you can call in 7 days, whichever comes first. Always have deadlines.

**When to use.** Every giveaway, scholarship or promotional voucher.
