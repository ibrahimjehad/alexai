Offer your guarantee only to people who pay in full up front, which gets more yeses and more cash.

## The idea
Guarantees reverse risk, so more people buy. Tie them to prepayment, with the reason that people who invest up front are more committed, so you can guarantee their outcome.

## Script (credited to Dr. Kashi)
After they agree to buy: "Would you rather pay less today, or get all your money back?"
- **Pay less today:** payment plan, no guarantee (e.g. $2,000/month x 3 = $6,000).
- **Get all your money back:** $6,000 up front with a results guarantee.

Most people choose the upfront option with the guarantee.

## When to use
Whenever you'd offer a guarantee anyway and want to improve cash collected up front.
