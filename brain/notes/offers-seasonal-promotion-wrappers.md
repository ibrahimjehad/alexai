Repackage the same core offer each month under a seasonal name with a visible end date to create urgency and novelty.

Example sequence: New Year promotion ends January 30; Valentine's promotion ends in February; "Sexy by Spring" ends March 31; an April Fools-themed promo ends April 30. The offer underneath can be identical; the dated wrapper gives a start and finish.

Put the dates everywhere (landing pages, copy, ads). Launching a new page and campaign with new dates takes minutes and can lift conversions sharply. Dates must be real.

**Local businesses:** Hormozi's number-one strategy, because local audiences are small and see the same ads repeatedly, so they need more frequent variation than national advertisers.

**When to use.** Any business that sells year-round, especially local ones whose ads fatigue quickly.
