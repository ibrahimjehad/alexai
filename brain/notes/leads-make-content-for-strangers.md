Create every piece as if the viewer has never heard of you; strangers then like and share it and the audience grows faster.

## The idea
Inside jokes and assumed context welcome only existing fans and slow growth. This is one of the most common creator mistakes. Existing followers won't mind the context; they appreciate the reminders.

## When to use
Every post meant to grow the audience, and especially when growth has stalled despite strong engagement from current fans.
