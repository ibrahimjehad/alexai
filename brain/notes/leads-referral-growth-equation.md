Monthly % of customers referred minus monthly % of customers churned = monthly compounding growth rate from word of mouth alone.

## Formula
% clients referred per month − % clients churned per month = % monthly compounding growth

## Decision states
- Referrals > churn → you grow every month with no other advertising (how PayPal and Dropbox exploded).
- Referrals = churn → you need other advertising to grow.
- Referrals < churn → you must advertise just to stand still (most small businesses: the "hamster wheel of death").
With referral-driven growth you can keep compounding at any size; with Core Four alone you eventually hit a wall because you must keep scaling inputs.

## Action
Measure your baseline referral % and churn %, then build goodwill (six value levers) and ask for referrals (seven asks).

## When to use
Diagnosing plateaus in subscription/repeat businesses; setting retention and referral targets.
