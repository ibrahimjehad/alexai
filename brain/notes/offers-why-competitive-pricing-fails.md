Most owners set price by averaging competitors and going slightly below, which locks them into thin margins alongside competitors who are already broke.

**The typical (broken) process:**
1. Look at the market.
2. See what everyone offers.
3. Take the average price.
4. Go slightly below to be "competitive".
5. Offer what competitors offer plus a little more.
6. End up with "more for less".

This prices for market efficiency: new entrants keep offering a little more for a little less until margins barely cover costs, the bottom 10-20% wash out and new naive entrants repeat the cycle. The aim is the most money, not the most customers.

**When to use.** When auditing how a client set their price: if it came from competitor averaging, rebuild it from value delivered instead.
