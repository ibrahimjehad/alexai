Make a piece of content for every main customer concern, list the best converters in a spreadsheet, and let reps send them before or after calls.

**Steps.**
1. Make content that overcomes each major concern customers have about your product or service.
2. Notice which pieces convert: people DM you saying "I didn't know that, now I'm interested."
3. Put those in a sheet mapped by concern so every rep has them.
4. Reps send the matching piece before a call, or after one with a booked follow-up: "Let me send you this video, it might answer your concern. Let's touch base tomorrow after you watch it."

**Why.** The content does some selling for colder leads without extra rep time or training. Hormozi says sales rose when he did this in his fitness business and again at Gym Launch.

**Mindset.** Founders often resent their sales team ("they only want laydown leads"). Hormozi's view is the opposite: sales is the cash flow of the business, so a big part of the company should exist to support reps.

**When to use.** Any team selling to colder leads or longer cycles.
