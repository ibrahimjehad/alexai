A swim school doing ~$300k/year, spending ~$1,400/month on ads at ~$12 per lead, had only ever sold to referrals and was closing few cold leads.

**Diagnosis.** "It's just a sales problem." Hormozi: referrals don't count as selling; they had never really sold. With the script he expected 15-20 sales instead of 5 from the same spend.

**Call script.** CLOSER, with: closing the other doors (home pool, time, a relative), an authority three-pillar pitch (right environment, right expertise - master artist vs high-school kid analogy, right process), a time anchor ("you should have started in December; what's the first day this week?"), and payment by link while staying on the line.

**Offer changes.**
- Replace $100/month plus a $40 registration fee with a **$600, 12-session front end** with a guarantee: attend every session and if your kid can't float, we keep working with them until they do. (At 15-20 sales that's ~$12k upfront from $1,400 of ads.)
- **Halfway through**, sell the next level: "She's finished phase one of our six-phase water-mastery process." A ~$2,500 back end, crediting the $600, then downsell ("let's just get you through phase two").
- Add branded merch (swim caps, shorts) as an easy one-time upsell, like a karate gi, to offset acquisition cost.
