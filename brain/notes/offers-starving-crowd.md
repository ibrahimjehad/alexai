The single biggest advantage a business can have is a crowd that desperately wants what it sells.

Hot dog story: asked what one advantage he would want for a hot dog stand (location, quality, price, taste), a professor's answer was "a starving crowd". With enough demand you can have a mediocre product, a bad offer and no persuasion skill and still sell out (e.g. toilet paper at $100+ a roll early in COVID).

You do not create demand, you channel it. The rest of the book assumes at least a "normal market": one growing at about the rate of the overall economy, with common unmet needs in health, wealth or relationships.

**When to use.** Before building or fixing an offer: confirm real, growing demand exists. If it does not, no offer or sales skill will save the business.
