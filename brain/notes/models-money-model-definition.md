A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers (what you offer, when, and how) designed to increase how many customers you get, how much they pay and how fast they pay it.

**Core idea.** Find every problem a customer will have and offer to solve it at the moment they realize they need it. Because each solution reveals a new problem, good money models contain many offers in a specific order. If each offer arrives when the customer needs it, you can make as many offers as you like without feeling pushy.

**Standards.**
- A *good* money model makes more profit from a customer than it costs to acquire and serve them within the first 30 days (the bare minimum).
- A *$100M money model* makes enough profit from one customer in 30 days to acquire and serve at least two more, which removes cash as a constraint on growth.

**Building blocks.** Four offer types: attraction, upsell, downsell and continuity (see [models-four-offer-types]).

**Where it sits.** *$100M Offers* answers "what should I sell?", *$100M Leads* answers "how do I find buyers?", and *$100M Money Models* answers "how do I get them to buy (more, faster)?"

**When to use.** As the frame for any monetization review: map the current sequence of offers, find the gaps (no attraction offer, no next offer, nothing for people who say no, nothing recurring) and check 30-day profit per customer against CAC.
