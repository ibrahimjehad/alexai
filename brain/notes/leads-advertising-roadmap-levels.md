Six levels acquisition.com uses to locate a business on its advertising journey and know the primary action for reaching the next level.

## Levels
1. **Your friends know about your stuff.** One offer, one avatar, one platform. Primary action: warm outreach.
2. **You consistently let everyone you know about your stuff.** You know the exact inputs per engaged lead and scale them to your personal capacity. (Hormozi added paid ads with a case-study lead magnet but wishes he'd started content.) Primary action: as much warm outreach and content as possible, consistently.
3. **You get employees to help you advertise more.** You've maxed your personal inputs, not the platform. (He hired a videographer and media buyer.) Primary action: hire people to advertise profitably for you.
4. **Your product is good enough for consistent referrals.** Build goodwill; target 25%+ of customers from referrals. (He updated the product from customer feedback every two weeks and launched a big-incentive referral program.) Primary action: focus on product until referrals are consistent, then scale advertising again with a bigger team. Most people mess up here by letting product slip.
5. **You advertise in more places, more ways, with more people.** New audiences on your best platform → all placements and media types it supports → once the team is consistent, add another platform, lead getter or Core Four activity. (He advertised to potential affiliates, launching affiliate programs.) Primary action: advertise profitably with at least two methods on multiple platforms.
6. **You hire killers.** Experienced executives build departments per method/platform without you — hire experience, not potential, with strong incentives (equity, "expanding the pie"). Hormozi capped his earlier companies here; with this, acquisition.com's portfolio passed $100M then $200M+.
(Level 7 left open until he crosses $1B.)

## The $100M leads machine (end state)
Media team scaling content across platforms; regular warm offers for customers or affiliates; an audience that makes launches instantly profitable; teams scaling paid ads on multiple platforms; cold outreach team; affiliate manager launching/integrating affiliates; recruiters and agencies bringing lead getters; a product so good a third of customers refer; executives driving it all without you.

## Time horizon
Real business is messy; you must try many audiences, lead magnets, methods and platforms long enough to know. Expect 5–10 years for owners who don't know what to do; Hormozi and his wife took 10+ years to reach their first $100M net worth. Bigger goals need longer horizons — play games where if you wait, you win.

## When to use
Diagnosing a business's stage and choosing the single next primary action.
