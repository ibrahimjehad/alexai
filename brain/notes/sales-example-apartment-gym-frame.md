For the first Gym Launch ad, Hormozi had to sell memberships into a "gym" that was two empty apartments with barred doors above a convenience store near Washington DC.

**The frame.** He asked what would have to be true for this to be valuable: a discreet place where people (say, politicians) could train unrecognised. So the odd layout was "by design": "Many clients don't want to be recognised. If you see someone you know, don't go up to them." Then he bridged to his weight-loss buyers: "Ever been to a gym and not wanted people staring? That's why we have individual rooms," with a central area for socialising.

**Why it worked.** Nothing he said was untrue; the prospect's imagination filled in the rest, and the frame carried through the whole conversation. See [sales-frame-congruence-what-else-would-have-to-be-true].
