The front of the call only needs milestones; what you must know cold are the stories that break the three most common beliefs and the overcomes you'll use in the "red zone" after the price.

**1. Three stories.** One explains the vehicle (how it works, as an analogy); the other two break the top two obstacles prospects usually raise. For a gym-marketing offer: "It won't work in my market" and "I don't understand Facebook ads or technology." You should already know your top two from selling.

**2. Obstacle overcomes.** The red zone is no place to think about what to say. But you don't need hundreds: Hormozi's team has 10 to 15 overcomes per objection, and Dan Kennedy tells of a top 1950s vacuum rep who had to memorise 356 overcomes and actually used three. Try them, keep the couple that feel natural to how you talk, and use those.

**3. Questions.** For the opening, know the milestones you must hit before moving on (why they're here, the problem, past attempts), not exact wording.

**When to use.** Building a training plan for new reps; trimming a bloated objection binder.
