Every solution reveals a new problem; pick a narrow, meaningful problem for the lead magnet whose solution exposes a problem your core offer solves for money.

## The idea
Problems and solutions form a never-ending cycle, and small cycles sit inside larger ones. The winning lead magnet solves one small problem completely, and the problem it uncovers is the one you charge to solve.

Example (helping homeowners sell): selling a home is the broad solution. Steps before it are narrow problems: what the house is worth, how to raise its value, photos, cleaning, landscaping, small repairs, moving, staging. Solve one of these for free; the bigger problem (they still need to sell) becomes more obvious, and you've earned trust to sell your core offer.

Example (the book itself): *$100M Leads* solves the engaged-lead problem for businesses under $1M profit; once they cross $1M they qualify for the core offer (Acquisition.com investing).

## Steps
1. List the narrow problems your customer faces before, around and after your core offer.
2. Pick one that is narrow and meaningful.
3. Check that solving it reveals a problem your core offer solves. If yes, you have a winner.

## When to use
Step 1 of building any lead magnet, or when a lead magnet attracts people who never buy (it probably solves a problem unrelated to the core offer).
