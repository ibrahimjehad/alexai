A business grows in only two ways, more customers or more value per customer, and you get more customers through more, better and cheaper leads, obtained reliably.

## The idea
Hormozi grows every portfolio company with this frame. *$100M Leads* focuses on the first lever.

## Formula
1. **Get more customers**, by getting:
   - more leads,
   - better leads,
   - cheaper leads,
   - reliably (from many places, so no single source can kill you).
2. **Make each customer worth more** (pricing, upsells, retention; covered elsewhere).

Rule of thumb: all else equal, doubling leads doubles the business. The underlying problem for most businesses is simply not advertising enough, so potential customers don't know they exist.

## When to use
At the start of any growth plan: decide which lever you are pulling. For the "more customers" lever, audit lead volume, lead quality, cost per lead and how many independent sources feed the business.
