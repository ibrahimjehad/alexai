How a lead magnet is presented matters more than anything else, so test its headline, image and subheadline, in that order.

## The idea
Leads must notice a lead magnet before they consume it. Citing Ogilvy, most people read only the headline (about five times more than the rest), so writing the headline spends most of your advertising dollar. Improving the name, headline and display can 2x, 3x or 10x engagement. If nobody shows interest, nobody learns how good it is.

## Steps
1. Write two or more candidate headlines. Test the headline first; if you test only one thing, test this.
2. Then test images, then subheadlines.
3. Testing methods, from best to scrappiest: polls to your following (a few votes give a directional answer); a post on every platform asking people to reply "1" or "2" and counting; directly messaging people and asking.
4. Pick the winner. If people engage in droves, you have a winner. If poll voters ask when they can get it, you have a mega winner.

Small wording changes matter: adding "how to" or removing a single word swung tests 70/30.

## When to use
Step 4 of the lead magnet process, and before naming any product, book, course, ad or offer. Among the highest-leverage uses of time.
