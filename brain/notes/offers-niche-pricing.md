The more specifically a product targets one avatar and problem, the more that avatar will pay, often many times the generic price for nearly identical content.

**Ladder (from Dan Kennedy):**
- Time management: $19.
- Time management for sales professionals: $99 (tie it to one more closed deal).
- Time management for outbound B2B sales reps: $499 (one deal nets them $500+).
- Time management for outbound B2B power tools and gardening sales reps: $1,997.
Same core principles, up to about 100x the price, because the buyer thinks "this is made exactly for me" and the messaging speaks to them directly, so they get more real value from it. Likewise generic weight loss at $19 vs a program only for shift nurses at $1,997, even though both are "eat less, move more".

**Positioning template:** I solve [this problem] for [this specific person] in [this unique, counterintuitive way] that reverses their deepest fear.

**When to use.** When a product is priced like a commodity: narrow the avatar and rewrite the name and messaging for that avatar before changing the product.
