A step-by-step process for turning your personal contacts into your first customers and referrals.

## Steps
1. **Get your list.** Export every contact from your phone, every email account and every social platform, and count them. For many people this is their first ~1,000 leads.
2. **Pick a platform.** Start with the one where you have the most contacts.
3. **Personalise your message.** Open with something you actually know about them (check their profiles first) as your reason to reach out, e.g. "Saw you just had a baby, congrats! How's the baby doing? How are you?" You aren't asking for anything yet.
4. **Reach out to 100 people a day.** Contact each up to three times (once a day for three days, or until they reply); with physical mail, once a week. The first message is the slowest; it gets fast. Be okay with sucking at first.
5. **Warm them up when they respond.** Use ACA (acknowledge, compliment, ask) to steer the conversation toward your offer. On email or voicemail, be more upfront: a 2-3 sentence personalised opener, then go straight to the offer.
6. **Ask about their friends.** After a normal amount of conversation (3-4 exchanges by message or phone, 3-4 minutes in person), make the "do you know anyone who..." offer built from the value equation.
7. **Make it the easiest offer in the world: free.** Free in exchange for using it, giving feedback and leaving a review if earned.
8. **Start at the top.** After one platform is done, move to the platform with the next most contacts. 1,000 leads is about 10 days of work, or a month including follow-ups.
9. **Start charging.** When customers start referring, raise prices in steps every five customers.
10. **Keep your list warm.** Give regular value (email, social), then probe with the nine-word email; replies become top-priority warm outreach.

## When to use
Getting the first five clients for any new business, product or service. Advanced businesses use the same steps to re-engage old leads and customers.
