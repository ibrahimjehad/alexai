Expected conversion rates for warm outreach, used to predict customers per 100 reach-outs.

## Benchmarks
- About **1 in 5** contacts replies: 100 reach-outs, ~20 replies.
- About **1 in 5** repliers accepts the free offer: ~4 people.
- About **1 in 4** free takers converts to a paid offer later: **~1 customer per 100 warm reach-outs.**
- Rates vary with offer value and trust, and improve with practice.

## Money math
Assume 1% of reach-outs buy a $400 offer: 500 reach-outs/week means 5 customers, $2,000/week, ~$104,000/year from warm outreach alone (about twice US median household income when written).

## Daily commitment
Yourself; first five free; any channel; your contacts; first 4 hours of the day; 100 attempts a day with 2-3 follow-ups each; until you have customers.

## Tips
- Join communities of people using the same method for support and current tactics.
- Never do anything sketchy or legally borderline; it always comes back.
- About 20 hours of focused effort makes you decent at almost anything; most people delay the first hour. The first 10 days of 100 reach-outs teach more than everything you've read.

## When to use
To set targets and diagnose a warm-outreach funnel: low replies point to personalisation or list, low acceptance to the offer, low conversion to delivery/value.
