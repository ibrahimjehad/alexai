Hormozi's rules of thumb for services: consumer impulse ~$500-600; consumer high ticket ~$3,000-10,000; small-business cheap ~$400-800/month (call it $500); small-business mid-tier ~$2,000-3,000/month.

**Notes.**
- These are for services ("I'll help you do or fix something"), not assets like houses and cars.
- B2B depends on company size: Main Street owners can be budget-constrained; a company like Disney can buy a billion-dollar thing. $500/month is expensive for a consumer, cheap for a business.
- If you're not making money yet: add a zero to your price and ask what you'd deliver for that.

**When to use.** Sanity-checking a proposed price or tier.
