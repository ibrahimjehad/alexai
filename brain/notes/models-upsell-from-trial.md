Every trial user falls into one of three groups; meet each and make a tailored next offer.

1. **They like it.** They are already set to auto-bill. Meet anyway and offer a longer term, a higher-value version, or both; successful customers get even more from better offers.
2. **They hate it.** Ask what they wish were different, agree, and be angrier about it than they are (never blame them; only one person can be angry and it should be you). Ask for a chance to make it up; now that you understand their needs, offer your higher-level option. Hormozi converts about half of these.
3. **They don't use it.** Reach out several times before it gets to that point. Offer to waive the fee if they meet with you, then get them back on track or offer something better. Hormozi personally avoids billing non-starters: a small fee is not worth a one-star review.

**When to use.** Mid-trial check-ins and trial end for any trial or challenge.
