Admit a "downside" of success, such as their identity changing, so the prospect hears you talking as if the result is certain and trusts you more.

**Script (weight loss).** "Since when have you been overweight?" "Since I was 16." "Do you identify with it? Is it part of who you are?" "I guess I've always struggled." "Then I'm warning you: once we get you to your goal weight, your identity will change. People will treat you differently and it'll mess with you."

**Why.** A warning about life after the result presupposes the result, and a seller who volunteers a negative sounds honest. The prospect thinks "he's really talking to me like I'm going to get there."

**When to use.** Transformation offers (health, money, status) during value building, right before you move to belief and the close.
