Get the highest number of likely buyers to see your ad by seeding a lookalike audience with your best lists, then layering simple filters on top.

## The idea
Choosing a platform is the first round of targeting (a 100M-user platform already excludes ~99% of the world). The second round happens inside the platform. The right message to the wrong audience falls on deaf ears no matter how good the ad.

## Two targeting methods (use separately or combined)
1. **Lookalike audience** — upload a list and the platform finds a bigger, similar audience (once you meet its minimum list size). Bigger and higher-quality seed lists produce more responsive lookalikes. Build the seed in order of quality: current/past customers → add warm-outreach list if too small → add cold-outreach list to hit the minimum. Forcing size can make the lookalike broad; that's acceptable.
2. **Chosen factors** — age, income, gender, interests, time, location, etc. Exclude ranges that never buy (e.g. nobody under 25 or over 45 ever bought → exclude them); show car-parts ads around car shows/channels; add "pets" interest if only pet owners buy.

## Local businesses
A local market is already ~0.1% of a nation, so add few extra filters — "as specific as possible, but no further." More filters = more efficient but you "burn through" the audience faster. Early wins from small specific audiences fund broader audiences later; that's how you scale.

## Action steps
1. Bring all lead lists into one place, separated into customers, warm outreach, cold outreach (later add: people who opted in from ads but didn't buy).
2. Build a lookalike from those lists in order of quality.
3. Add filters on top if the platform allows.
4. If you can't build a lookalike, start by targeting interests.

## When to use
Setting up or rebuilding any paid campaign's audience.
