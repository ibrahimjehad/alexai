A portfolio company broke past $1M a month by fully automating scraping, writing and sending cold emails.

## Numbers
- One virtual assistant using several tools sends **2,000 emails a day** (~60,000 a month).
- This produces **40 engaged leads a day** (2%; response dropped because personalisation was stripped out).
- 10% of engaged leads buy, giving **4 new customers a day**, past the 100-customers-a-month barrier.
- Customer value ~$20,000; the company joined at $250K a month (then Acquisition.com's minimum).

## Lesson
Automated volume can beat higher response rates when the offer value is high. It breaks beliefs about how much outreach is possible.

## When to use
To show what a mature, automated outbound engine looks like and to model volume targets for high-ticket B2B offers.
