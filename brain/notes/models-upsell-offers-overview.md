An upsell is simply whatever you offer next; every solution reveals a new problem, and you sell the solution to the problem your last offer revealed.

**Why they matter.** Upsells often make most of the profit and make or break a money model. The thing you sell most is often not what you profit most on (see [models-example-burger-upsell-math]).

**What to upsell (three directions).**
- *More* (quantity): two burgers instead of one.
- *Better* (quality): sirloin instead of mystery meat.
- *New or complementary*: fries and a drink with that.

**Why upsells fail.**
1. Wrong thing: something they do not want, too different, or not solving their problem.
2. Wrong time: before they have experienced the problem.
3. Wrong way: they do not believe you.
(or a combination.)

**The four Hormozi uses.** Classic upsell, menu upsell, anchor upsell, rollover upsell.

**Maxim.** "Solve rich people problems. They pay better." Upsells are how many businesses go from burning cash to printing it.

**When to use.** Stage two of a money model, once an attraction offer brings in customers; goal is to push 30-day profit well above CAC plus delivery cost.
