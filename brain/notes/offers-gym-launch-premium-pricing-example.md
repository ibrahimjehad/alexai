How Gym Launch charged far more than any competitor and still gave clients a deal.

**Origin.** Hormozi did 33 in-person gym turnarounds in 18 months (fix and relaunch in 21 days, average about $42K extra sales in 21 days, fee = 100% of the revenue brought in). At up to eight gyms a month travel became the constraint. When one owner insisted on help, Hormozi coached him remotely while the owner did the work; the gym made almost $44K in 30 days (4x its prior month). Proving the process worked "done-with-you" instead of "done-for-you" removed the travel cap; they went on to sell 4,000+ gyms.

**Pricing.** Competitors: $500/month full-service marketing at the low end, one at $5,000. Gym Launch: $16,000 for a 16-week done-with-you intensive (about 3x the top competitor, 32x the lowest), then about 35% upsold into a three-year, $42,000/year agreement. The average gym owner takes home about $35K/year, so many committed half a year's income.

**Value proof** (survey of 158 gyms after 11 months): +$239K/year top-line revenue, +$160K/year recurring revenue, profit up from about $2.9K to $8.9K/month (3.1x), +67 clients, churn down from 10.7% to 6.8% monthly, +$4.4K/month retail, average price per member up from $129 to $167.

**Framing that sells it.** To his worried father: "If I made you $239K extra this year for about 15 hours a week, paid as you earn, would you pay $42K?" Answer: yes. Charge a fraction of the value created and the price stops looking extreme.
