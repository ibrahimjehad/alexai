On a price objection, offer a discount in exchange for advertising that is worth more to you than the discount costs.

**Example deal.** "$100 off if you (1) leave a review on all sites, (2) record a video testimonial, (3) post publicly at the beginning, middle and end of the program showing progress, and (4) introduce me to two friends who'd want to do this with you. Deal?"

**Why it is not a plain discount.** It is a trade: they give something to get something, so you are not simply cutting the price of the same thing.

**When to use.** Price objections from good-fit prospects; new businesses that need social proof.
