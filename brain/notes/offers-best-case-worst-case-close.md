Pair a strong guarantee with a close that shows both outcomes are safe, and that only walking away is risky.

Hormozi's version: best case, you get the body you want and your money goes toward staying on for your long-term goal; worst case, you tell me we were not good enough, I write you a check and you got six weeks of free training. Both are risk-free; "the only thing guaranteed not to help you is walking out of here today." He credits the line with a lot of sales.

**When to use.** At the close, once a satisfaction or refund guarantee is part of the offer and the prospect is hesitating over risk.
