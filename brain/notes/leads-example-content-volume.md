Hormozi paid ~$120K for four calls with a big influencer; the "secret" was simply far more volume.

## The story
- Call one: post regularly on every platform. Over 12 months his audience grew by 200,000+.
- Call two: he asked for the blueprint. The influencer compared their accounts platform by platform (one Instagram post vs three; one LinkedIn post a week vs five a day): "You just got to do more."
- Over the next six months he put out 10x the content and added ~1.2M followers; the audience grew 10x faster.
- Later framing: a ~5M audience in 24 months, recording only about two days a month.

## Lesson
Volume works. Buying someone's experience saves the time of figuring it out yourself.

## When to use
To justify volume targets to a client who posts occasionally and expects growth.
