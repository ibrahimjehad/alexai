The whole book as a checklist: the purpose of a money model, its standards, and the 15 named offers with their one-line scripts.

**Trilogy.** *$100M Offers*: what to sell (an offer so good people feel stupid saying no). *$100M Leads*: how to find buyers (advertise). *$100M Money Models*: how to get them to buy (a money model).

**Principles.**
1. A money model is a series of offers that increases how many customers you get, how much they pay and how fast.
2. Good: more profit per customer than the cost to get and serve them in the first 30 days (bare minimum).
3. $100M: more profit from one customer than the cost to get and serve many customers in 30 days, removing cash as a limit on scaling.
4. Four offer types: attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity.

**Attraction (free or discounted, often plus a better deal at a higher price).**
- Win your money back: hit the goal, get your money back (cash or credit).
- Giveaways: enter to win a big prize; everyone else gets it discounted.
- Decoy: advertise free/cheap, present a far better premium beside it.
- Buy X, get Y free: the more and more valuable the free stuff, the more people buy.
- Pay less now or pay more later: full price later (guaranteed) or discount plus bonuses now.

**Upsells (whatever you offer next: more, better, new).**
- Classic: "You can't have X without Y."
- Menu: "You don't need that, you need this."
- Anchor: "No worries, if you don't care about X, this may be a better fit."
- Rollover: "Since you already spent $500, I'll credit that toward staying a full year."

**Downsells (whatever you offer after a no).**
- Payment plan: "When do you get paid? Half now, half then."
- Trial with penalty: "Do X, Y and Z and I'll let you start for free."
- Feature downsell: "If you're OK without a guarantee, I can knock off $400."

**Continuity (ongoing value, ongoing payments).**
- Continuity bonus: "Sign up today and you also get [valuable thing]."
- Continuity discount: free time, now or later, for signing today.
- Waived fee: month to month with a startup fee, or commit and it is waived (pay it if you cancel inside the term).

**Build order.** One stage at a time: customers reliably, then self-financing, then customers paying for other customers, then long-term value, then maximum ad spend.

**When to use.** Quick orientation before a strategy session, or to pick candidate offers for a client.
