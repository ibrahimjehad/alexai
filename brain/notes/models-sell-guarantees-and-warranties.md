If you already provide a guarantee, warranty or replacement policy for free, offer it as a paid add-on at about 5 to 50% of the price.

**Example.** An art studio replaced damaged portraits free. Hormozi had them ask customers to pay 10% extra for it; 30% of customers bought what used to be free, pure profit.

**When to use.** Products or services with existing free protection, especially physical goods. Weigh it against using a free guarantee to lift conversion (see [offers-guarantees]).
