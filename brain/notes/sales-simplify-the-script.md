Cut the script to short, plain words (below third-grade reading level), stop feature-stacking, and turn as much as possible into questions.

**Steps.**
1. Run the script through a reading-level checker (he uses Hemingway) and rewrite until it reads below third grade. Newer reps use big words to sound impressive and just confuse both sides; "if you can't explain it to a child, you don't understand it."
2. Remove technical features. Business-owner prospects don't care.
3. Shorten. Choose the pitch style (detailed vs three-pillar) and cut everything else.
4. Convert statements into questions: closers should be asking, not talking, and nobody can say "your question is wrong."

**When to use.** Any script with low close rates or low rep adherence. Hormozi says he does this in almost every company Acquisition.com takes on (see [sales-example-script-simplification]).
