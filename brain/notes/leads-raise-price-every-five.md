Referrals are the litmus test that you're good enough to charge; then step prices up every five customers.

## Steps
1. Once people start referring, swap "free" in your script for **80% off** for the next five customers.
2. Then 60% off for the next five, then 40% off, and so on.
3. Keep raising about **20% every five** customers until you find your sweet spot. There's no rule saying stop.

## Why it works
You charge as your skill grows, and "prices go up every five" is real urgency because they actually do.

## When to use
Right after a free launch phase, or for any operator unsure what to charge. Let demand and referrals set the pace.
