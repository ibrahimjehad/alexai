Cut delivery ideas by cost and value, then stack the survivors into one bundle.

**Trim.**
1. Estimate the cost to you of each delivery idea.
2. Remove high-cost, low-value items first.
3. Remove low-cost, low-value items.
4. Keep low-cost/high-value and high-cost/high-value items.
To judge value, ask which items the person will (1) value financially, (2) believe make success more likely, (3) feel reduce effort and sacrifice, and (4) get the result faster.
For something valuable but too costly (moving in to shop, cook and train with the client), step back one level at a time to a version you can deliver at scale, or raise the price until it is worth it.

**Stack.** Group the kept items under each problem into a named component, give each a stated value, and present them together so the buyer thinks "I get all this for only that?"

Reserve expensive one-on-one time for the biggest value adds; if a cheaper vehicle gives the same value, use it.

**When to use.** Step 5 of offer creation.
