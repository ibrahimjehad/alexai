Since you set customer expectations, set them slightly lower than what you deliver so the product feels remarkable — but stop lowering before it hurts sales.

## The idea
Expectations shape the experience (an over-hyped movie disappoints; a panned one surprises). Over-promising "everything and the kitchen sink" made fulfillment a nightmare for Hormozi; inching promises down while keeping quality created room to over-deliver and produced referrals.

## Decision rule
Gradually lower the promises in your offers. Keep lowering until your close rate starts to drop, then stop. That point maximizes both customers won and goodwill built.

## When to use
When customers are satisfied but not raving, or fulfillment struggles to meet sales promises.
