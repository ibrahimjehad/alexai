A plain one-line question email that surfaces which people on a warm list are currently interested.

## Template
"Are you still looking to [desire]?"
No images, no links, no extras: just the question.
Examples: ...buy your dream home? ...get more sales leads? ...tone up your arms? ...open an online store? ...start a YouTube channel?

## How to use
1. Keep the list warm first by giving regular value (email, social).
2. Send the question.
3. Treat every reply as an engaged lead and make those your top priority for warm outreach.

Hormozi says this is among the first things he does when he invests in a new business. A warm list treated well is a consistent, growing source of engaged leads.

## When to use
Any business with a dormant email/SMS list, past leads or past customers.
