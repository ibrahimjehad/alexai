Hormozi's seven-step process for building a lead magnet so good people feel stupid saying no, even though it's free.

## Steps
1. **Pick the problem and who to solve it for.** Choose a narrow, meaningful problem whose solution reveals a next problem your core offer solves (problem-solution cycle).
2. **Decide how to solve it.** Choose a lead magnet type: reveal their problem, sample/trial, or one step of a multi-step process.
3. **Decide how to deliver it.** Software, information, services or physical products.
4. **Test what to name it.** Test headline, then image, then subheadline.
5. **Make it easy to consume.** Offer every format and access path you can.
6. **Make it darn good.** Give away the secrets, sell the implementation.
7. **Make it easy for them to say they want more.** A clear call to action plus reasons to act now.

(Step zero: if you're struggling to get leads, make an amazing lead magnet.)

## Principle
Grand slam offer principles work as well or better for free stuff as for paid stuff. Having several insanely valuable offers, some free, is good: the business that provides the most value wins.

## When to use
Whenever you design or audit a lead magnet. Walk through the steps in order and check each one before spending on promotion.
