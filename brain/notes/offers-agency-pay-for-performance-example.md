Worked numbers showing how a Grand Slam Offer multiplies response, close rate and price at the same ad spend (based on a lead-gen agency using one of Hormozi's software companies, rounded).

**Old commodity offer:** $1,000 down then $1,000/month retainer for agency services.
$10,000 ad spend -> 300,000 impressions -> 0.13% response -> 40 appointments -> 75% show (30) -> 16% close -> 5 clients x $1,000 = $5,000. ROAS 0.5:1: you lose money upfront and only break even after month two, assuming nobody churns.

**New Grand Slam Offer:** one-time payment, no retainer; client covers ad spend; the agency generates and works the leads and is only paid if people show up; guarantee of 20 people in the first month or the next month free; plus the full industry playbook free (daily sales coaching, tested scripts, price points and offers, sales recordings).
Same $10,000 and 300,000 impressions -> 0.33% response (2.5x) -> 100 appointments -> 75% show (75) -> 37% close (2.3x) -> 28 clients x about $4,000 one-time (4x price) = $112,000. ROAS 11.2:1.

**Takeaway.** About 2.5 x 2.3 x 4 = 22.4x more cash collected upfront. You are paid to acquire customers, so cash flow stops being the bottleneck and capacity to deliver becomes the limit. Hormozi reports going from $500K/year to $28M/year in 18 months on under $1M of ad spend with this kind of offer.

Note: the transcript reads the new price as "39.97"; the 4x multiplier and $112K total imply roughly $3,997-$4,000.
