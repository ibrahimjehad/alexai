When launching a new product or service, give it to the first five people free in exchange for using it, giving feedback and leaving a review; the goal is learning, not earning.

## Script (paraphrased)
"Since I'm only taking five people, I can give you all the attention you need to get brag-worthy results, and it's free as long as you promise to (1) use it, (2) give me feedback, and (3) leave a great review if you think it deserves one. Sound fair?" Be honest, don't pretend to be more advanced than you are.

## Why (the number matters less than the reasons)
1. You get reps making offers with low stakes.
2. You probably aren't good yet, and people forgive a lot when they haven't paid.
3. You get better by doing more, so a few "guinea pigs" work out the kinks.
4. People who get value, especially free, leave testimonials, give feedback and refer friends.

Free customers make money three ways: they convert to paying, they refer paying customers, and their testimonials attract paying customers.

**Stack early yeses.** Hormozi's first "sale" asked clients to donate to a charity of their choice instead of paying him: low pressure, still invested. **Learning vs earning:** ignore "don't undervalue yourself"; your product isn't valuable yet, so learn first and earn later.

## When to use
Any brand-new offer or operator with no track record. Stop when referrals start (see raise price every five).
