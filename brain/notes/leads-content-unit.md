All audience-growing content is built from content units, the smallest piece of material that hooks, retains and rewards attention.

## The idea
Content grows an audience by rewarding people for consuming it. To be rewarded, a person needs a reason to consume, has to pay attention long enough, and must get that reason satisfied. Reversed, that gives three jobs:
1. **Hook:** give them a reason to redirect attention to you. Measured by the % who start consuming. Levers: topic, headline, format.
2. **Retain:** keep them consuming. Main driver: curiosity through embedded questions (lists, steps, stories).
3. **Reward:** satisfy the reason they started. Measured ultimately by audience growth.

A unit can be tiny (a tweet, a meme, a jingle) that does all three at once, which is why such things go viral.

**Short vs long form:** not much difference. Longer content is just more units linked together (hook-retain-reward repeated). Linking more good units takes more skill, like a new comic getting five minutes while only masters get an hour. Start short and build up.

## When to use
To plan, script or critique any piece of content on any platform. Diagnose a weak piece by asking which of the three failed.
