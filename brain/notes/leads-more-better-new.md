Any lead-getting activity can be boosted three ways: do more of it, do it better, or do it somewhere new — usually in that order.

## The framework
1. **More** — crank volume to max capacity. Even with zero improvement, doubling inputs (reach-outs, posts, ads, spend) gets more engaged leads. Biggest gains often come from here. Tool: the Rule of 100.
2. **Better** — test to improve conversion at each step, focusing on the constraint (biggest drop-off). Do more until it breaks (CAC climbs too high), then fix what broke. More and better work together: hold volume while fixing the constraint.
3. **New** — only when returns from more/better fall below what the same effort would earn somewhere new: new placement → new platform → new Core Four activity.

## Order decision rule
- Under ~$1M/year profit: More → Better → New (not enough volume yet for percentage gains to matter).
- Above ~$1M/year profit: flip to Better → More → New (improvements become the cheapest, highest-return lever).

## Diagnostic question
Before investing, Hormozi asks: "What's stopping them from doing 10x what they currently do?" If nothing, do more. If something, make it better first. Exhaust more/better before new.

## Why combinations help
Each added channel compounds with the others: money, systems and experience from one method help master the next (content makes ads perform better; outreach plus content warms leads). Hormozi's own businesses each grew on one or two channels (e.g. gyms on content + paid ads, Gym Launch on paid ads + cold outreach, Prestige Labs on affiliates, acquisition.com on content) — master one and you can always feed yourself.

## When to use
Whenever a business plateaus or wants more leads from what it already does; the default growth review for any lead channel.
