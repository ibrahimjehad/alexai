Give a bonus worth more than the first continuity payment to anyone who joins today; advertise the bonus, not the membership.

**Bonus forms.** Products: many small items or one big item that complements the subscription. Services: a defined program, onboarding, setup or a value-adding feature. Bonuses and discounts are interchangeable: add good stuff (bonuses) and remove bad stuff (discounts) to get more people to start, with urgency for joining now. The bonus can be sold standalone or available only with membership.

**Examples.**
- Dog food: every toy the company has made ($800 value) free when you join $59/month shipments; plus a new toy each month.
- Service: a $1,000 short-term accelerator free with a $100/month membership; VIP perks (first-in-line event access, longer support hours, better reps).
- Newsletter: 40 past issues (stated value $15,880) free when you join today at $399/month after a 30-day trial; pay today to lock a lifetime rate of $200/month, plus early digital access and a monthly print copy.

**Rules.**
1. **Focus on the bonus, not the membership.** "Get this free valuable thing" beats "join my membership".
2. **Bonuses work like upsells**: more of the same (two years of back issues), complementary (nutrition with a gym membership), upgrade (gold membership for the price of bronze, limited).
3. **Keep bonuses related** to the core offer, or you attract the wrong customers (free T-shirt for T-shirt printing, not for tech services).
4. **Use things you already have or do** (back issues, onboarding you do anyway; put a price on it).
5. **Cross formats**: physical bonuses with digital memberships (hat, shirt, tool) and digital bonuses with physical ones (live-streamed classes for a boxing gym). Often lowers CAC by more than the bonus costs, even if some take the bonus and run.
6. **Realistic values.** Big anchors help only if believable; products you have actually sold give real prices.
7. **Anchor the bonus, then reveal how to get it free.** Sell its benefits and price first, then "Do you want to know how you can get this for free?" Join as a member today, or buy it for [big number]. Then stack more bonuses with individual dollar values.
8. **Force continuity** by making bonuses available only to members.
9. **Optionally require commitment** (3, 6, 12+ months) for the bonus: more commitments, fewer takers. Start simple: standalone bonus plus month-to-month.

**When to use.** Any membership or subscription; especially to convert front-end customers (challenges, trials) into continuity.
