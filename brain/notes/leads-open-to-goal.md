The step after the Rule of 100: instead of committing to a number of actions, commit to a number of outcomes and work until you reach them, however long it takes.

## Origin
A successful gym chain let sales managers set their own schedules on one condition: sign five new members a day. Done by lunch? Leave early. Takes 18 hours? So be it. Elite entrepreneurs and salespeople across industries use some version of this.

## Rule
- Set a daily outcome goal (e.g. X engaged leads or customers).
- Work until it's hit — maybe 50 actions, maybe 5,000 a day for years.
- Drop "doing your best"; do what's required. Sometimes your best needs to get better.
- Maxims: "Do more than they do and you'll have more than they have." When you think "why bother?", remember this is where most people stop.

## When to use
Once the Rule of 100 is a habit; for owners or sales reps with outcome targets.
