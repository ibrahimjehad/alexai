Hired for a day to train a mortgage-leads company's sales team, Hormozi spent the morning rebuilding the script into CLOSER and question-based selling, then sat with the weakest rep.

**The moment.** He asked the rep, John, "How good are the leads?" John hesitated: "Well, you know..." Hormozi stopped him and showed how a believer answers: these leads are unbelievable; I'm studying for my real-estate exam so I can use them myself; I'm sending them to my aunt who's a realtor; I'm trying to get my brother in; I don't know how long I'll work here because these leads are killing it.

**The point.** If you believe in the product, you don't need every sales skill; you sell the right way because you want to help. That is why the most important part of the sale is the product, and why conviction fixes tone faster than training ([sales-build-conviction]).
