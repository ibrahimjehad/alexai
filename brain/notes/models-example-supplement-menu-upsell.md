Three accidents across three years produced the four menu-upsell tactics, taking supplement sales from zero to tens of thousands per launch.

**2013: A/B and card on file.** After 19 straight nutrition consults with no sales, he forgot his science pitch and simply asked a client, "Chocolate or vanilla?" She picked one, then a pre-workout flavour, and agreed to "use the card we have on file". He sold the next 20 customers in a row and emptied his $5,000 shelf.

**2014: Prescription.** A client wanted exact instructions, so he wrote dosages and timings on paper. The next client asked for the same; he wrote them on the order form and asked, "Want to use the card on file?" She bought everything. Detailed, personalized instructions outsell vague ones. Monthly supplement sales reached $5K to $10K per challenge group.

**2016: Unselling.** Out of stock while launching other gyms, he told a client where to get a cheaper substitute, then started crossing out items she did not need (weight gainer, testosterone booster). The goodwill made her buy everything he did recommend. His supplement sales at launches covered his flights, hotel and ad costs.
