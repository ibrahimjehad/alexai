Contact each lead several times across several channels as if you really needed to reach them, then restart the list a few months later.

## Multiple times, multiple ways
- Most people never follow up. Act like you're trying to reach your parents in an emergency: call, text, voicemail, then again soon after.
- People respond to different channels (Hormozi ignores calls but answers DMs). Multi-channel, quick follow-up signals importance and triggers fear of missing out.
- Hormozi emails first, because most people don't reply, which gives a natural reason for the next touch: "Calling to follow up on my email."
- Expect 2-3 conversations before a high-ticket cold sale.
- **Respect opt-outs:** if someone asks you to stop, stop. Turning neutral into yes is more efficient than no into yes, and reputation follows you.

## Recycle the list
When you finish the list, start again at the top **3-6 months later**, because people (1) never saw the first messages, (2) saw them at a bad time, or (3) their circumstances changed (e.g. weight gained over the holidays). Same list, a new batch of engaged leads.

## When to use
Designing cadences for any outbound campaign, and before buying new lists (re-run the old ones first).
