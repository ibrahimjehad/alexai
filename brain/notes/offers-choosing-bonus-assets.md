The best bonuses cost time or money to create once but can be given away endlessly, and feel like they could stand alone.

**Build a bonus vault.**
1. Create checklists, tools, swipe files, scripts and templates: hard to make yourself, easy to use.
2. Record every workshop, webinar, event and interview; reuse recordings to crush specific objections.
3. Negotiate group discounts and referral commissions with adjacent businesses.
Information products work well (high perceived value, near-zero cost, just another login), as do tickets to virtual events and a month of a higher service tier with fixed cost, which also doubles as an upsell into that tier. The longer you are in business, the bigger the vault.

**Bonus vs core.** Pull out items with "wow factor" that should not get lost in the mix: distinct, short but high-value pieces. A checklist or infographic (e.g. a product launch map) may not justify a big price alone but feels very valuable as a bonus.

**When to use.** When assembling or refreshing an offer stack.
