Wake early, start work immediately, and protect the first ~8 hours from meetings so your most productive hours go to your most important work — usually advertising.

## The three habits
1. Wake early (4–5 a.m.) — which really means go to bed early.
2. Get right to work — no rituals or routines; coffee and go.
3. No meetings until noon — uninterrupted, fully focused work.
The magic isn't early rising; it's a long stretch of uninterrupted work right after a long stretch of uninterrupted sleep. Set the daily goal based on what you know you can do in that block; handle fires, people and admin only afterward. Hormozi wrote the whole book in this block because it's an asset that brings him more companies.

If ~12-hour days overwhelm you, throttle back a few hours and build up.

## When to use
Structuring an owner's day to execute the one-page advertising plan.
