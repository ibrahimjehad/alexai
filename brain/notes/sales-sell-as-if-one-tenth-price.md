Imagine your offer cost 80-90% less and you knew it would sell out; then sell at your real price with exactly that tone, posture and scarcity, changing only the number at the end.

**Why.** The price only feels big to the rep. A director selling a service worth ~$50,000 agreed that at $10,000 he would easily fill capacity, and that he would sound completely different: relaxed, unhurried, "if you want in, do it soon, we'll be full". All of that exists between his ears, and prospects take your lead because they can feel when you don't need their money.

**Steps.**
1. Cut your price by about 80% in your head. How crazy a deal would that be?
2. How would you sound, pause, sit? How sure would you be the slots fill?
3. Ask: if everyone you spoke to today, this week and this month had bought on the spot, how would you talk? Talk that way.
4. Use real scarcity language only where it's true ("it's unlikely I'll even get through this week's calls").

**When to use.** Before every sales block, and in rep coaching when tone, not script, is the gap.
