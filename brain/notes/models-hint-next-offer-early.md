Mention early on that people always ask about your next offer and that you will cover it at the end; it primes demand so more people buy when you finally offer it.

**Script shape.** "Everyone asks when my next [program] starts because it sells out fast; I'll get to it at the end, but please pay attention to this part first because I want you to get [promised result]."

**Origin.** In nutrition consults, people kept interrupting to ask about supplements. Hormozi started saying everyone wants to know which supplements to buy and he would get there; this implied that everyone buys them, the nods showed real demand, and more people bought when he finally offered.

**When to use.** Workshops, webinars, presentations, consultations, any delivery that ends with an offer.
