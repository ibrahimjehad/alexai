A fur-coat dealer advertised free earmuffs with coat storage, then charged to store the earmuffs and closed with a question designed to get a "no".

**The play.** Customers came in to collect free earmuffs and store their coats. Staff said, "Great, and we'll store those too for $30. You don't want to store anything else, do you?" Customers said no, and paid $30 to store something they had just received free.

**Tactics shown.**
- A free bonus can create the problem your upsell solves (the earmuffs cost materials and labour, but paid for themselves through storage).
- "You don't want anything else, do you?" turns a reflexive "no" into a purchase.
