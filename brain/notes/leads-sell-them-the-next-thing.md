Customers who love your product want more; if you don't sell them the next thing, someone else will — and every extra product is another thing they can refer friends to.

## The idea
Sell either a new thing or more of the thing they bought. This adds goodwill, extends customer lifetime, and creates new referral opportunities: in one weight-loss company, many customers who didn't refer the first-year product did refer friends to the pricier follow-up. Owners obsess over front-end offers and neglect the back end; customers who fall off rarely refer.

## Action steps
- Treat every customer like it's the first time you're selling them.
- Make the next offer more compelling than the first.
- Remind them to buy more after each big win.

## When to use
Designing back-end offers and post-purchase journeys.
