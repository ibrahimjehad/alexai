Start content in a narrow niche you can own, then widen step by step as your track record grows.

## The idea
A small local business making general business content loses to people with better track records. Narrow to what you do and where you do it (e.g. plumbing in one town) and become king of that puddle. Then expand: local business pond, then brick-and-mortar chains lake, then general business ocean.

## When to use
Choosing a content niche for a small or new business, or when general content gets no traction.
