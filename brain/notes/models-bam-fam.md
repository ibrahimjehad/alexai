End every appointment by scheduling the next one; more meetings mean more upsell opportunities and more people upsold.

**Rule.** Do not let customers leave without booking. A customer should know the next time they will see you and why before they leave (a principle Hormozi credits to a public-company CEO friend). Agree on the when and why right then.

**When to use.** Any service, clinic, coaching or account-management business with repeat interactions.
