A smart operator with a great product and a zero-risk offer still declined because his market was shrinking 25% a year.

Lloyd sold software that let newspapers add digital ad products, charging only a share of revenue he added (zero risk for the buyer), and was a natural salesman. Yet the business shrank year after year. Product, offer, sales and team were fine; the market (newspapers) was dying. When COVID hit he pivoted to automated mask manufacturing, undercut import prices, and within five months was doing millions per month: same entrepreneur, different market.

**Lesson.** Entrepreneurs hate quitting and will keep fixing everything except the market. Check market growth first. Illustrates the "growing" indicator in [offers-market-selection-four-indicators].
