Aim to charge many times what delivery costs (Hormozi says think up to 100x, not 2-3x), as long as the buyer still gets more value than they pay.

Drop notions of what is "fair". Large companies charge far above marginal cost: phone carriers pay pennies per extra user, drug makers pennies per pill, media companies little per user, yet all charge heavily. A big gap between cost and price is the only route to outsized wealth. The constraint is value: never charge more than the product is worth, and if enough value is created, a very high price is still a steal.

**When to use.** When an owner sets price by cost-plus or feels guilty about high margins: anchor the price to value delivered, then check that cost of delivery is a small fraction of it.
