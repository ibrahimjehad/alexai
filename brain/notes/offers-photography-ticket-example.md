A portfolio company in photography used the book's tactics over two years to raise average ticket from $300 to $1,500 (5x).

Weekly profit rose from about $1,000 to $38,000 (38x), while time spent per customer fell and satisfaction rose, which funded expansion to multiple locations. The method: find what customers valued most, triple down on it and ruthlessly cut everything else. Customers "voted with their dollars" that the new offer was better.

**Lesson.** Because costs are mostly fixed, a price increase backed by more perceived value flows almost entirely to profit, so a 5x ticket can mean a far larger profit multiple.
