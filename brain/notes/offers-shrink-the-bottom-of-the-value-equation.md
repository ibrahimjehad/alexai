Big claims (dream outcome, proof) are easy to copy; reducing time delay and effort is harder and therefore more differentiating.

Beginner marketers make bigger and bigger promises: easy, lazy and undifferentiated, since anyone can promise. The best companies compete on making things immediate, seamless and effortless: Apple made the phone effortless, Amazon made buying one click with near-immediate delivery, Netflix made TV instant. Hormozi shifted his own focus over time to this "hard stuff", and believes the market rewards it most.

**When to use.** When an offer already has a strong promise but still struggles: look for ways to cut waiting and customer effort rather than inflating the claim.
