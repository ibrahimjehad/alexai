Once ads break even or better, set spend by working backward from how many customers you want or can handle, padded 20% for falling efficiency.

## Formula
1. Customers wanted (or capacity) next month x CAC = base spend.
2. Pad by 20% because ads get less efficient as they scale.
3. Divide by 30 for daily budget, then commit to it.
Example: capacity 100 customers x $100 CAC = $10,000 → +20% = $12,000/30 days = $400/day.

Ask "how many customers do I want/can I handle?" instead of "how much should I spend?" If the number terrifies you, you're doing it right — trust the data. Most people never scale because they won't commit.

## When to use
Phase three (profitable ads), when planning monthly growth targets.
