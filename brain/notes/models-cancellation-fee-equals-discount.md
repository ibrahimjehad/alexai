Decide cancellation terms up front; Hormozi's favourite is that cancelling early costs exactly the discount the customer received for committing.

**Example.** A customer got $600 in discounts for a commitment; they may cancel any time by paying $600, which puts them back on the month-to-month rate. Simple to explain and fair.

**Other options.** 30 or 60 days' notice, cancellation fees, cancel anytime. Light terms get more sign-ups and more cancellations; harsher terms get fewer of both.

**Make cancelling easy to find.** If customers cannot complain or cancel inside your business, they vanish and complain outside it (one-star reviews). A clear route gives you a chance to save them.

**When to use.** Every continuity discount offer.
