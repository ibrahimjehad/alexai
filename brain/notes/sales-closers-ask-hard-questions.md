If the first minutes tell you this call won't close, shift the frame with challenging questions; with a prospect who came to prove nothing works, agree with them and let them argue the other side.

**Script.** Instead of "No, I think you can succeed" (which they'll fight), say: "It sounds like you're out of luck. It sounds like there's no way for your business to succeed. Does that sound about right?" They will start selling you on why you're wrong, and in doing so prove to themselves there is a way.

**Why.** People take contrarian stances. Arguing for their success makes them defend failure; voicing their failure makes them defend success.

**When to use.** Within the first five to ten minutes when you sense no close, or the prospect is commanding the conversation. A "state shift" is needed before you can pitch ([sales-dont-pitch-until-you-know-yes]).
