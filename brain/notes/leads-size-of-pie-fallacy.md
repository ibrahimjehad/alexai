Owners mistake the tiny slice of the market they reach — one channel, one platform, one style — for the whole market, and then think they've maxed out.

## The idea
A small business uses one Core Four activity on one platform in one way, sees three or four competitors doing the same, and assumes they split the entire market (the "me / me + 1 / me + 3 competitors" pie picture). In reality the market is far bigger. Saying "I'm as big as I can get" is emotionally easier than "I'm not as good at advertising as I thought," and this belief keeps businesses small.

Example scale: an owner at $3M/year in weight loss feared that spending past $40k/month would saturate a platform with 1B+ daily users selling into a ~$60B U.S. industry.

## When to use
Whenever an owner claims market saturation; reframe toward more/better/new before accepting any ceiling.
