A lead magnet is a low-cost or free offer that fully solves one narrow problem and, once solved, reveals another problem your core offer solves.

## The idea
- It shows who is interested. People who take a free or cheap offer now are more likely to buy a related, more expensive offer later. Someone who pays with time now is more likely to pay with money later.
- **Salty pretzels analogy:** a bar gives away pretzels. They solve a narrow problem (hunger) and reveal a new one (thirst), solved by a drink they pay for. The drink revenue covers the pretzels and makes a profit.
- It should be valuable enough that you could charge for it, and after consuming it people should want more.
- Free is not free: people pay with time. If the magnet isn't worth their time it is "overpriced" and they won't buy again. If they think it was worth their time, they'll assume your core offer is worth their money.

## A good lead magnet does four things
1. Engages ideal customers when they see it.
2. Gets more people to engage than the core offer alone.
3. Is valuable enough that people actually consume it.
4. Makes the right people more likely to buy.

## When to use
When a core offer alone doesn't engage enough leads, especially for expensive offers. Build it with the seven steps.
