An affiliate is a lead getter: an independent business with its own audience and advertising that offers your stuff to its engaged leads in exchange for money, free stuff, or both.

## Affiliates vs referrals
They look similar but differ: affiliates (1) run their own business and advertising, and (2) agree to promote you for compensation. You get them by advertising to them like customers, but the offer is different: not your product, but a fast, simple, easy way to make commissions promoting it.

## Why build an army (leverage math)
- Sell 10 customers/month at $10k each → capped at $100k/month, $1.2M in a year, then plateau.
- Same effort, sell 10 affiliates/month who each bring one $10k customer per month → revenue grows by $100k every month: ~$7.8M in 12 months and still climbing.
Each affiliate adds another ongoing stream of leads.

## Two ways to compound
Find people who never stop buying (referrals/retention) or people who never stop selling for you (affiliates). In theory an affiliate army means you never need to advertise again — as long as the deal makes sense for them. You're only as good as your goodwill with affiliate partners; arranged right, they can spend more to acquire customers because of a more compelling offer, higher profits, or both.

## Why most don't
They don't know it's possible, don't know how, or don't want to.

## When to use
Businesses whose ideal customers already gather around other businesses (complementary products/services).
