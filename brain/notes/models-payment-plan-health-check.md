After adding payment plans, total close rate should rise while the share of prospects paying in full stays the same.

**Example.** Talk to 10 leads: 3 pay in full. With downsells, 3 more buy on plans, 6 in total. You keep the up-front cash from the first 3 and add plan revenue from the next 3.

**Warning sign.** If paid-in-full numbers drop, you are moving would-be prepayers onto plans, increasing risk without adding customers. Start high (full price first) and work down only after a no.

**When to use.** Reviewing sales metrics after introducing any downsell.
