Advertise first on a platform that passes four tests, and start with the ad placements your competitors already use.

## Four criteria
1. You have used it as a consumer and gotten value, so you understand how it works.
2. You can target people on it who are interested in your stuff.
3. You know how to format ads specifically for that platform.
4. You have the minimum money needed to place an ad there.
Platforms change constantly; the criteria don't.

## Placement rule
Most platforms have multiple ad types (e.g. LinkedIn message vs newsfeed ads; Instagram feed vs stories; YouTube sidebar, mid-roll, pre-roll). Look at where others in your space place ads and start there — if they can make it work, so can you. "Replicate before you iterate."

## Action step
Pick ONE platform meeting all four criteria and start deliberately consuming the ads on it as the first step to learning to make one.

## When to use
Before the first ad dollar is spent, or when deciding whether to add a new platform.
