To make affiliates a lifelong lead source, build your offer into theirs: they give away your lead magnet, sell your lead magnet, or sell your core offer.

## 1. They give away your lead magnet with their purchase (easiest)
Your lead magnet makes their offer more valuable, so they can charge more and get more leads; you upsell your core offer and everything after. Lead magnet types:
- **Sample/trial:** personal training studio next door includes a free massage from you with every package.
- **Reveal a problem:** free or discounted posture assessment with each package (adds less value, but some will do it); then offer to solve the revealed problem.
- **One step of a multi-step solution:** e.g. step one of a massage/stretching/adjustment plan; value from one step creates fear of missing the rest. Hormozi had gyms give every new member a free nutrition consult where his products were upsold.

## 2. They sell your lead magnet
They sell anything of yours that turns their customers into yours (book, event, service, software, sample, discounted massage, workshop assessment, part one of your solution) and keep the cash — all profit, no work for them. You profit by selling your main offer for more than the lead magnet costs to deliver, with no split on the core offer. Hormozi's gyms sold an hour-long nutrition consult ($99–$199) and kept the money; letting them keep it all motivates more referrals.

## 3. They sell your core offer (hardest)
They sell your full product (or bundle it with theirs at a higher price) as an extra income stream — for some, their whole business. You split the money: upfront cash, all cash for a period, or all cash forever (Hormozi prefers forever, uncapped). They take a bigger share of LTGP, but you only deliver.

## Choosing
All three work. Hormozi ran strategy 1 twice a year as a big event and strategy 3 ongoing; other portfolio companies use 2. Treat affiliates like customers: make it make sense for their business and so good they'd feel stupid saying no.
