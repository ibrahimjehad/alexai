Almost any process breaks into something before, something during and something after, which gives you an instant three-step framework.

Because processes happen in time, you can always split them temporally: attract, convert, deliver; pre-pitch, sale, close. When you can't find your three pillars, use this to create a sequence-type pillar set ([sales-three-pillar-pitch]).

**When to use.** Building a pitch, a course outline or any named framework.
