Newcomers make up for lack of skill with volume: copy the top performer exactly at twice their activity, and only innovate after beating their numbers.

## Steps
1. Shadow the best person on the team.
2. Double their inputs (they make 200 calls, you make 400). Volume negates luck, and twice the reps means good in half the time.
3. Once you beat their numbers, then experiment. "Replicate before you iterate."

## When to use
Onboarding new reps or a founder learning a channel; also as a rule against tinkering with a proven script before mastering it.
