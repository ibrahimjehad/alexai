Recruiting is just advertising to potential employees, so every customer-getting method has a hiring equivalent.

## Mapping (customers → employees)
- Warm outreach → ask your network.
- Cold outreach → recruiting.
- Posting content → posting job openings.
- Paid ads → promoted job postings.
- Customer referrals → employee referrals.
- Affiliates → associations, guilds, listservs, etc.
- Agencies → staffing firms.
- Employees → employees (your staff recruiting for you).

## Implication
If you can get customers you can get employees, and vice versa. Build a reliable, repeatable process for both — you need both to scale. Need more talent? Do more.

## When to use
Planning recruiting for lead-getting roles; owners good at one side but not the other.
