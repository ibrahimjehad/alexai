Instead of having the same conversation a hundred times in DMs, invite everyone who messages to one weekly live group session and run the structured pitch there.

**How.**
1. Once a week, hold a live session: "I'm doing a live thing with other parents so you won't feel alone. Come attend." Send reminders.
2. Run the same structured webinar and FAQ ([sales-structured-webinar-pitch]).
3. Keep big launches (with paid traffic and the waitlist) separate; the weekly session can be organic and mops up everyone in between.
4. Route every platform to one conversion point: YouTube and TikTok bios say "DM me on Instagram"; Instagram DMs get pushed to the webinar. A single person (or AI) can point DMs to it.
5. Small ad tactic he mentions: delay the on-video call-to-action banner about three seconds after the hook; they found it brought higher-quality traffic.

**Bigger groups.** Running live calls for eight people at a time is a waste; get 50 or 100 on.

**When to use.** Founders stuck in DMs or one-on-one calls for a low-to-mid-ticket offer.
