All marketing tries to raise demand and lower (perceived) supply; the five offer enhancers do this without changing the core product.

More demand lets you sell more units; less supply lets you sell each for more. The ideal is high demand and low perceived supply. Each enhancer's job:
1. **Scarcity**: decreases supply to raise price, and indirectly raises demand through perceived exclusivity.
2. **Urgency**: increases demand by lowering the prospect's threshold to act now.
3. **Bonuses**: increase demand and perceived exclusivity.
4. **Guarantees**: increase demand by reversing risk.
5. **Naming**: re-stimulates demand and widens awareness among the target audience.

These "outside" positioning variables are often more powerful than the core offer itself. (Assumes a normal business, not one chasing mass-market share for some other strategic reason.)

**When to use.** After the core offer is built: layer these five on top before launching.
