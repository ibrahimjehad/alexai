Hormozi's supplement company was sold entirely by gym-owner affiliates: a slow first three weeks, then $400k+ weeks with no ads or sales team.

## Setup
After a year of prep, $1M+ on custom affiliate software and training, and $3M of inventory, Hormozi pitched gym owners (from Gym Launch, a natural affiliate base whose members are active adults) on selling Prestige Labs supplements, and trained them.

## Results
First three weeks: ~$150k total — at that pace it would take five years to break even and inventory would expire. Week four: ~$450k+ ($100k Monday, $110k Tuesday...). Following weeks roughly $380k–$450k each; 300+ orders/day across 400+ active affiliates. No paid ads, no sales team; the affiliates did everything, and it kept producing for years.

## The model
- **Launch:** give gym owners advertising materials to re-engage current and former members via warm outreach and content for a free 28-day challenge; sell supplements to use with the challenge.
- **Integrate:** every new member gets a nutrition orientation where the owner sells $50–$1,000 of supplements. 20 new members/month x 70% buying = 14 customers/gym/month; across ~4,000 gyms at ~$200 average order, that's large monthly revenue.
- Bulk purchase requirements and "sell three, get it free" kept affiliates invested.

## Lesson
Don't judge an affiliate launch on the first weeks; activation lags.
