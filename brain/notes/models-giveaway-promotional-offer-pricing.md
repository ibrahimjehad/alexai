Keep the actual discount small (rule of thumb 10 to 30% of gross margin) and frame it against the grand prize's stated value.

**Worked example.** Grand prize: $5,000 value, $2,000 retail. Everyone else gets it for $1,800, a 10% discount off retail. On the call, compare value to price: "$5,000 of value for $1,800", a 64% gap between value and cost. A 10% discount looks like a 64% saving.

**Note on the rule of thumb.** The transcript states the discount should equal "10 to 30% of your gross margins", but the worked example applies 10% to retail price; treat it as a modest discount that preserves margin, and test.

**When to use.** Whenever a giveaway or scholarship needs a profitable consolation offer.
