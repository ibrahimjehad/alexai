Do 100 primary advertising actions every day for 100 days straight and you will get more engaged leads — Hormozi's one promise.

## Applied to each of the Core Four
- **Warm outreach:** 100 reach-outs/day (email, text, DM, calls).
- **Free content:** 100 minutes/day making content; publish at least one piece per day on a platform; post more as you improve (short/long video, articles, podcasts, infographics).
- **Cold outreach:** 100 reach-outs/day (email, text, DM, cold calls, flyers). Lower response rates, so use automation.
- **Paid ads:** 100 minutes/day making ads (direct-response media, direct mail, seminars, podcast spots), running for 100 days at the daily budget reversed from your goal; aim for client-financed acquisition.

## Why it works
Volume is the most reliable lever: even unimproved activity yields more leads when doubled, and 100 days of reps produces the data and skill to improve.

## When to use
The "more" step of More-Better-New; any owner unsure how much activity is "enough".
