Imagine you lost every customer but one and are banned from the Core Four — all future customers must come from that one customer. Design what you'd do.

## Questions to answer (write them out with your team)
- How would you treat this customer?
- What would make their experience so valuable they send all their friends?
- What results would they need to get?
- What would their onboarding be?
- What type of customer would you pick?
Then actually do it, acting as if your advertising privileges could be revoked at any moment.

## When to use
Team workshops on customer experience; consolidating the six value levers into one exercise.
