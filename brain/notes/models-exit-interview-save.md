When someone wants to cancel, ask for an exit interview (offer to waive the cancellation fee for it), let them vent, and solve or upgrade.

**Steps.**
1. Offer: "I'll waive your cancellation fee if you come in and tell me what I could do better."
2. Let them vent; be angrier about the problem than they are. Sometimes they calm you down and save themselves.
3. If it is solvable, solve it. If they wanted more, rollover-upsell them into a higher level (many cancel a cheap tier because they wanted a premium feature).
4. At minimum, learn what went wrong and leave them with nicer things to say.

**Result.** Hormozi saves a third to half of customers who agree to exit interviews.

**When to use.** Every cancellation request in a recurring business.
