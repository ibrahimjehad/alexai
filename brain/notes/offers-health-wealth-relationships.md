The three markets that will always exist are health, wealth and relationships, because lacking any of them causes deep pain.

Pick a subgroup inside one of these buckets that scores best on the other indicators: most pain, most buying power, easiest to find, and growing. Example: a relationship coach does better serving people in the second half of life than college students. Older singles feel more pain, have more money, are easier to target, and the group is growing (more people turning 65 each year than turning 20).

**How.** Ask what you are good at within health, wealth or relationships, then who values it most (pain), can pay (money), can be found (targeting), and is not shrinking.

**When to use.** When an owner needs to pick or narrow an avatar.
