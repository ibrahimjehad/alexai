An offer is the goods and services you agree to provide, how you accept payment, and the terms of the agreement.

Business is a value exchange: money for value. The offer is what starts that trade and is the first thing a new customer interacts with, so it is the lifeblood of the business. It sits upstream of every acquisition effort: Hormozi found he could not teach any acquisition topic without first fixing the offer.

**The ladder** (offer quality drives life quality):
- No offer: no business.
- Bad offer: negative profit, no business.
- Decent offer: no profit, stagnating business.
- Good offer: some profit, an okay business.
- Grand Slam Offer: fantastic profit, freedom.

Changing the offer is one of the easiest, fastest changes a business can make compared with management or culture work, yet it moves both client count and profit.

**When to use.** At the start of any strategy engagement: write down exactly what is delivered, payment terms and conditions before touching ads or sales.
