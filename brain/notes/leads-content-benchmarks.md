Track how big the audience is and how fast it grows each month, holding your posting and ask cadence constant so the feedback means something.

## What to measure (monthly)
1. **Size:** total followers across platforms, and reach (people who saw your stuff).
2. **Speed:** month-over-month growth rate = new / starting. 1,000 to 1,500 followers is 50%; reach 10,000 to 15,000 is 50%. Going from 10 to 15 listeners is also 50%, which is progress.
- Track absolute and relative growth ("the more ways you measure, the more ways you can win").

## Rules
- You control only inputs. Pick a posting cadence and an ask cadence per platform, then don't stop; outputs only teach you if inputs are consistent.
- Example: Hormozi posted a podcast twice weekly for four years before reaching a top-100 list; by year five *The Game* was top 10 US business.
- **First post:** you've probably given value to people for years, so you can make an ask on your first post. If it doesn't work, give for a while and ask once you've earned it. Don't fear others' opinions.
- **Daily checklist:** yourself; give until they ask; any platform; current followers; every morning, 7 days; written/image/video/audio; **100 minutes a day**; as long as it takes. Keep doing warm outreach alongside: content grows the warm list that outreach works.

## When to use
Setting KPIs for a content program and judging whether it's working.
