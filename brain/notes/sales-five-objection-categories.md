Anything other than a yes after you state the price is an obstacle, and almost all of them fall into five types.

**The five.**
1. **Specifics / preference** - something about the product doesn't match what they pictured ("no one-on-one calls", "no PDF"). See [sales-preference-objection].
2. **Time** - "I don't have time", "wrong time in my life".
3. **Money** - "out of my budget", "can't afford it right now". Hormozi notes a straight "this is really expensive" is rare; people usually give another reason.
4. **Decision maker** - "I need to talk to my spouse / partner", handing authority to someone else. (Ep 910 also calls "give me a little time" a stall rather than an objection.)
5. **Avoidance** - "I need to think about it": stalling for fear of making a mistake.

**What to do.** Script an overcome for each, then use the overcome mainly as a reason to ask again ([sales-ask-the-most-times]) while keeping rapport ([sales-aaa-framework]). Separate true logistical blockers (they need a five-seater and you sell a two-seater) from these, which are not.

**Earlier view: three main obstacles (Ep 47, Ep 305).** In older episodes Hormozi says there are only three real obstacles, the same "since the dawn of time": **money** (price), **decision maker**, and **delay / stall** ("I need to think about it"). Everything else (time, "not sure about the workouts") he calls not really an obstacle, because you can get past it. The later five-type list splits out time and preference. Ep 305 explains the cause of each of the three: price means you didn't articulate value ([sales-value-is-the-problem-said-back]); decision maker usually means a mistake earlier in the sale ([sales-decision-maker-past-agreements]); a stall means they need help making a decision ([sales-think-about-it-three-questions]). Specific money closes: [sales-most-expensive-because-best], [sales-vote-with-your-dollars].

**When to use.** Building a sales script, objection-handling training and the content library reps send to prospects ([sales-objection-content-library]).
