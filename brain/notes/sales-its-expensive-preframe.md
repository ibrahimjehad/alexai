Before stating the price, say "Before I tell you the price, it's super expensive"; it sets an emotional anchor that fits every buyer's budget and gives you a breath.

**Why it works.** A wealthy prospect imagines what "expensive" means to them (often a bigger number), then hears yours and thinks "oh, fine". A less wealthy prospect braces for a big number, so yours lands softer. Either way the anchor matches their buying power. It also gives the rep, who often chokes right at the price, a pause: you breathe, they breathe, then you deliver.

**Other tactics for price choking.** In person, write the price down and slide it across, or turn a calculator toward them.

**When to use.** Any high-ticket price reveal, especially for reps uncomfortable with big numbers.
