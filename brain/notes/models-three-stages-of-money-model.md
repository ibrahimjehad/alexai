Build money models in stages that each pay for the next: attraction offers (get cash), upsells and downsells (get more cash), continuity (get the most cash).

**Stages.**
1. **Get cash**: attraction offers get more customers for less.
2. **Get more cash**: upsell and downsell offers make more money from them, faster.
3. **Get the most cash**: continuity offers maximize total spend over time.

**Target.** Make enough from one customer to acquire and serve at least two more within 30 days.

**Evolution order (how Hormozi's businesses actually developed).**
1. Get customers reliably.
2. Make customers pay for themselves reliably.
3. Make customers pay for other customers reliably.
4. Maximize each customer's long-term value.
5. Spend as much on ads as possible.
Keep improving each stage until it is reliable, financially and operationally.

**Warnings.** A bootstrapped business that tries to launch with a finished money model collapses; every Hormozi business, including Acquisition.com, started at stage one. When the money model starts working, the business starts breaking: get someone who can build and lead the team. Many different $100M money models exist; these are the ones he has used.

**When to use.** Sequencing monetization work for a client: identify which stage they are reliably at and work only on the next.
