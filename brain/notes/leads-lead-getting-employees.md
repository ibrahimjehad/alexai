Employees you train to run ads, post content and do outreach get you leads the same way you did — more of them means more leads and less of your own work.

## The idea
As lead flow grows, workload outgrows one person; solve "too much work for one person" with more people. Employees take work, but less than doing it all yourself.

## The leverage math
- Trade 40 hours of doing for ~4 hours of managing → 36 hours saved.
- Repeat: 200 hours/week of work for 20 hours of management; then hire a manager who takes ~4 hours/week to lead. Result: ~4 hours of your time for 200 hours of lead getting.
Employees create an enterprise that grows without you.

## Who to hire
People who already know how to get leads are great but cost more; if you can't afford them, hire for will and train them using this book's methods. For ground-level jobs, how you train matters more than whom you pick — advertising takes guts more than genius, and guts can be taught. Get picky only for expensive, specialized senior hires. Hire, train, keep the winners, treat them well.

## When to use
When the owner is the bottleneck on lead-getting volume (the "more" in More-Better-New).
