Let bulk or prepaid attraction offers roll automatically into a month-to-month subscription when they end, getting attraction and continuity benefits from one offer.

**Example.** "Buy six months, get six months free" auto-renews month to month after month 12; dog food "buy four months, get two free" continues monthly after six months, cancel anytime.

**When to use.** Any prepaid or bundled front-end offer for something with ongoing value.
