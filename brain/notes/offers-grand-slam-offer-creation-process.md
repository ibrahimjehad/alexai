A repeatable sequence that turns a customer's dream outcome into a differentiated, high-value bundle.

1. **Identify the dream outcome**: what the customer actually wants at the destination (lose weight, not "a gym membership"). Sell the destination, not the plane flight.
2. **List every problem**: every obstacle before, during and after using your product, in the order they arise, using the four value drivers to prompt you.
3. **Turn problems into solutions**: rewrite each obstacle as a "how to" outcome.
4. **Create solution delivery vehicles (the how)**: brainstorm every way to deliver each solution (one-on-one, group, one-to-many; DIY, done-with-you, done-for-you, etc.).
5. **Trim and stack**: (5a) cut high-cost/low-value and low-cost/low-value items; (5b) bundle what remains into one offer with each component named and valued.

Result: an offer that solves all perceived problems, gives you conviction it is one of a kind, and cannot be compared with the competitor down the street, so the buyer decides on value, not price. You only need to do this once for a product that may sell for years.

Then enhance it with scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees and naming.

**When to use.** Whenever creating a new offer or rebuilding a commoditized one.
