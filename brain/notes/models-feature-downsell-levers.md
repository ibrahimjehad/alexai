A menu of service dimensions to remove or reduce in a feature downsell, or to raise in a premium tier.

- **Time availability**: specific times vs any time; Mon/Wed/Fri vs any day; 9 to 5 vs 24 hours; 15- vs 60-minute calls.
- **Location**: one location vs all locations.
- **Cancellations**: reschedule fees vs free rescheduling.
- **Response speed**: minutes vs hours vs days.
- **Delivery speed**: wait in line vs priority; same day vs next day vs next week.
- **Service ratio**: one-to-one vs one-to-many vs many-to-one.
- **Communication**: tech support vs chat vs video call.
- **Provider qualifications**: owner vs long-time employee vs new employee.
- **Live vs recorded.**
- **DIY vs done-with-you vs done-for-you.**
- **Expiration**: forever vs X time vs specific times.
- **Personalization**: generic vs made for you.
- **Guarantee/insurance**: length (one year vs life), coverage (specific vs any bad thing), terms (unconditional vs conditional).
- **Products**: quantity, older versions, cheaper or lower-status materials.

**When to use.** Building tiers, feature downsells, or continuity bonuses (the same elements make good bonuses).
