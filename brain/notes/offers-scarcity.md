Scarcity is a fixed quantity that triggers fear of missing out; fear of loss motivates action more than hope of gain.

**Three types.**
1. Limited supply of seats or slots, overall or per period.
2. Limited supply of bonuses.
3. Never available again.

**Physical products.** Limited releases of flavours, colours, designs or sizes (e.g. "100 boxes of mint chocolate protein bars this month").
- Always sell out: better to under-order and sell out than over-order and lose the effect.
- Announce that you sold out: it is social proof, and those who missed out want it more next time.
- Repeat on a cadence, not too often; about monthly is the sweet spot for most companies Hormozi knows.
Chanel sends only one or two of each piece per store, so each item is the last or second-to-last, supporting prices far above market.

Scarcity compounds: each sell-out makes the next one faster. Keep it honest; fake scarcity destroys credibility.

**When to use.** Any offer where you can legitimately limit quantity; for services see the capacity caps.
