Strangers give you little time and need lots of incentive, so lead with something so valuable, given so quickly, that ignoring it feels stupid.

## The idea
- You can offer the core offer, a lead magnet, or both, but it must be **big and fast**. Mediocre value blends into the noise and gets ignored.
- "Give away the farm." Offer something people **actually pay for**, not just something "so good they should pay for it".
- Case: Gym Launch's first four months of cold outreach offered a "game planning session" (really a sales call) and most gyms declined. Switching to giving as much free *service* as possible **tripled take rates** and made cold outreach a monster channel. Nothing else they tested came close.
- If the offer or lead magnet isn't working, up the ante until it's so good they feel stupid saying no. They either buy or say nice things about you.

## Scripts
Keep phone and chat scripts to a page or two and cold emails to half a page. Get the first 100 conversations or 10,000 emails out before tweaking; there's no prize for the prettiest script.

## When to use
Designing the offer for any cold campaign, and first thing to change when cold response is weak.
