For small, hand-to-hand bolt-ons, phrase each recommendation so a reflexive "no" confirms it, and repeat for every extra; Hormozi says this lifts take rates from the normal 20-30% to 90%+.

**What Ep 222 adds to the basic tactic** ([models-you-dont-want-anything-else]).
- **Benchmarks.** Typical upsell uptake is 20-30%. An assumptive negative question gets 90%+ because people are so pre-programmed to refuse being sold that they say no on autopilot.
- **Bundle it into the description.** "So we'll store and condition your coat. You don't want anything else, do you?" Conditioning was the paid extra, but it was spoken as part of the service.
- **Chain it.** After each yes, find the next small fix and ask again: "Want us to take care of this loose button? This seam?" "Sure." "You don't want anything else, do you?" "No."
- **Variant on price.** "You don't want to pay full retail for that, right?" "No." "Cool, so I'll send it with this discount to the address on file?"
- **Supplement/retail version.** "These are the four supplements you'll need. You don't want to buy anything else, do you?"

**Where it fits.** Not complex or core-offer sales: small add-ons such as supplements or meals with a gym membership, a scarf with an outfit, orthotics at physical therapy, special toothpaste at the dentist, night serums with a beauty package. These "fries and Coke" items carry high margin and help liquidate acquisition cost; many brick-and-mortar service businesses leave them on the table. The full furrier story is in [models-example-fur-coat-earmuffs].

**When to use.** At checkout or right after the main purchase, in person or on the phone.
