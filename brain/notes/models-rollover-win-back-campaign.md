Contact customers 6+ months since their last purchase and offer to credit part of what they spent before toward something new.

**Steps.**
1. Pull customers with no purchase in 6+ months.
2. Look at how much each paid before; decide how much to roll over.
3. Offer it personally, as a one-time offer.

**Result.** Hormozi recorded personalized videos for 200 past customers offering $4,000 of credit to return. About 20% accepted; one day of recording added about $1.9M in annual revenue.

**Lead source variant.** Scrape contact details from negative reviews of competitors' products (where allowed), or create a place for people in your industry to complain, then rollover-upsell them.

**When to use.** Any business with a list of lapsed customers; a fast, cheap revenue lever.
