Record about 10 new main ads a week plus 30+ different opening lines, then combine them to test hundreds of variations cheaply.

## Steps
1. Each week record ~10 new main ad bodies.
2. Record 30+ alternative first sentences/questions — ~5-second call-out clips (what people see before deciding to keep watching).
3. Combine: 30 call-outs x 10 bodies = 300 variations in a few hours.
4. Find the winning call-out and apply it to all the ads.

## Why it works
The call-out is the highest-leverage, most-tested part of an ad, and it is short and cheap to produce, so mass-producing hooks maximizes learning per dollar. Hormozi calls it one of the highest-ROI ad tips he has.

## When to use
Any video/audio ad program once you can produce creative weekly.
