Money models combine four offer types: attraction (get customers), upsell (sell the next thing), downsell (sell something when they say no) and continuity (get paid again and again).

**Why all four.** Each fills the gap the previous leaves:
1. **Attraction offers** turn strangers into customers, usually with something free or discounted. Without one you get fewer customers.
2. **Upsell offers** are whatever you offer next: more, better or new. Without one you get customers but little cash.
3. **Downsell offers** are whatever you offer after a "no": change how they pay or what they get. Without them every "no" is lost revenue.
4. **Continuity offers** provide ongoing value for ongoing payments until cancelled, adding guaranteed cash month after month.

**Flexibility.** Use one, two, several of one type, or all four, in any order and combination; each can make money on its own. Hormozi's most profitable businesses use all four.

**Order he teaches and builds in.** Attraction first (if you lack customers, nothing else matters), then upsells, then downsells, then continuity.

**When to use.** As the checklist for auditing any business's monetization: which of the four exists, which is missing, which is weakest.
