Headlines tell the audience why to consume; news research points to seven components that drive interest, and a good headline uses at least two.

## The components
1. **Recency:** as recent as possible (an hour ago beats a year ago).
2. **Relevancy:** personally meaningful to the audience (nurses care about nurse news).
3. **Celebrity:** prominent people or authorities (we care what Jeff Bezos eats for breakfast).
4. **Proximity:** geographically close (the neighbour's house on fire).
5. **Conflict:** opposing ideas, people or forces (pineapple on pizza, hero vs villain, freedom vs security).
6. **Unusual:** odd, rare, bizarre (the six-fingered man).
7. **Ongoing:** developing stories with twists that people want updates on.

## When to use
Writing any hook, title, subject line, ad headline or lead magnet name. Then test variants.
