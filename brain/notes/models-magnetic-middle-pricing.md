With small, medium and large options, set the middle price to push the undecided toward the size you want.

**To sell more large:** small $5, medium $8 (instead of the "rational" $7), large $9. People who always buy small or large do not change; medium buyers now take the large.

**To sell more medium:** small $6 (instead of $5), medium $7, large $9. Most small buyers move up to medium.

**Decision rule.** Many buy small: bump them to medium. Many buy medium: bump them to large. Many buy large: raise all prices.

**When to use.** Any tiered product (sizes, packages, plans).
