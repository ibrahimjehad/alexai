A free attraction offer brings more leads, while a discounted one brings fewer leads but a higher show-up rate.

**Decision rule.** If appointment show rates are low, switch from free to a small paid discount (e.g. "$21 for 21 days" instead of free). Especially important where a no-show is expensive: doctors, lawyers, dentists.

**When to use.** Choosing between free and discounted framing for a front-end offer; diagnosing poor show rates.
