Unlike other methods, cold outreach lets you choose exactly who to target; build the list by working from the most to least accessible sources.

## The idea
You can target extremely specific people: hedge fund managers running $1B+, golf apparel retailers over $3M in sales, influencers with 50K+ monthly page views.

## Steps
1. **Lead-scraping software.** Subscribe to several tools and search your criteria. Test a sample (a few hundred per tool). If contact info is current, leads respond and they match the claimed profile, pull as many as the tool gives.
2. **List brokers.** If software can't find the audience, ask several brokers to build a list to your criteria, test their samples, and stick with those that perform.
3. **Elbow grease (manual).** Join groups and communities where your audience gathers; take contact info from directories or profile links, or message within the platform as a last resort (finding contacts outside the group avoids looking like you're milking it).

**Freshness:** anyone can search the same databases; hand-built lists are less over-contacted, so they're fresher but slowest. Outsource once you've done it yourself.

**Interest groups are the warmest cold audiences:** concentrated pools of people looking for a solution. Software can scrape them too.

Action: search "outbound lead scraping tool" or "database lead scraping", find brokers the same way, and assemble your first 1,000 names. With more time than money, start at step 3.

## When to use
Step 1 of any cold outreach campaign, and whenever response quality drops (test a new source).
