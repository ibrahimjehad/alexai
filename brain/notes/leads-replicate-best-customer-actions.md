Find the actions your best-result customers took, force new customers to repeat them, and make those actions the conditions of your guarantee.

## Steps
1. Survey customers to find those with the best results.
2. Interview them about what they did differently.
3. Identify the actions they had in common.
4. Make new customers repeat those actions.
5. Measure the improvement in average customer results (speed and outcome).
6. Match your guarantee's conditions to those success actions.

## Why the guarantee trick works
The guarantee sells more people; its conditions make them do what produces results. More success → more goodwill → more referrals. "You win, they win."

## When to use
Onboarding design, conditional guarantee design, raising average customer outcomes.
