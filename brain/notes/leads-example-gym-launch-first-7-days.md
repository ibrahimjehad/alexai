By comparing best vs average customers, Gym Launch found gym owners who ran ads and made a sale within their first seven days had triple the LTGP.

## Story
They tracked activities like speed to running first ad, speed to first sale and call attendance, then compared average and best customers. The standout: ads live and a sale in the first 7 days → 3x LTGP. They refocused onboarding on getting everyone to launch and sell in week one; average results skyrocketed, followed by more customers, testimonials and referrals.

## Lesson
Find the leading success action and make it the onboarding's first goal.
