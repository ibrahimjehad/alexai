Good selling feels good; avoiding structure, upsells or higher prices because selling feels "bad" just means selling poorly and helping fewer people.

**The argument.**
- Count what you bought last month: you probably felt sold to rarely, yet someone carefully designed how to get you to buy each one. They just did it well.
- Unstructured selling wastes the buyer's time. A coach running 3-4 hour live sessions to avoid "bro marketing" was losing half her audience before she explained how she could help; a 45-minute structured pitch raises value per second for them.
- The business must make a profit to survive and grow, and it has to take care of customers to do that. You can be in it for any reason; the business is in it for profit. You can help more people and make more money, not one or the other.
- The middle path (credited to a friend, Taki): neither a starving artist nor a soulless corporation. Give lots away free; offer a paid option for those who want more help.

**When to use.** When an owner resists webinars, scripts, upsells or price rises on moral grounds.
