Stop saying "I'm not techy" and place one real $100 ad on a platform today; the lesson is worth far more than the money.

## The idea
Saying you suck at something keeps you poorer. Hormozi avoided tech for four years, then fixed it in four hours of focused effort. Platforms spend huge sums making ads easy to place.

## Steps
1. Search "how to place a <platform> ad."
2. Place one for $100 — don't chicken out at the final step. Spend the money.
3. Then set a monthly amount you accept losing, expect to lose it (you're learning, not earning), and run the paid-ads checklist.

## When to use
Owners who have never run an ad or are paralyzed by the tech.
