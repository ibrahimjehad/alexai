Hormozi's first paid ad was ugly plain text with a local call-out and a free offer, and it turned $1,000 into ~$5,700 — showing call-out and offer matter more than polish.

## Story
In 2013, working for minimum wage at a gym owner's (Sam's) gym, Hormozi got $1,000 to test Facebook ads. The ad was all-caps text, no image or video: looking for five local (Chino Hills) residents for a free six-week challenge in exchange for using their before/after photos; click to sign up. Leads came in within hours; he called them all, booked appointments, texted a reminder an hour before, and pitched the challenge in person. With no sales skill but strong conviction, he sold 19 people at $299 (~$5,700).

## Structure visible in the ad
- Call-out: local label ("Chino Hills residents") + scarcity ("five").
- Value: free six-week challenge; reason why it's free (marketing photos).
- CTA: click to sign up.

## Outcome
The six-week challenge became the gym industry's biggest promotion for seven years, taught to 4,500+ gyms and copied by many more, driving an estimated $1.5B+ in revenue.
