Hormozi's milestone of $101,018 in personal savings (March 2017) and why the first $100K matters.

After years of money vanishing into overhead, payroll and mistakes, he hit about $100K in personal cash (not revenue or business profit). At their then cost of living (about $33K a year) it meant three years of runway even with zero income. He describes the feeling as relief more than happiness, a move from fear to security, and still the richest he has ever felt despite later having far more. Opening quote: Charlie Munger on doing whatever it takes to get the first $100,000.

Hormozi's claim: a first true Grand Slam Offer should be able to get you to your first $100K.

**When to use.** As a motivating benchmark for early-stage owners: aim the first Grand Slam Offer at banking $100K of personal security.
