Give each rep an explicit number of leads per week, a bit under their capacity, so they work every lead fully and can be held to absolute quotas.

## Steps
1. Estimate how many leads a rep can work at full capacity (e.g. 100).
2. Give them about 70%, leaving time and energy to squeeze value from every lead. Too many leads get wasted.
3. Give every rep the same number weekly, so you can set absolute deal quotas: "I give you 70 leads, you give me 7 appointments, no lead left behind."
Reps should treat their leads "like their children".

## When to use
Managing any outbound or appointment-setting team.
