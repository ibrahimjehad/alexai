Every ad or outreach promotes one of two things: your core offer (go straight for the sale) or a lead magnet (a cheaper/free first step); try the core offer first.

## The idea
- An **offer** is what you promise in exchange for something of value. A **core offer** is your main product or service exchanged for money.
- Advertising the core offer is the direct path to money and may be all you need. Try it first.
- If people need to know more before buying, which is common with more expensive products, you will usually get more engaged leads by advertising a **lead magnet** first.
- Hormozi's default when unsure in content asks: use the lead magnet, it is lower risk.

## When to use
Decide this for every campaign. Low-priced or simple offers: lead with the core offer. Higher-priced or considered purchases, or when core-offer ads are not engaging enough: lead with a lead magnet.
