A coach running a $2,500/year group program for parents of children with speech delay (~$770k revenue, ~72% margin) did every Instagram conversation herself and resisted "bro marketing".

**Starting point.** 3-4 hour unstructured live sessions with up to eight parents, ~9-10% conversion from mostly warm leads, ~10% taking the all-access back end (only mentioned at the end), ~30% buying via checkout, and a $150/month one-on-one add-on.

**Fixes, in his "back to front" order (offer, conversion, then traffic).**
- **Offer:** bill $2,500 every six months on auto-renew (he says it converts the same as yearly) with a reminder before rebilling; price one-on-one at ~5x or drop it.
- **Conversion:** a 45-minute structured webinar with slides; FAQ section in order of frequency; $2,500 at checkout vs $3,000 via a call; bonus and lower price for buying live; three-day no-sweat window for absent husbands; onboard next day; weekly group webinar for DMs.
- **Delivery:** an AI tool that suggests past answers for her team to approve.
- **Traffic:** cut her 30-50 weekly Q&A answers and 200 unposted testimonials into short clips; answer popular questions as green-screen videos (don't open with "a lot of people ask me"); funnel all platforms to Instagram DMs; run winning posts as ads.
- **Mindset:** charging more funds helping more families free ([sales-good-selling-feels-good]).
