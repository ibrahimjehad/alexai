Making a lead magnet easier to consume can raise take rates and consumption by 2-4x or more for the same work.

## By delivery type
- **Software:** works on phone and computer and in multiple formats; they pick the easiest.
- **Information:** offer every format: video, text, audio, images. (*$100M Offers* sold almost exactly a quarter each as e-book, physical book, audiobook and free video. One format alone would have lost 3-4x the readers.)
- **Services:** be available at more times and in more ways: more hours, more days, video, phone, in person.
- **Physical products:** simple to order, fast to arrive, easy to open, simple instructions (Apple's packaging needs no manual).

## When to use
Step 5 of the lead magnet process, and whenever take rate or consumption of a lead magnet is low. Package it every way you can before creating a new one.
