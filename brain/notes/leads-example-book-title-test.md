Hormozi picked the name of *$100M Leads* through head-to-head audience polls, showing how small word changes swing results.

## Results
- **Headline:** "Advertising" beat "Promotion" 69-31; "Leads" beat "Advertising" 58-42; "Leads" beat "Marketing" 54-46. Final: *$100M Leads*.
- **Image:** real photo beat cartoon.
- **Subheadline:** "How to get strangers to want to buy your stuff" beat:
  - "...more people..." (strangers won);
  - "How to get **more** strangers..." (71-29: removing one word won);
  - "How to get as many leads as you darn well please";
  - "Get strangers to want to buy your stuff" (70-30: adding "how to" won).
- He avoided "How to get strangers to buy your stuff" on purpose: that promise is sales, while the book's job ends at a raised hand.

## Lesson
You can't predict winners. Test, because one or two words can move response by a large margin.

## When to use
Show it to a client as proof that naming should be tested, and as a template for running quick A/B polls.
