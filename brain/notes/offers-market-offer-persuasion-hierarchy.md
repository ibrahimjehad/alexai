Starving crowd matters more than offer strength, which matters more than persuasion skill.

Rate each element great, normal or bad, and read left to right:
- A "great" on a higher element overpowers weaknesses below it.
- A "normal" passes the outcome to the next element.
- A "bad" stops everything unless a "great" higher up overrides it.

**Scenarios.**
1. Great market + bad offer + bad persuasion: still makes money (hot dogs to the 2 a.m. bar crowd).
2. Normal market + Grand Slam Offer + bad persuasion: makes a lot of money. This is most businesses, and the book's target.
3. Normal market + normal offer: only exceptional persuasion makes it work, the hardest path.
4. Bad market: nothing works.

**When to use.** To prioritize where to invest effort: confirm the market is at least normal, then pour effort into the offer before spending on sales training.
