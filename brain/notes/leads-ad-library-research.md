Mine platform ad libraries for long-running ads, assume they are profitable, and break down ~50 of them.

## Steps
1. Search "<platform> ad library" to find the public database of current and past ads.
2. Filter for ads that have run a month or longer; assume they are profitable.
3. For each, note the call-outs, how they illustrate the value elements, their CTAs, the exact words used and how they demonstrate claims.
4. Break down ~50 ads for a massive head start on creating winners.

## When to use
Before writing your first ads in a new market or platform, and whenever you need fresh angles.
