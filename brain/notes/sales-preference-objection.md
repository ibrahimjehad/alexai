Any objection about features (no one-on-one calls, no email support, no PDF) is a preference objection: they want your outcome delivered their way.

**What it means.** They came for the outcome but have a preconceived picture of how it should be delivered. Hormozi used to call these fit objections.

**How to handle.** Don't add the feature and don't say "no, you don't get that" (the sale ends). Ask why or what specifically they want it for, then show how your existing features already serve that intention, and why your design works better than the way they imagined ([sales-answer-questions-with-questions]). You have delivered this many more times than they have.

**When to use.** Diagnosing objections; a true logistical mismatch (they need five seats, you sell two) is different and can't be reframed.
