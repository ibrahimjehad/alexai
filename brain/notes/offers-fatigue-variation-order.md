When results fade, change the lightest elements first and the operationally heavy ones last.

**Order.**
1. Creative: ad images and video.
2. Body copy of the ads.
3. Headline / wrapper (offer name), e.g. "Free 6-Week Lean Down Challenge" to "Free 6-Week Tone Challenge", or "Holiday Hangover" to "New Year, New You".
4. Duration (6 weeks to 28 days or 8 weeks).
5. Enhancer: the free or discount component.
6. The whole money model: the sequence of offers and price points (covered in later books).

Most of the time only steps 1-3 need changing, again and again. The lower the step, the more operational drag, so exhaust the light ones first. Once an offer is monetized, rarely change it: entrepreneurs love change, but it usually just adds inefficiency.

**Getting initial traction.** Launch with the structure and headline you think most likely to work and stick with it; if several work, keep the highest-return one. Rotating between proven offers without operational drag is the strongest position ("multiple aces in the hole").

**When to use.** Whenever lead volume or conversion declines on an existing offer.
