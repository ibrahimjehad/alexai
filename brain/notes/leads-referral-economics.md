Referred customers are worth more and cost less, and word of mouth grows exponentially while the Core Four grows linearly.

## The idea
A referral = a referrer sends an engaged lead to you. Anyone can refer; the best referrers are customers.
1. **Higher LTGP** — referrals buy more expensive stuff, buy more often, and tend to pay more cash up front.
2. **Lower CAC** — a customer sent by a customer costs nothing.

## Worked numbers
LTGP:CAC of 4:1 means you spend 25% of a customer's LTGP to get the next. If every customer brings two more, effective ratio becomes 12:1 — ~8.3% of LTGP per new customer, three customers for the price of one.

## Exponential vs linear
Core Four output is roughly linear in input (double reach-outs or spend → roughly double leads). Word of mouth compounds: one brings two, two bring four, four bring eight. "Nothing scales like word of mouth." Few businesses scale this way because they lose customers faster than they gain referrals.

## When to use
Justifying investment in product quality and referral programs; modeling growth.
