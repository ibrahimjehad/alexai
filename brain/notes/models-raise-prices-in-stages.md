Launch new offers cheap to get many yeses and feedback, improve the product, then keep raising price until the extra cash from yeses no longer makes up for the lost sales.

**Steps.**
1. Price a new offer low enough to get lots of yeses.
2. Use customer feedback to improve the product.
3. As the offer becomes reliable, raise the price, and keep raising it.
4. Stop when total profit stops increasing (more revenue per yes no longer offsets the extra nos).

**When to use.** Any new offer or new market; complements the step-up rule in [leads-raise-price-every-five].
