A three-part reply pattern that lets you talk naturally with anyone and steer the conversation toward your offer.

## Steps
1. **Acknowledge** what they said by restating it in your own words (shows active listening). "Two kids and you're an accountant..."
2. **Compliment** what they told you, tied to a positive character trait if possible. "Wow, supermom. Managing a full-time career and two kids takes serious work."
3. **Ask** another question that leads where you want, toward a topic near your offer:
   - therapy/coaching: "Do you get any time for yourself?"
   - fitness: "Do you have time to get your workouts in?"
   - cleaning: "Does anyone help you keep the house tidy?"

## Why it works
People love talking about themselves and being complimented. When they feel good talking to you, they like and trust you more. It also builds the habit of finding the good in everyone. It takes practice.

## When to use
Every live reply in warm outreach, and for qualifying engaged leads from cold DMs or calls before inviting them to a call.
