A lead is simply a person you can contact, nothing more.

## The idea
People use "lead" loosely (an ad click, a phone number, a booked call, a walk-in, a subscriber, a door). Hormozi spent months failing to define it and settled on the simplest version: if you can contact them, they are a lead. A bought email list, contacts scraped from a database, the numbers in your phone and people on the street are all leads.

Precise words matter because they shape what you think, and what you think shapes what you do. Agreeing on this definition prevents chasing the wrong output.

The catch: plain leads are not enough. What you actually want are engaged leads (people showing interest).

## When to use
When setting up metrics or comparing channels, count "leads" (contactable people) separately from "engaged leads" (people who showed interest) so the numbers mean the same thing across the business.
