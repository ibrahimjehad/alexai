Companies that hire many people make easy, underused affiliates for direct-to-consumer businesses: give their staff your lead magnet as a perk.

## Examples
A free massage in every new-hire packet, or free massages for employees at lunch. Employers want to give their teams more value, it costs them nothing, you get leads, and since they're in a different business there's no competition concern.

## When to use
Direct-to-consumer and local service businesses looking for low-friction partners.
