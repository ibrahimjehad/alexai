Before raising prices, tell every prospect in the pipeline that the price is going up so they can get in at the current rate.

Never raise prices silently. Announcing it shows strength and creates honest urgency, producing a burst of cash from fence-sitters.

**When to use.** Every time you plan a price increase (which Hormozi expects most readers should do soon).
