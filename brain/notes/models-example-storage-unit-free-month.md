A storage-facility owner advertised a free first month yet collected about $127 from each new renter by selling everything the free unit made them need.

**The sequence (each offer solves the problem the previous one revealed):**
1. Free first month of storage (the attraction offer).
2. A heavy-duty lock that fits the units (about $47): a unit is useless if you cannot lock it, and cheap locker padlocks do not fit.
3. Boxes in many sizes, plus tape, labels and markers.
4. Moving help: an affiliate kickback from a local moving company, or rental of dollies, hand trucks and straps for a fee.
5. Insurance: $500 of cover is free, but customers with the facility's special lock can raise it to $100,000 for about $10/month (one product makes the next one possible).
6. One size up: people always underestimate how much they have, so the owner routinely offers the next unit size.

**Lessons.**
- "Free" can be the most profitable thing you sell if you know what customers need next better than they do.
- The owner ran 27 locations; once you truly understand how one business makes money, you see the same patterns in every business.

**When to use.** As a teaching case when an owner cannot see how a free or loss-leading front end can be profitable; prompt them to list everything a customer needs right after the first purchase.
