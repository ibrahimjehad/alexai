A referral program has three components — how you give the incentive, what you incentivize with, and how you ask; these seven combinations worked best for Hormozi.

## The seven
1. **One-sided referral benefit.** Pay your average CAC to the referrer *or* the friend ("I'd rather pay customers than a platform"). Ask for a real three-way introduction (call/SMS/email, not just a name and number), right when they buy. E.g. CAC $200 → $200 check to referrer when the friend signs up, or $200 off for the friend. Always ask for the spouse and give a household discount.
2. **Two-sided referral benefit** (Dropbox/PayPal). Split CAC between both: e.g. $500 program, $200 CAC → $100 cash to referrer + $100 off for the friend, up to three friends. Worked very well for local businesses.
3. **Ask right when they buy.** On the contract or checkout page, ask for names/numbers of people they'd like to do it with; show they get better results with a friend. Script shape: "People who do [program] with someone else tend to get [X times] the results. Who else could do this with you?" Ask *who*, not *if*.
4. **Referrals as a negotiation chip.** Trade a discount for introductions: "I can't go below [price], but if you make a three-way text intro to a few friends right now, I'll cut the [fee]." Ethical because the terms changed. If a full-price customer objects: "[Name] got [$] off for referring three friends — happy to do the same for you. Who do you have in mind?"
5. **Referral events.** 1–4 week windows with points, credits, cash or bragging rights for bringing friends; sell the selfish benefits of doing it with others using success stats. Names: Bring a Friend, Spouse Challenge, Accountability Buddy, Coach Challenge (teams of staff and customers). Good for coaching-style businesses.
6. **Ongoing referral program.** Talk about the benefits of doing it with others all the time, across content, outreach and ads. A friend's virtual event: 250k of 1M ticket buyers were referred (~33% boost).
7. **Unlockable referral bonuses.** Unlock VIP perks, courses, status, training, merch, premium support or extra service hours for referring and/or leaving a testimonial. Good if you'd rather not pay cash; can apply to both parties.

## Rules
- Make the offer so good they'd be stupid not to refer.
- Match the incentive to what you sell (free T-shirts for a T-shirt company attract T-shirt buyers; a T-shirt for an IT firm doesn't).
- Pro tip: asking for referrals at signup routinely adds 25–30% more signups in Hormozi's service businesses; at >3:1 LTGP:CAC the referral cash often covers the ad cost ("run your paid ads for free").

## When to use
After goodwill is in place, to capitalize on it; at point of sale and throughout the customer lifecycle.
