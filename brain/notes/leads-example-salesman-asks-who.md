A new salesperson at a portfolio company shattered event ticket sales records simply by asking every buyer who else they'd want to bring and requesting an introduction.

## Story
Asked how he outsold everyone, he said he did the same as everyone else, except he asked buyers who else they'd want to come with them and asked them to introduce him. Half his sales came from referrals.

## Lesson
Ask at the moment of purchase, and ask "who," not "if.
