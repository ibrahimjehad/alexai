Hormozi's own back-of-napkin summary of the whole book, useful as a map of which idea solves which problem.

## The map
1. Define what you're after: engaged leads, not just leads.
2. Turn leads into engaged leads with an offer or lead magnet.
3. The Core Four — the only four ways to let people know about your stuff:
   a. Warm outreach — reach out to people who know you; ask if they know anybody.
   b. Free content — hook, retain, reward; give until they ask.
   c. Cold outreach — lists, personalization, big fast value, volume.
   d. Paid ads — targeting, call-outs, what/who/when, CTAs, client-financed acquisition.
4. Maximize the Core Four with More, Better, New: ask what stops you doing 10x the volume and solve it; find the constraint, test until it's freed, do more until constrained again.
5. Four lead getters: customers (referrals), employees (scale advertising without you), agencies (teach you new skills), affiliates (launched and integrated).
6. Real-world execution: Rule of 100 and open to goal; the five-step one-page advertising plan.
7. The levels of advertisers and the $100M leads machine.
Promised result: more, better, cheaper, more reliable engaged leads.

## When to use
Orienting a strategist on which part of the leads system applies to a business's current problem.
