A year has 13 four-week cycles but only 12 months; billing $100 every four weeks instead of $100 a month gets the same buyers and 8.3% more annual revenue.

**Profit impact.** At a 20% margin, +8.3% revenue is about +41% profit, for no extra work. Hormozi calls this his highest value-per-word note and says it has made him millions. Bill weekly, biweekly, every 4 weeks or every 12 weeks rather than monthly or quarterly.

**When to use.** Any recurring billing; check local consumer rules on disclosure.
