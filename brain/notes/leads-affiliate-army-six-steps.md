Recruit and keep affiliates by treating them like customers through six steps: find, offer, qualify, pay, launch, integrate.

## Why it's hard
You must convince them (1) to advertise someone else's stuff, (2) to advertise *your* stuff, and (3) to keep advertising long-term. Hormozi's two affiliate-built companies (Alan, Prestige Labs) did $75M+ from ~5,000 affiliates.

## Steps
1. **Find your ideal affiliates** — businesses with warm audiences full of your customers.
2. **Make them an offer** — a new way to make money (call-out + value + CTA aimed at affiliates).
3. **Qualify them** — get them invested (buy the product and/or pay for certification).
4. **Figure out what to pay them** — what you pay for, and how much, based on maximum allowable CAC with tiers.
5. **Get them advertising** — big launches (whisper, tease, shout).
6. **Keep them advertising** — integrate your lead magnet or offer into their business.

## Getting started
Advertise your affiliate offer until you have 10–20 affiliates; get them results and use their feedback to fix the offer, terms, launches and integration; then scale by turning their results into lead magnets for recruiting more affiliates. Advertising always works — it's only a matter of efficiency, so keep going until it does.

## When to use
Designing any affiliate or partner program.
