Deliver a lead magnet as software, information, a service or a physical product; crossed with the three types that gives up to 12 lead magnets for one narrow problem.

## The four mechanisms (with Hormozi's gym-owner examples)
1. **Software:** a tool that does a job for them (spreadsheet, calculator, small app). A dashboard ranking a gym's stats against industry averages.
2. **Information:** teach them (courses, lessons, expert interviews, keynotes, live events, mistakes, tips). A mini-course on writing gym ads.
3. **Services:** do work for free (adjust a back, audit a website, apply the first sealant coat, turn a video into an e-book). Running a gym's ads free for 30 days.
4. **Physical products:** something they hold (posture chart, supplement sample, sealant bottle, boxing gloves). The book *Gym Launch Secrets*.

## Tactic
Make as many versions as you can and rotate them. It keeps advertising fresh and low effort, and shows which ones work best; results are often surprising and can't be predicted.

## Steps
1. Write your lead magnet idea.
2. Write a version of it for each of the four delivery methods (there is always a way).
3. Pick one to launch, and keep the others to rotate and test.

## When to use
Step 3 of the lead magnet process, and whenever a lead magnet fatigues and needs a fresh variant.
