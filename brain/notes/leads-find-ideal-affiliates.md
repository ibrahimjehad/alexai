Build a hit list of businesses that already have warm audiences full of people like your customers.

## Questions about your best customers
- What do they buy? Who provides it?
- Where do they go? What businesses are around those places?
- What do they like to do? Who provides those services?
- (Direct-to-consumer) Where do your consumers work, and what jobs do they have? Employers and employees can be affiliates too.
In a nutshell: who's got my leads?

## Six categories to fill in
Software, products, equipment, services, groups they belong to, events they attend. (For Alan, Hormozi listed ~200 products and services agencies use and sorted them into these.) A business that appears in multiple categories likely has lots of good leads for you.

## Action step
Make a sheet with the questions and categories; fill it in by searching online; if stuck, call customers and ask. Output: a lead list of highest-potential affiliates — that's where your Core Four effort goes.

## When to use
Step one of an affiliate program.
