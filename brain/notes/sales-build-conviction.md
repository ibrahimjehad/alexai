You can train tonality slowly or trick it fast: real conviction corrects your tone automatically, so make building belief a daily routine.

**Why tone needs it.** Hormozi (crediting Jordan Belfort) puts the words at about 10% and how you say them at about 90%: the same script makes one rep a star and another a flop, the way a comedian's script isn't funny when you read it. Trained tone control (raising pitch at the end to make a question, dropping to a near-whisper to signal importance) takes years; conviction gets you there now.

**Habits.**
1. **Reread testimonials out loud daily in front of the team.** If your business has none, find another business: without proof you can't sell hard ethically.
2. **Owners: fix everything you can about the product.** Never blame a customer for lack of success; ask what would have made the next person like them succeed and plug that hole. Then you know, alone in the mirror, that the effort merits the price. "Outwork your self-doubt."
3. **Manage your state.** Everyone has hot streaks; world-class closers never go cold because they know how to recreate the state of conviction on demand.
4. **Know the script so well it takes no attention** ([sales-script-memorization-blackout]), so your attention stays on the prospect and your tone stays natural.
5. **Fight numbness** (Ep 112). People who see results daily stop feeling them ("another 60-pounder"). Remind yourself what it means to that person and their family ("generational health").

**Burnout.** Reps rarely burn out from hearing no; they burn out from how they feel about themselves when they don't believe in what they sell. Former clients who succeeded often make the best salespeople, because they believe.

**When to use.** Daily team huddles, onboarding, and whenever a rep has the script but not the results.
