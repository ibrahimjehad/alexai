For fewer steps or newer salespeople, frame monthly payments as the bad option and let the prospect trade a bigger down payment for smaller monthly payments.

**Script structure.**
1. "Would you rather have giant monthly payments or tiny ones?" (They say tiny.)
2. "Normally it's $X; if you prepay today you get a big discount and zero monthly payments. Does that work?"
3. If they cannot afford it: "Totally get it. The more you put down now, the lower your monthly payments; let's adjust the down payment until you get a monthly amount you like."
4. Still no: check they still want it. If yes, move your chair to their side of the table and walk through options together: the sale becomes a team effort.

**When to use.** Teams without experienced closers; high-ticket offers where down payments vary.
