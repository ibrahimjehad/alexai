Using agencies the "normal" way tends to follow a predictable decline: great senior attention at first, then a junior rep, worse results, cancellation, and repeat with a new agency.

## The cycle Hormozi went through with 10+ agencies
1. They hype the leads they'll bring.
2. Onboarding feels valuable (sometimes is).
3. Their best senior rep is assigned.
4. You see some results.
5. The senior rep moves to the newest client.
6. A junior rep takes over; results suffer.
7. You complain.
8. The senior rep occasionally reappears to reassure you.
9. Results still suffer; you cancel.
10. You find another agency and repeat.
11. You wonder why results never match the first time.

## Why
You only get a fraction of an agency's attention, and it shrinks as they win new clients; your own team stays focused on you full-time.

## When to use
As a warning before hiring an agency to "just do it for us" indefinitely.
