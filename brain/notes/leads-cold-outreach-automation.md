To make up for low cold response rates, automate both crafting/delivery of messages and their distribution, trading some personalisation for scale.

## Automated delivery (record once, send to all)
- Manual: live scripted calls, a personal voice memo per lead, handwritten letters.
- Automated: pre-recorded DM voice memos, ringless voicemail drops, templated emails and texts, pre-recorded videos.
You get more engaged leads per hour even if a lower percentage respond. The cold pool is huge, so burning through it matters less.

## Automated distribution
- Manual: dialling each number, clicking send on each message.
- Automated: dialers calling multiple numbers at once; blasts of 1,000 emails, texts or voicemails at a time.

## Personalisation vs scale rule
Personalised messages get higher response. **The fewer leads in your market, the less you should automate** (only 1,000 qualifying hedge fund managers means personalise all of them; tens of millions of women 25-45 who want to lose weight allows less). The ideal is max personalisation at max volume, which technology increasingly makes possible.

## Embrace new tech
AI and similar tools keep automating human work; technology's job is more output per unit of time. Put **10-20% of effort into new, untested technology** (e.g. one day a week on a new dialer, compared to the standard one). Automate only where ethical.

## When to use
Once a cold script and offer are working manually and you need more volume.
