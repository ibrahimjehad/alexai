Prospects won't act on what you teach them before they buy, so instead of giving tactical advice, coach them by breaking the beliefs that stop them from moving.

**The mistake.** Expert founders and knowledgeable reps slip into teaching: early on, Hormozi would tell gym owners their pricing was wrong and list fixes. They took notes, didn't buy, forgot who he was, and nothing changed. He thought he was helping; he wasn't.

**The principle.** Hormozi credits Taki Moore: everyone benefits from having a belief broken; only about 10% benefit from tactics, and those few probably aren't buying from you. Breaking beliefs serves 100% of the people on calls, so it is more valuable to them and to you.

**How.** Use questions, restatements and short stories ([sales-three-things-to-say]) to lead them to the conclusion they haven't been able to reach: that they need this kind of solution. Approach it as "whether they work with me or not, this is the solution they need"; it takes your ego off your close rate and, by extension, you close more.

**When to use.** Expert-led sales (consulting, coaching, agencies) where reps or founders over-explain.
