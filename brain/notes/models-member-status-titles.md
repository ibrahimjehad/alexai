Award titles at milestones (3, 6, 12+ months: silver, gold, diamond, double diamond); customers can come to value the title more than the perks.

**Evidence.** A friend of Hormozi's found members cared more about their titles than other bonuses and introduced themselves by title.

**When to use.** Any membership; a zero-cost bonus when you cannot think of anything else.
