A gym marketing six-week challenges started offering the challenge free to anyone who became a member, plus member-only bonuses, then upsold a prepaid six months.

**Pitch.** After normal price presentation, "Want to get it for free?" Become a member and the challenge is free; members also get better class times, tanning, VIP events. Then: "Want to save even more?" A prepaid discount plus bonus for six months.

**Numbers per 100 prospects.**
- Before: 34 bought the challenge; about half (17) converted to membership weeks later.
- After: about 15 bought the challenge alone, 40 went straight into membership, and about 8 of those 40 took the six-month prepay.
- Membership sales more than doubled (17 to 40, which Hormozi calls tripling), while still collecting challenge cash and stacked prepay cash.
