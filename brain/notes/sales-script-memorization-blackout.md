Read the script aloud, black out one word, read it again, and repeat until the page is blank and you are "breathing the script".

**Steps.**
1. Print two copies: one to correct yourself from, one to read and mark.
2. Read the whole script aloud, enunciating every word.
3. Black out one word with a marker; read the whole thing again from the top.
4. Repeat, one more word each pass. You end up saying it as many times as it has words, from a blank page.

**Why.** If you sound like you're reading, you don't know the script well enough. When it's automatic, your attention goes to what the prospect is saying: "a lot of sales is in your ears, not your mouth," and "the person who speaks the most in the sale loses."

**Script is gold.** The words were fought for and are known to close. If reps say different things, you can't diagnose or coach anything: Hormozi says this describes about 95% of sales teams and is why they aren't world-class.

**When to use.** Onboarding every rep and rolling out any script change.
