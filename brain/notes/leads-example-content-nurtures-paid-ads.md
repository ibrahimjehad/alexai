Gym Launch's paid ads weakened after Hormozi stopped making gym content, revealing that free content was nurturing the demand ads got credit for.

## The story
In January 2020 the team debated why paid ads were underperforming (creative, copy, offer, pages, price). Layla asked what they had stopped doing. Answer: Hormozi had switched from gym content to general business content. A survey of gym-owner clients found **78% had consumed at least two long-form pieces of his content before booking a call.**

## Lesson
Free content warms prospects moving between cold methods and makes every channel return more, even though it's hard to attribute. Keep making content relevant to the buyer.

## When to use
When a client wants to cut content because it "doesn't convert directly", or when paid performance drops for no obvious reason.
