Let people choose between paying full price later (card on file, cancel if not satisfied and qualified) or paying a discounted price now with extra bonuses.

**Why it works.** The pay-later option removes all risk: it combines delayed payment with a satisfaction guarantee, so almost anyone agrees, and you can advertise it as free. You also get a card on file. Once they accept pay-later, you offer pay-now with a hefty discount and bonuses.

**Steps.**
1. Advertise the free (pay-later) offer with a clear promise.
2. Pay-later terms: $0 today, full price billed on a set date unless they cancel; they may cancel only if they met the conditions (e.g. attended).
3. After they accept pay-later, offer pay-now: 20 to 50% off plus bonuses (instead of the guarantee). Charge the card on file.
4. Deliver the promise, then present the upsell (have something more, better or new ready).

**Examples.**
- Real estate workshop: pay later $0, billed $500 after three days unless cancelled; pay now $299 plus recordings, a one-on-one call with an expert and printed materials; upsell $30,000 done-with-you program to close a first deal in six months.
- Lawn and hedges: pay later $0, then $600 if satisfied; pay now $369 plus lawn treatment; upsell $199/month lawn care, pitched by the rep after the work.
- Clothing: 14-day trial, $0 now, billed $149 at day 14 (must return in like-new condition to avoid billing); pay now $97 plus a matching accessory; upsell a monthly clothing subscription.

**Rules.**
- Promise a clear yes/no, measurable result you can deliver in the window (e.g. rate shoulder pain 1 to 10 before and after). Easy to track, hard to refute.
- Make the guarantee conditional: only those who did the qualifying actions (attendance, appointments, submitting data) can cancel; pick actions that maximize value.
- Too many take pay-later: deepen the pay-now discount and/or improve bonuses. Too many take pay-now: do the reverse.
- More than 10% of pay-later buyers cancelling means you promised too much, conditions are too loose or the price is too high. Some cancellations are normal; budget for them.
- Give extra attention to anyone who says they did not get the promised result before the cancellation deadline; you can often turn them around.
- Recurring version: higher ongoing rate starting in 30 days, or pay less today and keep the lower rate for good, plus bonuses.

**When to use.** Digital products, events/workshops and short-duration services; physical products with returns. Also used as a downsell for physical products or one-time services (see [models-downsell-trial-with-penalty] for the recurring equivalent).
