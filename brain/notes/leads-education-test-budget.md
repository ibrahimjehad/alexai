Reserve a fixed slice of the advertising budget — 1%, 5% or 10% — to test new campaigns, channels, pages and crazy ideas, treating it as education rather than an expense expected to return.

## The idea
An advertiser doing ~$3M/month told a room of eight-figure owners he no longer learns from courses; he learns by spending a percentage of revenue on tests. Every test teaches something; occasional winners make far more than all tests cost and raise the bar for the business and the owner. This gives permission to fail: "you either win or you learn."

## Steps
1. Pick a percentage of ad budget (1–10%).
2. Spend it on new campaigns, channels, audiences, pages or ideas without expecting a return.
3. When a test wins, double down until it breaks; then test for the next winner.

## When to use
Any business with an established ad budget that has stopped finding new winners.
