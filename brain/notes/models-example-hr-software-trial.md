An HR software company won Hormozi's business (100+ employees at Gym Launch) by waiving its onboarding fee for customers who completed the training, and charging those who skipped it.

**What happened.** Leila Hormozi took the card-on-file trial, completed the training to avoid the fee, learned the complex software, and did not want to learn anyone else's: they stayed. The product itself was not special; the trial structure forced adoption.

**Takeaway.** Penalties that push customers into the actions that create value also create switching costs. The vendor used it as an attraction offer; Hormozi prefers it as a downsell.
