Offer building is a divergent problem: generate many possible solutions to one problem rather than converging on one right answer.

**Convergent:** known variables, fixed conditions, one correct answer (e.g. 100 calls per rep, 4 calls per sale = 25 sales per rep; 110 sales needs 4.4, so 5 reps). School trains this because it is easy to grade.
**Divergent:** known and unknown variables, changing conditions, many right answers, with some far better than others. Life and business pay for this.

**Brick exercise (warm-up).** Set a 2-minute timer and list every use of a brick. Then vary its attributes (size, material such as gold or plastic, shape, holes, interlocking) and notice how many more uses appear: paperweight, doorstop, plant holder, weight, Lego, payment (gold), seat, and so on. Offers work the same way: you combine building blocks in many ways to create value for a known desire.

**When to use.** Right before brainstorming problems, solutions and delivery vehicles for an offer.
