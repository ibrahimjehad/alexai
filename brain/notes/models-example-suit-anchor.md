A suit-shop owner put Hormozi in a $16,000 suit first; after his visible shock, a $2,200 suit plus $300 of accessories felt cheap, against a $500 budget.

**Sequence.** Measured, tried on two suits; Hormozi flipped the tag (16,000) and blushed. The owner asked, "Do you care about the designer much?" On "not at all", he immediately draped a $2,200 suit on him (about one-seventh of the price, already pulled before the reaction). Hormozi bought it, then socks, a handkerchief and a shirt for another $300. He spent about five times his budget and felt fine about it.

**Lesson.** Anchor, gasp, rescue (ask about secondary features), main offer, then add-ons while the anchor still makes everything look cheap.
