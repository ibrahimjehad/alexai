Find what your most successful customers have in common, target only people like them, and you raise product results, goodwill and referrals at once.

## Why
Best-fit customers get the most value; most value → most goodwill → most referrals. "Increase the quality of the prospect and you'll increase the quality of the product." Narrower call-outs also make leads cheaper because messaging can be more specific.

## Steps
1. Identify your most successful (e.g. lowest-churn, best-result) customers.
2. Find their common traits (niche, goal, situation).
3. Change call-outs/targeting to attract people like them.
4. Sell only people who meet the new criteria.

## When to use
High churn, plateaued growth, or a customer base with a clear best-fit segment.
