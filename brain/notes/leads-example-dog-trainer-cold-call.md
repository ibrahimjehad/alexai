A sample cold call showing personalisation, social proof from a nearby client and a soft close to a scheduled walkthrough.

## Call flow (paraphrased)
1. Say their name, pause. ("Yeah, who's this?")
2. Give your first name, pause, then a specific personal compliment: you watched their videos and read their recent dog-training post, and a tip helped your own dog.
3. "Sorry, got ahead of myself." State what you do: you help dog trainers fill their books and partner with the best in each area.
4. Local proof: "We work with [business] about half an hour north of you. Heard of them?" (either answer is fine) "We got them 100 appointments in 30 days with texts and some ads. Do you offer similar services?"
5. Light assumptive close: "Then we could run the same campaign in your market. If you got a pile of new high-paying clients, you wouldn't be upset with me, would you?"
6. Book the next step: "I can walk you through the whole thing later today. Will you be around at 4?"

Contrast: opening with "Want to buy marketing services?" gets a hang-up.

## When to use
As a template for B2B cold calls or voice/video DMs; adapt the compliment, local proof and result to the niche.
