Most people dramatically underestimate the volume advertising needs; the right action in the wrong amount still fails, so input enough effort to get a readable output.

## The idea
- Engaged leads are an output of advertising inputs. People who see output as outside their control are usually making low, unreliable inputs.
- Most do a half, a third, or far less of what's needed. Example: "I reached out to 100 people over six weeks and got one customer" — the Rule of 100 means 100 per *day*, so that was ~1/42nd of the required work.
- Neil Strauss's line (paraphrased): success is doing the obvious thing for an uncommonly long time without convincing yourself you're smarter than you are.
- Use sample sizes big enough to judge: for flyers/mail, ~0.5% response is decent and 1% is a winner, so tests need thousands of pieces.

## When to use
When an owner says a channel "doesn't work" — check volume and duration of inputs first.
