Hormozi defines lifetime value as the gross profit a customer generates over their whole relationship with you (LTGP), not their total revenue.

**Formula.** LTGP = price per purchase x gross margin x number of purchases over the customer's life.
Example: $1,000/month x 90% margin x 5 months average stay = $4,500.
Indirect costs (admin, software, rent) are excluded. Other sources define LTV on revenue; Hormozi uses gross profit, sometimes labelled LTGP for clarity.

**When to use.** Any time you set an acquisition budget or compare offers: always ask for price, gross margin and average customer lifespan, and compute LTGP rather than revenue LTV.
