When a customer offers a referral, make the introduction happen right then as a three-way connection instead of just collecting a name.

## Steps
- **In person:** take the customer's phone, snap a photo of the two of you, and text it from their phone to the referral and to your own number, creating a group thread.
- **Virtual:** screenshot the video call and do the same.
- **Fallback:** at least get a three-way conversation that the referring customer starts.

## When to use
Every time a happy customer, especially a free early one, mentions someone who could use your service.
