Facebook found its top 0.1% of advertisers split-test about 11 times more creative than everyone else; volume of tests is how you find scalable ads.

## The idea
Often you can't scale an ad profitably because you can't scale a *mediocre* ad profitably. The only way to find exceptional ads is to make many more of them. More creative → better creative → more leads. "Success leaves clues": copy the testing volume of the top 0.1%.

## When to use
Arguing for a higher creative-production cadence; diagnosing ads that won't scale.
