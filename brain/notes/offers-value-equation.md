Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Perceived Time Delay x Perceived Effort and Sacrifice).

**The four drivers.**
1. Dream outcome: increase.
2. Perceived likelihood of achievement: increase.
3. Perceived time delay between purchase and result: decrease.
4. Perceived effort and sacrifice: decrease.

They map to the questions every buyer asks: What will I get? How do I know it will work for me? How long will it take? What do I have to do?

**Why division.** If time delay and effort approach zero, value approaches infinity however modest the outcome. Imagine buying a weight-loss product and instantly having the body, or signing with an agency and the phone starts ringing immediately. That is the theoretical limit to aim for.

**Perception rules.** Every driver is perceived. An improvement the prospect does not perceive adds no value, so communicate each driver clearly.

**How to use it.** Score your offer on each driver, then (a) add components that raise outcome and certainty, (b) remove waiting and effort. When two offers promise the same outcome, the outcome cancels out and the other three drivers decide which is worth more (why one beauty product is worth $5 and another $50,000).

**When to use.** For designing, auditing or pricing any offer, writing copy, and turning a list of customer problems into deliverables.
