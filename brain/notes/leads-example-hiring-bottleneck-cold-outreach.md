A portfolio company missed its cold outreach goal two quarters running; tracing the cause revealed a screening bottleneck, and fixing it doubled cold outreach sales in one quarter.

## Diagnosis chain (2021)
- Missed goal → not enough reps; they lost a rep every ~4 weeks (churn was actually below industry average).
- Hiring barely kept pace: sales hired 1 of every 4 candidates HR sent (~1 candidate/week).
- HR got ~1 qualified candidate per 10 one-to-one screens → ~40 interviews per low-skill frontline hire.

## Fix
Switch to group interviews, screen out only the obviously bad fits, and pass anyone with good work ethic and basic social skills to sales — "we can teach the rest." Within six weeks hiring outpaced churn; by quarter end cold outreach sales had doubled and made up over half of total sales.

## Lesson
The method, skills and offer were fine; there simply weren't enough people doing the outreach. Keep asking "why" until you hit the real constraint.
