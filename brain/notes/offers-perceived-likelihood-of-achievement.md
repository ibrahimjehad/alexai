The more confident a buyer is that the purchase will work for them, the more it is worth, even when the work is identical.

You would pay far more to be a plastic surgeon's 10,000th patient than their first, though the procedure is the same (and the experienced surgeon is probably faster too). A track record raises certainty and therefore price.

**How to raise it.** Messaging, proof (results, testimonials, track record), what you include or exclude in the offer, and guarantees.

**When to use.** When prospects believe the outcome is possible but doubt it will work for them specifically.
