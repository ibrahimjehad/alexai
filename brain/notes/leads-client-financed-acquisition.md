If a new customer pays you more than it cost to acquire and fulfill them within 30 days, customers finance their own acquisition and cash stops being a bottleneck to scale.

## The problem
Many businesses have LTGP > CAC overall, but the first purchase's profit is less than CAC, so LTGP trickles in over months. That cash-flow gap cripples ad scaling.

## The rule
Make gross profit collected in the first 30 days ≥ CAC + cost to fulfill. Why 30 days: any business can get interest-free money for ~30 days via a credit card. Acquire on the card, collect enough within 30 days to pay it off, and you have zero debt plus a customer who keeps paying. Repeat indefinitely — "the key to limitless scale."

## How
Immediately sell more: upsells, add-ons, prepay options, etc. — anything that raises 30-day cash. Hormozi says this is how he scaled every company for seven years past $1M/month within 12 months without outside funding.

## When to use
When ads are profitable on paper (good LTGP:CAC) but cash flow limits spend; when designing the offer stack and upsell sequence.
