Reverse each listed obstacle into solution language, usually by writing "how to" plus the opposite of the problem.

Examples (weight loss):
- "Buying healthy food is expensive" -> how to buy healthy food for less than your current grocery bill.
- "Cooking takes too much time" -> how to cook meals in under five minutes.
- "My family's needs get in the way" -> how to cook healthy food for you and your family at the same time.
- "I travel, so I won't know what to buy" -> how to eat healthy while travelling.

This gives a checklist of everything the offer must solve. Repetition across items is normal because all problems map back to the four value drivers. Commit to solving every one; how comes in the next step.

**When to use.** Step 3 of offer creation; the "how to" lines also become raw material for bullet copy and component names.
