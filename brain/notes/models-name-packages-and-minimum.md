Pre-built, named packages close more; name the top one after an aspirational status and the bottom one "the minimum".

**How.** Top tier: a status name the customer aspires to ("the whale package", "total transformation", "high roller"); think first class, business, economy. Bottom tier: "the minimum", which implies they should at least get that.

**Close.** If they reject everything else: "So nothing more than the minimum package then?" A "no" means yes to the minimum (same mechanism as "you don't want anything else, do you?").

**When to use.** Any tiered offer or downsell ladder.
