A reluctant gym prospect proposed his own deal, and it became Hormozi's most profitable attraction offer.

**Story (2013).** A gym owner, Danny, had a prospect who would not buy but wanted accountability. The prospect offered: "I pay $500, you train me for eight weeks; if I hit my goal I get my money back, and you can use my results in your marketing." He hit the goal, took the refund, then used the money to buy more training. Posting his workouts and before-and-after photos brought 13 referrals. Danny then offered it to everyone and made more money than ever.

**What Hormozi took from it.** Pay now with a chance to get the money back later. He applied it to private and group training and nutrition coaching, then put it in his ads: acquisition cost fell sharply and leads exploded.

**When to use.** As the origin case for [models-attraction-win-your-money-back]; shows that refunds paired with a follow-on purchase and public results can be net positive.
