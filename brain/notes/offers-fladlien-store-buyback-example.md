An outsized conditional guarantee that added millions in sales on a roughly $3,000 course.

Jason Fladlien sold an e-commerce course (about $2,997) with the promise: buy it, spend a set amount on ads using the methods taught, and if you do not make money, he would buy your store for $25,000. He attributed about $3M of extra sales to it and paid out only 10 buyouts ($250K), netting about $2.75M in extra sales.

**Lesson.** When customers who follow the steps are very likely to succeed, a dramatic outsized guarantee with action conditions costs little and lifts sales a lot. Illustrates the outsized-refund option in [offers-conditional-guarantees].
