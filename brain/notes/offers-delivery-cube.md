A set of "cheat code" dimensions for brainstorming many delivery vehicles for each solution.

**Dimensions.**
A. Personal attention: one-on-one, small group, one-to-many.
B. Customer effort: do it yourself, done with you, done for you.
C. If live, the medium: in person, phone, email, text, Zoom, chat.
D. If recorded, the format: audio, video, written.
E. Support speed and hours: 24/7, 9-5, within 5 minutes, within an hour, within 24 hours; which days.
F. **10x / one-tenth test:** what would I provide if they paid 10x (or $100,000)? If they paid one-tenth and I still had to make them successful, how would I do it? Stretching both ways produces very different solutions.

**Example (problem: buying healthy food is hard).**
- One-on-one: take them shopping, personalized list training, buy their food for them, orientation, real-time phone support while shopping.
- Small group: group shopping trip, group list workshop, group grocery delivery.
- One-to-many: live-streamed or recorded store tour, DIY grocery calculator, a list with each plan, grocery buddy system, pre-made Instacart carts.

Run this for every problem; ideas for one often solve another.

**When to use.** Step 4 of offer creation, and when redesigning delivery to cut cost or raise price.
