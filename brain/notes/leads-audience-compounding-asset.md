Posting free content is one-to-many advertising to your warm audience; individual posts fade, but the audience they build keeps growing and compounding.

## The idea
- Warm outreach means repeating yourself once per person. Content lets you say it once and reach everyone, so it yields far more engaged leads per hour.
- Loop: people who find a post valuable join your warm audience; they share it; people who like the share join too. Occasionally you make an offer, and the bigger the audience, the more money each offer makes.
- **Free content makes every other method work better.** Prospects reached by outreach or ads who find lots of valuable content are more likely to buy; those who find none are less likely.
- **Trade-offs:** harder to personalise, so lower response; you compete with everyone else posting; if you stand out you'll be copied, so you must keep innovating.
- Hormozi once dismissed content as disappearing noise. Seeing personal-brand businesses (Kylie Jenner, Huda Kattan, Conor McGregor's whiskey, The Rock's tequila) reach huge valuations fast, plus a famous friend's view that hate was a fair price for impact, changed his mind.
- Mindset: if someone makes more money than you, they're better at the game in some way. Drop the ego and look for the lesson.

## When to use
Add content once warm outreach is running (do both daily). It's especially important when a business relies on outreach or ads, since content is what prospects check before buying.
