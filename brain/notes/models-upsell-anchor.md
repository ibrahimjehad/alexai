Present a premium version 5 to 10 times the price of your main offer first; when the customer gasps, offer the main offer as the relief.

**Steps.**
1. **Present the anchor** (the really expensive thing), for real.
2. **Get the gasp.** Expect them to react to the price.
3. **Come to the rescue.** Ask whether they care about what makes it premium ("Do you care about the designer much?").
4. **Present the main offer.** Same primary features, fewer secondary ones; they feel relief and see the deal.
5. **Ask how they want to pay** ("Which card would you prefer?").

**Design rules.**
- Premium = 5 to 10x the main offer; after anchoring, the primary feature at about one-fifth the price looks like a great deal.
- Keep primary features identical (most people just want "a suit"); vary only secondary features (designer, material) that fewer people care about.
- Make the premium something you would genuinely be happy to deliver. A friend's made-up premium failed; a real one he actually wanted to sell tripled his profits.
- Never treat the anchor as fake. If you gloss over it, the customer never really considers it. Move on only after they pause, hesitate or ask for something else.
- The bigger the gasp, the more they buy. Have the main offer ready before the reaction. No gasp means they find the premium reasonable: ask for the card.

**Two bonuses.** Anchored customers spend more than they planned; a few buy the premium, adding outsized profit.

**Maxim.** Worse than making a $1,000 offer to someone with a $100 budget is making a $100 offer to someone with a $1,000 budget: you lose $900, not $100. Always have premium offers ready.

**Examples.**
- Lawn care: premium $1,000/week (owner's cell number, fancy mulch, natural pest control, biweekly maintenance); main $200/week (team number, generic mulch, normal pest control).
- Painting: premium $1,000 (protective packaging, 20-year insurance, gift wrap); main $200 (normal packaging, 1-year insurance, sticker).
- Newsletter: premium $199/month (all back issues, new issues, 24-hour early access); main $19/month (new issues only).

**When to use.** Any business with a core offer; build a genuine premium tier even if few buy it.
