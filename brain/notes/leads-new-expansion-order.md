When more and better stop paying, expand in the order that gets the most leads for the least work: new placements, then new platforms, then a new Core Four activity.

## When to go new
When the return from doing more or better is lower than what the same effort would earn from a new placement or way of advertising.

## Order (nine times out of ten)
1. **New placement on a platform you know** — e.g. Instagram stories, message ads and posts; YouTube shorts, long-form, community posts.
2. **Known placement on a new platform** — e.g. YouTube Shorts → Instagram Reels; Instagram messenger → Facebook Messenger. Then, once you know the new platform, add new placements on it.
3. **A new Core Four activity** on top of what you already do.
The governing rule: pick whatever gets the most leads for the least work. Then get it going, measure, and scale with more/better. Rinse and repeat.

## When to use
Planning channel expansion for a business that has optimized its current channel.
