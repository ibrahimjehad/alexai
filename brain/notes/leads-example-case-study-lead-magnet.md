Hormozi's eight-week webinar flopped, while a simple recorded case study booked his sales calendar solid overnight.

## The story
- In 2016 his mentor told him to run a webinar weekly. It took eight Sundays to build. Spending $150/day: 3 days, $450, 80 leads, zero sales. Nobody watched.
- He saw an ad for a free case study, a plain screen recording walking through a campaign, and copied the format: a ~13-minute video showing a gym's ad account, ads, spend, landing page and offer, then leads, appointments, shows, sales and the owner's revenue. It ended: if you want this done for you free, paid only from sales, book a call.
- New headline: "Free case study: how we added 213 members and $112,000 in revenue to a small gym in San Diego." The next page booked a call.
- The next morning strangers had filled Layla's calendar for a week.

## Lessons
- Give people something simple that they actually want. They didn't want the webinar; they did want the case study.
- Webinars can work (others made them work), but he lacked the skills then.
- Spend time testing the offer rather than perfecting an untested product.

## When to use
As a template for a B2B lead magnet (a case-study walkthrough with a done-for-you CTA), and as a warning against over-building before testing.
