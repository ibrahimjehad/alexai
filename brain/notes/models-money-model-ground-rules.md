Seven principles Hormozi sets before teaching any offer.

1. **Every business has a money model.** Replace "this won't work for my business" with "how will I make this work for my business?"
2. **Design your own.** Some models fit some businesses better; copying blindly disappoints. Adapt the mechanics.
3. **If a customer asks for a refund, give it.** Avoid the headache; fix your mistakes; spend the effort on finding better-fit customers.
4. **Hard selling is for weak products.** Do not convince people against their will. Make offers available when the customer has the problem; if they do not want it, find someone who does. It is a numbers game.
5. **Obey the law.** Advertising rules vary and keep tightening, especially around the word "free", sweepstakes and giveaways. Check with a lawyer.
6. **Be transparent.** State facts. If the facts are not compelling, change reality until they are, or frame them better. Never lie: a bad reputation cannot be wiped out like debt.
7. **Any offer can stand alone, at any time, in any order.** Most offers in the book can be profitable alone; in the right sequence they compound.

**When to use.** Whenever recommending aggressive offers (free, giveaways, penalties, fees): check each against these rules, especially legality, transparency and refunds.
