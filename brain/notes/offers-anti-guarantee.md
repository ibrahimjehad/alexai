State plainly that all sales are final, and give a believable reason that shows your exposure.

**Fits:** products that cannot be "un-seen" or lose value once delivered (a line of checkout code, opening lines for cold messages, your own internal funnels, ads and metrics), and high-ticket, heavily customized services.

**Why it can persuade.** Since nearly everyone offers some guarantee, refusing one draws attention. Framed as "this is so powerful and so easy to copy that once used it cannot be unused", it works as a damaging admission that implies big benefit. Example framing: "we're exposing the inner workings of our business, so all sales are final." For high-ticket work: "if you need a guarantee to jump, you're not who we want; we want motivated self-starters."

Lean in fully; do not be wishy-washy. The more real the exposure, the better it works.

**When to use.** Consumable or easily copied information, and premium services where you want only committed buyers.
