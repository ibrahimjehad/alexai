A $2M/year chiropractic business owner spending $30k/month on one platform believed he had saturated his niche — until another owner said he spent that much in one week across four platforms.

## Story
At a Q&A, the owner said he couldn't grow bigger. Questioning revealed: $30k/month on Facebook only, no tracking of click-to-close conversion, no other platforms, no content, no cold outreach. Hormozi pointed out that a $2M business on one platform could hardly have saturated a ~$15B industry. A second chiropractic owner added he'd spent $30k across four platforms the previous week.

## Lesson
Typical pattern: businesses reach $1–3M on one platform, plateau, and assume the market is tapped. The fix is the More-Better-New questions: could you advertise more? better? somewhere new? Also track overall throughput (click-to-close) so you know where the constraint is.
