The longest pause in your pitch should come right after you ask for the decision; let them process and answer instead of filling the silence.

**Evidence he cites.** Research he attributes to three meta-analyses (one from Columbia) found that waiting up to eight seconds after asking someone to buy closed about 30% more sales. In his own two-hour book-launch pitch, the longest pause was at the purchase ask.

**Why.** People need time to decide; nervous reps fill the space and cut that time off. And people believe what they say, not what you say, so their talking sells them better than yours.

**How.** Ask with a rising pitch, then pause long. Say nothing until they respond.

**When to use.** Every ask, including each re-ask after an objection.
