A neighbour built and sold a commercial trash business by offering big apartment complexes a full year of free service if they signed five paid years.

**How.** One truck, a credit card, and a bet that few competitors served large commercial sites. Complexes signed; the first year was brutal and no one, not even family, would invest. After the one-year mark "the cash came flooding", and after a few years he sold the company.

**Lesson.** Front-loaded continuity discounts win market share fast but require enough cash or credit to survive the free period; best where contracts are enforceable.
