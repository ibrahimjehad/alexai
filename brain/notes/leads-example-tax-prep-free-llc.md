A friend's $50M business (LLCs, bank accounts, incorporation for first-time founders) grew without paid ads by partnering with people who train new entrepreneurs.

## Model
- **Offer to affiliates:** help them sell more of their training by giving every one of their customers a free LLC setup (a high-cost lead magnet).
- **Launch:** a big kickoff seminar to the affiliate's audience; people take the free LLC.
- **Integrate:** affiliates build the free LLC into their core offer; his team calls each referred customer.
- **Monetize:** sell what they need next — bookkeeping, tax prep, etc.
- **True acquisition cost:** delivering the free LLC plus a percentage of each first sale to the affiliate. Zero ad spend.

## Lesson
Don't fight the big competitor head-on; plug a costly-but-valuable lead magnet into partners who already own the audience.
