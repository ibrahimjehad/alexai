Instead of asking if they want to buy, ask which of two options they prefer; both answers are a sale.

**Anything can be A/B.** Quantity (one bottle or two), start date (tomorrow or Monday), payment (cash or card), flavour (chocolate or vanilla), time slot (morning or afternoon), medium (read or listen), delivery speed (standard or overnight), size, colour, material (paper or plastic), personnel (John or Sarah), communication (call or text).

**Add a nudge** when customers are inexperienced: "This is my favourite", "X is usually a safe bet", "A lot of people love this", "Tuesday sessions are smaller if you like that", "Amy is great with high schoolers". Nudge harder on whatever you want to move faster.

**When to use.** Every upsell where two or more variants exist.
