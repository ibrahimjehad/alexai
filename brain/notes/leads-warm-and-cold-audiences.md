A warm audience has given you permission to contact them; a cold audience has not.

## Definitions
- **Warm:** people who know you, such as friends, family, followers, current and past customers and contacts.
- **Cold:** strangers, such as other people's audiences, bought or built contact lists, and access paid for on platforms.

The difference matters because it changes how you advertise. The key gap is **trust**: strangers give you less time, need more incentive, and are harder to reach and get replies from.

## When to use
Classify every lead source as warm or cold before choosing scripts, follow-up cadence, offers and benchmarks.
