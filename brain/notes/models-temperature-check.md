After two rejected downsells (or when money seems tight), ask how badly they want it on a scale of 1 to 10, and route the sale by the answer.

**Script.** "Got it. Real quick, I just want to make sure: on a scale of 1 to 10, how bad do you want this?"
- **8 or above:** "Awesome. Don't worry, we'll figure out a way to make this happen for you." Continue payment-plan downsells (they want it; the issue is cash timing).
- **7 or below:** "Why not a 10?" or "What would a 10 look like?" Then recombine features to fit their 10, or offer something different.

**Why.** No payment plan satisfies someone who does not want the thing; do not keep pushing price structures at a value problem.

**When to use.** Midway through any downsell sequence; lets you alternate between payment-plan and feature downsells.
