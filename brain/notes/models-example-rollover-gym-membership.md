His win-your-money-back program sold well but left him with no recurring revenue, until a friend showed him how to roll winnings into a year-long membership.

**Problem (2014).** A $600 fitness program with money back for hitting a goal. Winners put the $600 toward three months of membership, then churned before their first out-of-pocket payment, effectively "buy six weeks, get three months free, then leave". Revenue restarted at zero every month.

**Fix.** A friend, Justin, running the same attraction offer, had added about 100 recurring members. He rolled winnings into a year-long membership at $50/month off for 12 months, so customers started paying immediately: "What sort of business doesn't have paying customers?" They still got their money back, just over a year.

**Impact.** Hormozi calls it the missing link in his money model; it was then taught to thousands of gym owners.
