Every ad has three jobs in order: get noticed (call out), make them interested (value), and tell them what to do next (CTA).

## The framework
1. **Call out** — get the right people to notice the ad. By far the most important part.
2. **Value** — make them interested: show big benefits and tiny costs of the offer or lead magnet (built with the what/who/when framework).
3. **Call to action** — tell them exactly what to do next, then capture contact info.

Why: for advertising to work, the right people must notice the ad, consume it and get a reason to act now, then have an easy way to give permission to be contacted. Each part maps to one of those requirements.

## Training habit
Hormozi never skips or mutes ads and pays for no ad-free subscriptions: he consumes ads on purpose, studying how each business (1) calls out its ideal customer, (2) presents the value elements, (3) delivers the CTA. This turns a nuisance into continuous learning.

## Long vs short ads
The only difference is how many value angles you have room to cover. Keep the call-out (first seconds) and CTA the same; add or remove angles to fit the platform.

## When to use
Writing, reviewing or diagnosing any ad, paid or not.
