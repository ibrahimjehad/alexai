Hard-won content rules from Hormozi's own audience building.

## The lessons
1. **Say "how I", not "how to".** Share what you did and like ("my favourite way to get leads") rather than prescribing ("the best way"). Experience can't be argued with, and it avoids sounding preachy.
2. **People need reminding more than teaching.** Not everyone sees every post. Hormozi posts about his book daily, yet 1 in 5 who saw a post didn't know he had one. You'll tire of your message before your audience has seen it.
3. **Puddles, ponds, lakes, oceans.** Niche down first, then widen.
4. **Content is a sales tool.** Keep a "greatest hits" list, each labelled with the problem it solves and the benefit, so sales can send it before or after calls, especially pieces that answer common objections.
5. **Free content retains paying customers.** Buyers consume your free content more than anyone and count it in their ROI. Good free content increases loyalty; bad content lowers the perceived value of what they paid for.
6. **No short attention spans, only higher standards.** No such thing as too long, only too boring.
7. **Avoid pre-scheduling posts.** Manually posted content performs better, probably because the instant feedback makes you work harder on quality. Have a person press "post".

## When to use
As a checklist when coaching a founder or team on content strategy and execution.
