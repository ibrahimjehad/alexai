When someone refuses all your done-for-you options, invite them to a free orientation and offer a do-it-yourself product at the end.

**Script shape.** "Even though we're not going to work together on X, I still want to help. How about you come in for a free orientation on X tomorrow?" Then offer a DIY product that solves the same problem.

**Result.** Hormozi offered fitness refusers a free orientation; about half showed up and almost all of them bought supplements: money from people who would otherwise have said no.

**When to use.** Service businesses that also sell products or self-serve versions.
