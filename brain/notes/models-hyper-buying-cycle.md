When people start something new they enter a short window of peak excitement in which they spend a large amount quickly; keep making offers then.

**Examples.** Weddings, new hobbies, having a baby, moving house.

**Implication.** If your business serves these moments, do not shy away from upsells; customers expect to buy many things at once.

**When to use.** Businesses tied to life events or new pursuits; timing upsell sequences right after the first purchase.
