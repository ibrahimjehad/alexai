A portfolio company teaches chiropractors to recruit high-volume gyms as affiliates via workshops, then integrate adjustments into memberships.

## Model
- **Launch:** the gym owner promotes a ~3-hour workshop on correct exercise and posture — free, or sold at $29–$99/person, with the money split (or given 100% to the gym, so they want to repeat it). 30 attendees x $99 = $2,970 profit for the gym for a few emails and posts. The chiropractor soft-pitches services and gains a batch of patients.
- **Integrate:** convince the gym to include 1–2 adjustments with every new membership — raises the membership's value vs competitors and signals care for member safety. Every new member becomes a chiropractor lead.
- Repeated across ~30 gyms → more patients than they can handle.
