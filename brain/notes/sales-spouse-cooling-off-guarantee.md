When buyers say their partner isn't there, offer a three-day no-sweat cancellation (often legally required anyway), let them buy now, and onboard the next day so refunds stay low.

**Script shape.** "No worries, buy it now, and if over the next three days you talk to your husband and he doesn't want this for your child, just let me know and I'll refund you." (Hormozi's version spells out, half-joking, how unlikely a partner is to object to the outcome.)

**Protect against refunds.** Onboard ASAP: once people start getting value, the chance they get cold feet drops ([sales-reinforce-the-decision]).

**When to use.** Consumer offers where one partner usually buys alone (here, mostly mothers buying a children's program) and the decision-maker objection is common.
