Content is good if it satisfies the reason people started consuming it; quality is value per second, and the audience, not the creator, decides.

## The idea
- The same person who quits a 10-second video binges a 900-page book or an 8-hour series. There's no "too long", only "too boring". People don't have short attention spans; they have higher standards because better options exist.
- You can't guarantee reward, but you raise the odds by hooking the right audience (topic, headline, format), keeping them curious, then clearly delivering on the hook.
- Failing to reward: the hook promises seven ways to make up with your spouse and delivers four, or seven stale ones, or targets single men. Or dentists can't actually use your "four strategies for dentists". They won't share or return.
- **The test:** if your audience is growing, your content is good; if not, it isn't yet. Practise.

## Steps
Give more value than anyone else, keep your promises, and fully answer every question your hook raised.

## When to use
When reviewing content performance or when a client blames "short attention spans" or the algorithm.
