A portfolio PR company for small businesses had high sales but high churn and hadn't grown in years; narrowing its call-outs to its lowest-churn segment broke the plateau.

## Story
The lowest-churn customers shared a niche and a goal: raising money from investors. They were only 15% of the business, so the founder feared losing the other 85% if targeting changed — but the business wasn't growing anyway. After reviewing the data he changed ad call-outs to that narrow perfect-fit customer.

## Results
First growth in years, on track to add millions per month; advertising cost dropped (more specific messaging = cheaper leads); those customers got more value because the product fit them, and they began referring "like clockwork.
