Benchmarks for calls, email and DMs, plus a cost model; cold outreach works when customer lifetime profit is at least 3x what it costs to acquire them.

## Whoever runs it must know every metric
Hormozi's two failed attempts were run by people who didn't track metrics well; the third succeeded because the leader knew every stat.

## Example funnels
- **Phone:** 100 calls a day, 20% pick up, 25% of those take the lead magnet, giving **4 engaged leads**. Over 4 hours that's 1 engaged lead per hour. Once engaged leads convert to more profit than a rep costs, train someone else.
- **Email:** 100 personalised emails, 30% open, 10% of openers reply with interest, **3% of the list becomes engaged leads** (target ~3%). A niche high-ticket portfolio business got 4% with about a third closing, roughly 1 customer per 100 attempts.
- **DM:** 100 personal video or voice messages (say their name plus one personal line, then the standard message), 20% reply, giving 20 engaged leads; qualify with ACA and book calls.

## The rule
Succeeding means lifetime gross profit per customer is **at least 3x** acquisition cost. 3x is the floor; the portfolio company above gets 30:1+.

## Cost model (labour is nearly all the cost)
Rep paid $15/hour plus $50 per shown appointment; leads cost $0.10; 200 dials a day gives ~2 shows. Daily: $120 labour + $100 show bonuses + $20 leads = $240 for 2 shows ($120 per show). Close 33%, so **~$360 to acquire a client**. At $3,600 profit per sale that's 10:1. Then you add reps. "Boring and tedious, but brutally effective."

## Daily commitment
Yourself; hook plus lead magnet or core offer; any private channel; scraped/bought list; every day; **100 reach-outs a day**; follow up twice on day 1, twice on day 2, once on day 7; as long as it takes. **Count in hundreds:** never set a daily goal under 100 and don't stop for at least 100 days.

## When to use
Building or auditing an outbound plan: set targets per channel, calculate cost per acquisition, and check against the 3x LTGP threshold before hiring.
