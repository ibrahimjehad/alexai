Let affiliates rebrand lead magnets you already built for your audience and use them with theirs.

## The idea
Make unbranded versions (Hormozi gave gyms logo-free meal plans, grocery lists and food-prep guides) that affiliates put their logo on and hand to their customers. Their audience benefits from your work instantly and both sides get more leads. Make sure affiliates agree with how you deliver value and understand your call to action; at most a few copy tweaks are needed.

## When to use
When you have proven lead magnets and affiliates whose audiences overlap yours.
