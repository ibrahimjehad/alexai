Once someone joins month to month, offer a discount for prepaying a block of months; a small take rate raises 30-day cash a lot.

**Maths.** Offer "buy five months, get one free". If 1 in 8 new members prepays five months instead of paying one, those 8 members pay 12 months up front instead of 8: +50% 30-day revenue. The larger the discount, the more take it.

**Sequence.** Join (first month's payment), then immediately: "Want to save even more money?" Bulk prepay with discount/bonus; when the block is used up, they auto-renew into monthly continuity.

**When to use.** Right after any continuity sign-up.
