A $2 burger earning $0.25 profit becomes a $3 profit order through three upsells, showing the first offer need not be profitable.

**Numbers.**
- Burger alone: $0.25 profit; would need 10,000 burgers a day to barely survive.
- "Do you want fries with that?": +$0.75.
- "Make it a meal?" (adds a drink): profit reaches $2.00 total, 8x.
- "Supersize it for a buck more?": profit reaches $3.00 total.

**Transcript note.** The audio calls $0.25 to $3 an "11.6X increase"; the arithmetic is 12x.

**Lesson.** You make money on the second, third and fourth offers. "If you want to win, you have to figure out your version of 'Do you want fries with that?'"
