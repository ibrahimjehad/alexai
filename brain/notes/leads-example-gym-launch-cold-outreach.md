With ads crippled and clients closed during COVID, Hormozi hired a salesman from a $10M/month cold-outreach competitor to build an outbound team; it took almost a year but ended up driving over half of sales.

## The story
- July 2020: ~30% of customers closed; all three companies (Gym Launch, Prestige Labs, ALAN) depended on open brick-and-mortar businesses, and an Apple software update hurt ads.
- A rejected sales hire mentioned his gym-software employer did ~$10M/month with ~30 reps almost entirely from cold calls and emails. Hormozi gave him runway to build outbound himself (lists, software, scripts); he promised profit in 12 weeks.
- Monthly results: Sept 0 sales; Oct 2 ($32K), with the team asking to pull the plug; Dec 4 ($64K), asked again; Jan 6 ($96K); Feb 10 ($160K); Mar 14 ($224K); Apr 20 ($320K); May 30 ($480K). Today outreach brings in millions a month.

## Five lessons
1. A competitor making far more broke his belief about market size.
2. That competitor's advertising was private, so it was invisible.
3. If they could build it, so could he.
4. Set expectations: veterans said a year to scale; he guessed 12 weeks; it took almost a year.
5. After two earlier failures, working with someone who'd done it before made the difference.

## When to use
To set realistic timelines (months of little or no return) and to justify hiring an experienced outbound operator.
