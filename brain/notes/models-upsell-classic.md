Offer the solution to the customer's next problem the moment they become aware of it; current customers buy far more readily than strangers, and when timed right they upsell themselves.

**Structure.** Your core offer solves one problem and creates another; the upsell solves that next problem immediately. You cannot have a car without insurance and gas; a bike without a helmet; a clean car that lasts without sealant.

**Examples.**
- Car wash: wash, then sealant ("you'll undo the wash without it").
- Bicycle: helmet, then lights, then puncture-resistant tires.
- Exercise course: nutrition course ("you can't out-exercise a bad diet").

**Rules.**
1. **Actually do it.** Many businesses sell one thing: "You barely have a business. You have a front end." Owners who add an upsell often grow several-fold (Hormozi cites 5x).
2. **Offer the most profitable upsell first.**
3. **Make access immediate.** Something available now is worth more than the same thing later; bonus points if it is in their hands before they say yes (harder to give back).
4. **Name bundles.** One ask for a named package (fastest results bundle, transformation package, minimum package) gets several sales at once; you can peel items off later to downsell.
5. **Upsell as many times as there are problems you can solve.** Worst case is not "no"; it is that they would have said yes and you never asked.
6. **Use bonuses to create problems** the upsell solves (see [models-example-fur-coat-earmuffs]).

**When to use.** For any immediate problem revealed by the previous purchase; the first upsell every business should install.
