A ten-question template that turns any Core Four method into a concrete daily commitment.

## The template
- **Who?** Who does the work (at first: yourself).
- **What?** What you promote (free first five, value, hook plus lead magnet or core offer).
- **Where?** Which platforms or channels.
- **To whom?** Which audience or list.
- **When?** Time of day and days of the week.
- **Why?** The goal (customers, introductions, goodwill, engaged leads).
- **How?** The format or method (personalised messages using ACA, posts, calls, email blasts).
- **How much?** Daily volume (e.g. 100 reach-outs, 100 minutes).
- **How many?** Number of follow-ups or repeats.
- **How long?** Duration (until you get customers / as long as it takes).

## Filled examples in the book
- Warm outreach: yourself; first five free; phone/email/mail/SMS; your contacts; first 4 hours of the day; customers or introductions; personalised ACA; 100/day; 2-3 follow-ups; until you get customers.
- Content: yourself; give until they ask; any platform; current followers; every morning, 7 days; goodwill and engaged leads; written/image/video/audio; 100 minutes a day; as often as the platform shows it; as long as it takes.
- Cold outreach: yourself; hook plus lead magnet or core offer; any private channel; scraped/bought lists; daily, 7 days; engaged leads; calls, voicemail drops, email/text/DM blasts, video/voice messages, mail, handwritten cards; 100/day; follow-up pattern of two touches on day 1, two on day 2, one on day 7; as long as it takes.

## When to use
To turn a lead strategy into an operating plan. Fill one checklist per active Core Four method and review it against the benchmarks.
