Done-for-you services sell best with a detailed walkthrough of every step; do-it-yourself and done-with-you offers sell best with a light three-pillar pitch.

**Done-for-you (consulting, heavy implementation).** Educate in full detail: all 27 or 11 steps, the sub-points, why each matters (why aerating twice a year gives thicker grass). The message is "you can do all this yourself, or we can do it for you." Prospects come away believing this is what it takes and that they don't want to do it, so they default to you.

**DIY and done-with-you.** Hormozi treats these as the same sale: "with you" is just insurance added to a DIY product ("it's so easy you could do it alone, but we're there if you get stuck"). Here detail scares people off, so simplify to three pillars ([sales-three-pillar-pitch]).

**When to use.** Choosing the pitch format for any offer; a common mistake is using the detailed pitch on a DIY product.
