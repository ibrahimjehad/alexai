When a prospect asks for a better deal, never cut the price of the core offer; add a bonus instead.

Discounting teaches customers your prices are negotiable and puts you in a weak position. Adding value keeps the price intact, builds goodwill and keeps you in a position of strength.

**When to use.** Any negotiation or "can I get a discount?" moment, in sales calls or proposals.
