Gym Launch's ads were accidentally rejected for two weeks, yet the business still did about $500k per week from word of mouth, proving how much a great product refers itself.

## Story
In 2019 Hormozi noticed sales dip from 21 to 15 closes a week and found Facebook had rejected all ads two weeks earlier. Revenue was still ~$500k/week from referrals alone. Months later, at an event of 700+ gym owners (each paid $42k to attend), he asked who heard about Gym Launch from another gym owner — almost the entire room raised a hand; ~$30M of customers cited a referral.

## Lesson
Competitors copied offers, ads, lead magnets, landing pages, emails and scripts with little success because they thought it was about advertising. The best advertising is a happy customer; an amazing product turns every customer into a lead getter. As buyers research more and trust less, the product must delight, not just deliver.
