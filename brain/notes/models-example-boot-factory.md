A Nashville boot store tripled the price of a pair and gave two more away, turning a fair price into a free offer.

**Numbers.** One pair: $200 value. Offer: buy one pair for $600, get two pairs free. Customers still pay $600 for three $200 pairs, but the store's headline is "free". The store grew much larger over a decade while neighbouring bars and shops churned.

**Why it fits.** Customers were tourists and bachelorette groups buying matching boots for cowboy bars, mostly one purchase ever, so making that single purchase as large as possible was right.
