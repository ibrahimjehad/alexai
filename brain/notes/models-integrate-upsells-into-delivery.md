Build what you want to sell next into how you deliver the first thing, so customers ask for it.

**Examples.** Hormozi's meal plans included optional supplement suggestions, so people asked about supplements during nutrition reviews. Gym Launch's sales and marketing training suggested optional software, and gym owners bought it.

**When to use.** Designing onboarding, plans, curricula or deliverables; any time an upsell feels like a cold pitch.
