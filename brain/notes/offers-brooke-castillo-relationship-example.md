Life coach Brooke Castillo applied the problems-to-solutions step to a 90-day relationship course, showing the method works far outside fitness.

**Dream outcome:** an amazing, loving relationship in 90 days.
**Problems (sample):** no good or available options, no attraction or chemistry, poor communication, bad sex, no intellectual stimulation, no time, insecurity, unmet needs and expectations, dullness, wanting different things, too much pressure, fizzling out.
**Solutions (sample):** how to choose prospective partners in 90 days, how to be attracted to your chosen partner, how to keep the 90 days exciting, how to create chemistry, how to communicate in fun and meaningful ways, how to have great sex for 90 days, how to make time for daily connection.

**Takeaway.** Do not be fancy: get every problem down, then flip each into a "how to" solution.
