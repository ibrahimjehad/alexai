Cold outreach is one-to-one contact with strangers; it builds on warm outreach but must overcome a lack of trust, which creates three problems solved in a fixed order.

## The idea
- Use it when you want to grow faster or make lead flow more predictable, and when you've run out of warm contacts.
- Any one-to-one channel works: door knocks, cold calls, cold email, DMs, voicemails, texts. It worked 100 years ago and will survive platform changes.
- Key difference from warm: **trust.** Strangers (1) can't be contacted (you lack their details), (2) ignore you even if you can, and (3) aren't interested even when they listen.

## Solve in this order
1. **Get a way to contact them:** build a targeted list.
2. **Figure out what to say:** personalisation plus big fast value.
3. **Contact them until they're ready and able to listen:** volume through automated delivery and distribution, and more follow-ups in more ways.

Net: find many ways to reach the most qualified strangers, contact many of them many times in many ways, and overwhelm them with value up front so they show interest.

## Selling expensive things in steps
Hormozi sells expensive things better over multiple steps than on first contact. The outreach goal is a show of interest; then schedule a separate time to sell. If the lead magnet needs a second exchange to deliver, do that next; if it delivers value on its own, the next call discusses the value they got.

Cold outreach is a numbers game: once you know how much outreach produces one engaged lead, the only job is to do more.

## When to use
After warm outreach and content are running, for high-ticket or B2B offers, or for any business that wants a predictable, controllable lead source.
