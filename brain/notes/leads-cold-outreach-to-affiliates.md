If outreach costs are too high for a low-ticket product, aim cold outreach at affiliates who can sell many units, turning a low-ticket sale into a high-ticket one.

## The idea
Hormozi couldn't make cold outreach profitable for direct-to-consumer: reps are expensive and the average ticket was too low. Two approaches worked:
1. Sell affiliates a large bulk order up front, which they resell to their customers.
2. Recruit affiliates via outreach to sell to their customers for a commission after each sale.
One affiliate can be worth thousands of customers, so the economics work. (Affiliates are covered in more depth in the affiliates part of the book.)

## When to use
D2C or low-price businesses where cost per acquisition from outreach exceeds customer value.
