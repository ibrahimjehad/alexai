Ask whether they want to use the card you already have instead of asking them to pay; it removes the hidden costs of buying.

**Why it works.** It skips choosing a card, taking it out, being reminded of past purchases and the hassle of paying in a rush. Make buying easy and more people buy.

**No card yet?** Ask "Which card do you want to use?" to get one on file for the first purchase.

**When to use.** The final step of every upsell, especially right after a first purchase or membership sign-up.
