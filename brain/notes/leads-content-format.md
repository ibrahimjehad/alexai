Make content look like the best-performing content your audience already enjoys on that platform, or better-looking content will hook them first.

## The idea
People consume what resembles things they liked before. The same person presents differently on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and Tinder (Hormozi uses a well-known meme to show this; the image isn't available in audio). Format depends on the platform and the audience you want to hook.

## Steps
1. Find the best content on the platform aimed at your market.
2. Format your content to match the platform's norms first.
3. Then tweak it to hook your specific ideal audience.

## When to use
Before publishing on any platform, and when repurposing one piece across platforms.
