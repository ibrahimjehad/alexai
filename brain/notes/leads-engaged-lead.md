An engaged lead is a contactable person who has shown interest in your stuff; engaged leads are the true output of advertising.

## The idea
Examples: someone who submits their contact details on your site, a follower you can contact, a person who replies to your email campaign. Leads who show interest are the ones that matter, and getting more of them is the whole point of *$100M Leads* (the title just says "leads" because "engaged leads" would not sell).

The next question is always: how do we get leads to engage? Answer: give them something they want, a valuable offer or lead magnet.

## When to use
Make engaged leads the primary success metric for every advertising activity (outreach, content, ads). Judge a campaign by engaged leads per unit of time or money, not by raw contacts, impressions or clicks.
