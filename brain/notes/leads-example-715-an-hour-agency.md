In 2016, unable to afford an agency to run his gym ads, Hormozi paid an agency owner $715/hour to coach him while he ran the ads himself — and learned Facebook ads in about eight sessions.

## Story
New Facebook features (retargeting, interests, pixels) confused him; courses didn't help; ad costs threatened to close his gyms. Agencies wanted too much to run his ads — and he'd depend on them forever. He asked the second agency owner to show him how he'd run the account; the owner said his time wasn't for sale, then quoted $715/hour to scare him off. Hormozi accepted: one hour a week, he does the work while the expert watches and explains why, with homework between calls; four hours paid up front (~$3,000).

He came with notes and questions and recorded every call. Calls 1–2 the expert drove; 3–4 Hormozi drove; by 5–6 he understood the decisions and data; by 7–8 he no longer needed help. Total: ~8 hours and ~$6,000 for a skill that made him millions.

## Lesson
Buy the agency's knowledge, not its labor, so you aren't dependent on it.
