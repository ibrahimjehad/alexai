A 45-minute one-to-many pitch: big promise, your origin story, old way vs new way, break the three biggest false beliefs with proof, make the offer, then answer the FAQs.

**Structure.**
1. **Big promise** - why stay? What they'll get.
2. **Who am I** - quick origin story so they have a reason to listen.
3. **Core transformation, old way vs new way** - "I did this, it didn't work; then I discovered [secret/guide]; I implemented it and got this outcome, internally and externally. And it didn't just work for me, it worked for X, Y and Z" (hit each avatar).
4. **Three biggest concerns** - for each: what they think -> why that's wrong (often "I used to believe this too") -> what's right -> proof (stats, stories, live testimonials; as many forms as possible).
5. **Offer** - with a bonus and/or lower price for buying on the call.
6. **FAQ** - answer every common question, most frequent first ([sales-faq-close]).

**Rules.** It's not a special "webinar language", just clear communication. Use slides with visuals (many listen in the background). Pitch as if to a cold audience ([sales-sell-cold-to-convert-warm]). Keep delivery content and pitches separate: "sell when you need to sell, deliver when you need to deliver."

**Benchmark.** The coach's unstructured warm-audience sessions converted ~9% live; Hormozi expected structure to roughly double it.

**When to use.** Launches, weekly group calls, VSLs; whenever the same one-on-one conversation is being repeated many times.
