Capture contact info with a dead-simple landing page that matches the ad and frames the opt-in as consistent with the click they just made.

## Three layouts (pick one, start testing)
1. Headline → subheadline → opt-in field (email).
2. Headline → subheadline → image → opt-in field.
3. Headline → subheadline → image → contact fields + submit button → a few benefit bullets below the fold for those who scroll.
Simpler pages are easier to test; focus on the words and the image.

## Rules
- **Match the ad**: carry the same promise, look and language from ad to page. Avoid a "Frankenstein" experience; you want continuity from click to close.
- **Use consistency** (Cialdini): remind them of the action they just took and show how the next action aligns. Templates: "Now that you did A, do B to get the most out of A" or "Doing A makes you an A-kind-of-person; A-kind-of-people do B."
- You're not selling here — just asking if they're interested so they give a way to tell them more. That turns them into engaged leads.

## Action step
Build your first page now with a drag-and-drop builder or a cheap freelancer. Hormozi avoided it for four years out of fear and finished it before lunch once he tried.

## When to use
Any paid (or organic) traffic that should become contactable leads.
