Quote price plus a 3% processing fee, then offer to waive it if the customer provides a backup payment method (ideally ACH bank debit).

**Fee maths.** Hormozi says no one has ever refused to buy over a processing fee. 3% on top flows straight to profit: at a 10% margin, +3% revenue is +30% profit.

**Backup payment.** Recurring businesses lose a lot to failed payments (expired or changed cards, maxed cards, insufficient funds). Offer: "Want to save the processing fee? Give us a second form of payment in case anything happens to the first." Reason if asked: chasing new card details costs staff time; save us that time and we pass on the savings. Prefer ACH (Automated Clearing House, a direct bank link), the cheapest way to transact besides cash.

**When to use.** Any recurring or payment-plan business; check card-network and local surcharge rules.
