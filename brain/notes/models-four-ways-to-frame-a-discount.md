The same discount can be stated four ways; test which converts best in your market.

**Example.** A year-long service at $100/month ($1,200) sold for $900:
1. Percentage off: 25% off.
2. Absolute amount off: $300 off.
3. Free portion: three months free.
4. Total package: one year for $900 (with $1,200 crossed out).

All mean the same thing. Free portions often outperform because "free" draws more interest than a number (see [models-attraction-buy-x-get-y-free]).

**When to use.** Writing any promotional offer or ad; A/B test framings before changing the actual price.
