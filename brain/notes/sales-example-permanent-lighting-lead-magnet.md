An outdoor-lighting company (~$880k/year, ~40% from holiday lights) struggled for leads outside the holiday season.

**Diagnosis.** Once people say they want lighting, the owner closes them easily, so it's a lead problem: likely weak ad creative and a weak lead magnet ("free demo" is just a free sales pitch). Lighting is visual, so before/after creative should work.

**Fix.** Use a one-step-of-many lead magnet: light one small part for free (the path from sidewalk to door, or the driveway), "we'll do the install free, you just cover the lights", with markup on the lights and zero on labour so it breaks even. Then sell the ~10x bigger package to the share who buy the first step. Qualify by home value (address -> Zillow) and visit the highest-value homes first. "I just need them to say they want lighting; the sales process takes care of the rest."

**Seasonality.** Hormozi's preferred model: stay a lighting specialist, sell and collect commitments all year, and deliver in the ~45-60-day season (the alternative is adding services like lawn care to keep crews busy). The owner had already dropped lawn care for better margins and added permanent lighting with the same crew.
