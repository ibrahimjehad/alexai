Give each customer a personalized, expiring gift card worth about a third of the program price to hand to a friend — it gives the referrer status and creates urgency.

## Steps
1. Give every customer a gift card worth ~1/3 of their program's cost, usable by a friend who signs up with them.
2. Set expiration 7–14 days out to force use.
3. Optionally do a three-way introduction; text a picture of the card — bonus for writing the friend's name on it (personal, and a legitimate reason to ask for the friend's name).
4. Variation: sell the gift cards at ~90% off as gifts only for friends of customers — the referrer looks generous and you get paid to acquire customers.

## Why it works
"I have a $2,000 gift card, want it? I don't want to waste it" feels like a bigger deal and gives the referrer status, vs "join for $2,000 off." The incentive is your own product, so it attracts people who actually want it.

## When to use
Programs or services with meaningful ticket prices, especially local/coaching businesses.
