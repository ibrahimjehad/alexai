During the overview step, ask what they would do if they didn't buy, walk through each alternative until they admit it won't work, then confirm there is no reasonable alternative and ask permission to pitch.

**Script shape (swim school example).**
1. "If you don't do this, what would you do?" "Teach him at home." "Do you have access to a pool?" "Not really." / "Would you have time to take him three times a week?" "No."
2. Cover every other door: doing it alone, a relative, waiting. "Why is it not OK to wait? Why can't you do this on your own?"
3. "So is it safe to say there's no reasonable alternative for you right now?" "Yes."
4. "Honestly, you're the perfect fit for what we have. Can I tell you a bit about it?" "Yes."
5. Take the authority position ("No one's ever walked you through how to teach a kid to swim, right?") and pitch three pillars.

**Why.** Leaving doors open lets prospects leave through them after the pitch. Their own answers close each door, so you never argue.

**When to use.** The O step of CLOSER, especially with prospects new to buying (former referral-only businesses selling to cold leads for the first time).
