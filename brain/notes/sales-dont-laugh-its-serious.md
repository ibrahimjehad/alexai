When a prospect laughs off their problem, look them in the eye and tell them it isn't funny; laughter is a coping mechanism, and naming it makes them face why they came.

**Script** (Hormozi credits a fellow salesman). Prospect: "Ha, I'm overweight as can be." You: "Don't laugh. It's not funny. Laughter is how we cover up something we know we need to do. You know you haven't been getting where you want to go, so you cope by laughing about it." Pause. "You came here to change your life. That's what I'm going to do. Cool?" Then ask for the card.

**Why.** The mood of the call shifts instantly from banter to commitment. Hormozi calls it a gut punch: use it only if you mean it, because it works when you say it "with balls" and back it with real belief.

**When to use.** Consumer sales with emotional problems (weight, money, relationships) when the prospect deflects with humour. Use sparingly and only when you have rapport.
