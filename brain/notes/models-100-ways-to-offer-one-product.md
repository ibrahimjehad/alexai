Get as much as you can out of what you already have: it is less about having 100 products and more about having 100 ways to offer your product. "Simple scales, fancy fails."

**Example.** Personal training becomes many offers by selling one, two, three or four sessions a week (and by varying payment, guarantees, terms and bundles).

**When to use.** When an owner thinks they need new products to grow; reframe toward new offers (pricing, packaging, payment, terms) around the same product.
