Hormozi combines two checklists into the seven components of a closed deal: BANT (budget, authority, need, timing) that the seller needs from the buyer, and the 'three 10s' (trust in product, salesperson and company) that the buyer needs from the seller. He shares a friend's data that about 95% of sales came from applicants who wanted to start immediately or the next day, and Gym Launch's result from cutting its application to the four BANT questions: cost per booked call rose about 2.5x but gross sales still went up.
