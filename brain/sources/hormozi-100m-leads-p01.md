Opening section of $100M Leads. Hormozi frames the book as the sequel to $100M Offers: once you have an offer, you need leads, and advertising is simply making your stuff known to strangers. He tells the Gym Launch origin story, from a wiped-out partnership and $150K in refunds to accidentally licensing his gym lead system for $60K in a day, then scaling with ads, content and referrals. He lays out the only two ways to grow (more customers, more value per customer) and the four ways to improve leads (more, better, cheaper, reliably). The rest is the book's roadmap and Acquisition.com's model.
