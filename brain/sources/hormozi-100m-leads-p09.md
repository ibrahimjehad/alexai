Employees and Agencies. Lead-getting employees trade your doing for managing and make the business sellable; you recruit them with the internal Core Four, train them with document-demonstrate-duplicate, and measure them via payroll per engaged lead and the qualified-lead diagnostic for sales vs ad problems. Hormozi argues for better beliefs about talent (everyone is replaceable). On agencies, he describes the dependency cycle most owners fall into and his alternative: hire agencies explicitly to teach you a new method or platform within a deadline, run your team in parallel until it wins, then drop to consulting; plus a ten-point agency selection checklist and his $715/hour agency story.
