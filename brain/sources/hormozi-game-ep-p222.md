A short story from Hormozi's teenage job at a fourth-generation furrier, whose owner got customers to say yes by saying no: 'You don't want anything else, do you?' He explains it suits small hand-to-hand add-ons rather than core offers, claims it lifts upsell take rates from a typical 20-30% to 90%+, and shows variants (bundling the paid extra into the description, chaining small repairs, and 'you don't want to pay full retail, right?'). The core tactic and fur-coat example already exist from the Money Models book.
