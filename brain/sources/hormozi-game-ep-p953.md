A short call with a swim-school owner (~$300k/year, ~$12 leads) who had grown on referrals and never really sold to cold leads. Hormozi role-plays a full phone close: closing every alternative, an authority three-pillar pitch (environment, expertise, process), anchoring urgency around time, and taking payment by link while staying on the line. He also restructures the offer into a $600 12-session guaranteed front end with a halfway upsell into a six-phase back end, plus merch.
