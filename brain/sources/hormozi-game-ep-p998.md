A Scale or Fail coaching session with a founder of a $2,500 parents' coaching program (~$770k revenue, 72% margin) who resisted selling because it felt like "bro marketing". Hormozi argues people only feel sold to when selling is bad, then fixes offer, conversion and traffic in that order. Key moves: a 45-minute structured webinar (promise, story, three beliefs with proof, offer, FAQ by frequency), selling as if to a cold audience, a higher price for buying via a one-on-one call, a three-day no-sweat window for absent spouses with next-day onboarding, upselling mid-program instead of at the end, and a weekly group webinar to replace repeated DM conversations. Delivery automation and content repurposing tips are included in the example note.
