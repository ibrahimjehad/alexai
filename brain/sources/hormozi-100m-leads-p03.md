Section three introduces the Core Four (warm outreach, posting content, cold outreach, paid ads) from the warm/cold and one-to-one/one-to-many grid. The rest covers warm outreach: Hormozi's first business, the ten-step process for getting your first five customers from personal contacts, the ACA conversation framework, a 'do you know anyone' offer script built on the value equation, eleven perceived-likelihood boosters, making the first five free, hidden costs, three-way referral introductions, raising price every five customers, prepay-plus-guarantee, and the nine-word email. It ends with the daily checklist, benchmarks (about 1 customer per 100 reach-outs) and money math.
