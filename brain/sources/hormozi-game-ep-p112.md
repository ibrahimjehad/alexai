Hormozi simplifies CLOSER for teams into two yes/no questions: is the promise valuable enough for the price, and do you believe we can deliver it? He notes B2B makes value easy and belief hard, while consumer fitness sales make value the harder part, and shows how to stack value in the gap (years of life, children's health, 'generational health') along with a damaging-admission identity warning and the 'don't laugh, it's not funny' pattern interrupt. For belief he uses the testimonial wall, then diagnoses a past diet failure into missing pillars and closes with a money-back promise.
