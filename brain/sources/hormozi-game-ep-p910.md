A solo lesson on saying the right words the right way. Hormozi reduces tonality to three constants (volume, 135-185 wpm speed, articulation) and two variables (pauses, rising or falling pitch), notated with ellipsis, period and question mark, and stresses a long pause after the ask (citing ~30% more closes when waiting up to eight seconds). He covers memorising scripts by blacking out one word per read, why the top closers ask most often, the "that's exactly why you should" universal close, and a discovery method of pulling teeth for specifics, chunking up and matching problems to offers.
