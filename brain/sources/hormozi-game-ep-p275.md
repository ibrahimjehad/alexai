Hormozi explains, from a sales team game-tape review, that only three kinds of statements belong on a sales call: questions, restatements and short pre-packaged stories that break beliefs. He contrasts teaching (which prospects won't act on) with coaching beliefs, credits Taki Moore with the idea that everyone benefits from broken beliefs but only 10% from tactics, and gives weight-loss and gym-owner belief stories. Rules: don't pitch until you believe they'll say yes, refuse premature requests for the pitch, and use hard, contrarian questions to shift a call that's going nowhere.
