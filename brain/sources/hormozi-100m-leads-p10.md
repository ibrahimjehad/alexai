Affiliates and Partners, plus the Section Four conclusion. Affiliates are independent businesses that sell your stuff to their audience, offering compounding leverage (Alan's super-affiliate chain, Prestige Labs' launch). Hormozi's six steps: find who has your leads, make an affiliate offer, qualify by investment (bulk buys, paid certification at 10-20% of first-year earnings), pay against maximum allowable CAC with three tiers and product rewards, activate with whisper-tease-shout launches, and keep them advertising through three integration levels. Includes case studies (free-LLC tax firm, Prestige Labs, chiropractors via gyms), employers as affiliates, affiliate return math, and the natural order of lead getters with a 3-6 month patience rule.
