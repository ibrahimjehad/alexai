Opening of $100M Money Models. Hormozi tells how a storage-facility owner showed him that a free month of storage could earn $127 per customer through locks, boxes, moving help, insurance and size upgrades, and how his own gyms pre-sold empty buildings ($1 of ads returning $34 within 48 hours, with credit rolled into annual memberships). A marketer's advice to teach other gym owners led to Gym Launch and later Acquisition.com. The book's promise is to make so much money in a customer's first 30 days that acquisition cost stops being a problem; the rest is dedications, reading tips and promotions.
