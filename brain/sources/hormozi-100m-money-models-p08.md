Section 6 on building your own money model. Hormozi traces Gym Launch's growth from a decoy offer ($476K/month) through a classic upsell with payment-plan downsells and continuity ($1.5M/month), a menu upsell with feature downsells ($2.3M/month) and a second business ($4.4M/month by month 20). He defines three stages (get cash, get more cash, get the most cash), the order in which money models evolve, four build steps, and example models for a microgym, a newsletter and dog food. Key advice: perfect one offer at a time, measure in quarters, raise prices in stages, find many ways to offer one product, use affiliate products to fill gaps, auto-renew attraction offers into continuity, and mix offers freely.
