Opening of *$100M Offers*. Hormozi frames business as a game of bold bets with long-tail payoffs, where skill shifts the odds until you "become the house", and argues the offer is the starting point of every transaction. He tells the GymWatch story: broke after a partner drained his account and a processor froze $120K, he launched six gyms on a credit card and was saved by a Grand Slam Offer. He then defines an offer, introduces the "offer so good people feel stupid saying no" maxim, names the two problems every owner faces (not enough clients, not enough cash), and outlines the book: pricing, value, enhancing the offer, next steps.
