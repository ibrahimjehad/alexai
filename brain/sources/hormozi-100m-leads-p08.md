Opens Section Four (lead getters) and covers customer referrals. Hormozi frames leverage as getting other people (customers, employees, agencies, affiliates) to do the Core Four for you, ideally lead getters who recruit lead getters. Referrals are worth more and cost less and compound exponentially; growth from word of mouth equals referral rate minus churn. Most businesses lack referrals because the product isn't good enough and they don't ask; he gives six value levers to build goodwill (better customers, lower expectations, best-customer actions, faster wins, product improvement loop, next offer) and seven ways to ask, plus a gift-card promotion, Dropbox/PayPal cases and his own word-of-mouth stories.
