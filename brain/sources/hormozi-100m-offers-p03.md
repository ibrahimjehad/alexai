Chapter 6, the Value Equation, plus a short review request (chapter 7, dropped). Hormozi argues you should charge far more than delivery costs (up to 100x), as long as value exceeds price, citing a photography business that raised its average ticket 5x and profit 38x. He presents the value equation (dream outcome x perceived likelihood / time delay x effort and sacrifice) and explains each driver: status as the root of dream outcomes, certainty as the reason people pay experts, fast early wins, "fast beats free", and why done-for-you costs more. He stresses that perception is reality (the London Underground countdown map), favours psychological over logical solutions, and compares meditation with Xanax to show identical outcomes priced very differently.
