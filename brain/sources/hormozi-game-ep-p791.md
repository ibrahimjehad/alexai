A short lesson introducing the AAA framework for handling obstacles after the price: acknowledge, associate (generic positive, a foil just like the prospect, or authority) and ask again. The premise is that top closers ask the most times, which requires keeping rapport. Hormozi adds how to handle trap questions by answering with questions and asking for the intention behind them, and defines feature objections as preference objections ("your outcome, my way").
