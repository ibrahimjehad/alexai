A live training for high-ticket closers in which Hormozi presents his CLOSER framework in its earlier, fuller form (clarify, label, overview past pains, sell the vacation, explain away concerns, reinforce, with the R added years later), built on about 4,000 one-on-one gym sales and the shift to 'attaching a phone to the funnel' after lead costs jumped 5x. He argues a closer only says three things (questions, restatements, short belief-breaking stories), should pitch for no more than about three minutes, and should never ask for the sale before knowing the answer. He handles the three big obstacles (price as unarticulated value, decision maker via past agreements and a delayed close, and stalls via the main concern plus three questions) and adds a 48-hour post-sale reinforcement routine. The second half is his conviction framework: words are about 10% and tone about 90%, and real belief in the product corrects tone, illustrated by a mortgage-leads rep and backed by daily testimonial reading and relentless product improvement.
