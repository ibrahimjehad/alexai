Part three covers the second question of persuasion: 'What would you need to see to believe me?' Hormozi says testimonials are the most effective proof and describes choreographing the buyer's path: a customer group, a mandatory pre-appointment testimonial page with varied results, and a sales room covered in testimonials. Remaining hesitation is a limiting belief, which he handles by asking what would stop them and answering each excuse (too busy, never stick with things, unsupportive husband) with a named customer who had it and succeeded.
