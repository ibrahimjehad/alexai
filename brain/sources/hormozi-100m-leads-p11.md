Section Five: Advertising in Real Life, the Roadmap and the close. Hormozi tells how a fixed test budget changed his thinking and how Gym Launch scaled from $400k to $4M/month by testing and doubling down, then uses a failed 300-flyer campaign to argue advertising is a volume, inputs-outputs game. He introduces open-to-goal work, his early-morning habit stack, and a five-step one-page advertising plan, then lays out the levels of advertisers from warm outreach to hiring experienced executives, describes the $100M leads machine and a 5-10 year horizon, summarizes the book in a page, and ends with the many-sided die parable.
