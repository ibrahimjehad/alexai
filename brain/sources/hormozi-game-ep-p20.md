The first of a three-part series on selling expensive things. Hormozi repeats 'sell the vacation, not the plane flight' and extends it into levels of service: every tier reaches the same Maui, just by walking, bus, normal flight or private jet, and starting with the most expensive option anchors the rest. He introduces the two questions of sales: if it does what we say, is it worth the price, and what would you need to see to believe it?
