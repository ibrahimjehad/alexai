An audio-first lesson on "sales 301": setting a frame and asking what else would have to be true so every detail is congruent. Illustrated by selling gym memberships in two empty apartments framed as a discreet private gym, pitching your real price with the conviction you'd have at a tenth of it, engineering the environment (testimonial walls, exit lines) and overheard endorsements from clients. Benchmarks: ~30-35% typical B2B phone close, his 83% walk-in close and 90%+ on supplements.
