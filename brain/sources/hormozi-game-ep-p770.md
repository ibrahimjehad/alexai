A short lesson on simplifying sales scripts, credited with lifting a portfolio company's close rate from 14% to 35% in four months. Rewrite scripts below third-grade reading level and into questions; use detailed pitches for done-for-you services but a three-pillar pitch (sequence or requirements) for DIY and done-with-you; give each pillar an analogy the audience understands, then recap and ask. Before/during/after is offered as a meta-framework for finding three pillars.
