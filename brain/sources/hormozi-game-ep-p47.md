Hormozi's favourite price overcome, discovered selling gym memberships: agree it's expensive and say 'hopefully we're the most expensive because we're the best', explain the choice between being cheapest or giving people what they need, restate the value ('if it does what I say, would you believe me at $300?'), and ask whether they want to keep running the cheap cycle or solve it for good. He also shares the 'if you weren't afraid I'd send you out the door' reframe, Myron Golden's 'how long do you want not having $10,000 to be one of your problems?', a vacuum rep who memorised 356 overcomes but used three, and a phone-sales floor rule that no gasp at the price means you didn't go high enough.
