Chapters 13 (Bonuses) and 14 (Guarantees). Bonuses: an offer split into named components and stacked is worth more than the same offer presented as one thing; add bonuses instead of discounting; in one-on-one sales ask first and use bonuses to answer specific objections; an 11-point checklist for presenting bonuses; partner bonuses from other businesses (with discounts and affiliate commissions); and which assets make good bonuses. Guarantees: risk is the biggest objection, and better guarantees can lift conversion 2-4x. Hormozi covers the refund math, the "if you don't get X in Y time, we will Z" structure, four types (unconditional, conditional, anti-guarantee, implied/performance), stacking, naming the guarantee, a long catalogue of conditional guarantees, performance and revenue-share models, and how to build your own from the buyer's biggest fears.
