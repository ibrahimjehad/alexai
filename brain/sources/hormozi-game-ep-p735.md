A short starter lesson giving Hormozi's CLOSER script for new sellers: clarify why they're here, label the problem, overview past experiences (the pain cycle), sell the vacation, explain away concerns across five obstacle types, and reinforce the decision with promises kept in the first 24 hours. He adds a team tactic: arm reps with a spreadsheet of content mapped to each customer concern to send before or after calls.
