Hormozi's 'we vote with our dollars' price overcome: agree, talk about someone just like the prospect instead of the prospect, then tell a story about a teenage girl happily budgeting monthly for makeup because the purchase marked a new identity. The bridge is that a new identity brings new priorities, so the objection is a priorities issue, not a budget issue, broken down to dollars per day. He says it works best for recurring payments, where a permanent fix brings a permanent cost.
