Section 1 defines a money model as a deliberate sequence of offers, illustrated by a rental car counter turning a $19/day booking into $100/day with five offers. Hormozi explains how slow payback starves businesses of cash, sets the goal of covering acquisition cost within 30 days (credit-card float), and shows how more valuable, faster-paying customers compound growth. He introduces the four offer types (attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity), the chapter structure, and seven ground rules: every business has a money model, design your own, refund on request, no hard selling, obey advertising law, be transparent, and any offer can stand alone.
