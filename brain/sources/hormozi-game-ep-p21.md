Part two of the series digs into the first question of persuasion, value. Hormozi corrects his earlier claim that prospects nearly always agree: that holds for B2B ROI sales, but emotional sales such as weight loss need value built. His method is to paint the ideal picture (the six-months-from-now question, 'tell me more' instead of repeated whys), sell permanence, improvement and identity rather than a short-term number, 'throw stones' at their past quick fixes, and frame the purchase as the day their story changed.
