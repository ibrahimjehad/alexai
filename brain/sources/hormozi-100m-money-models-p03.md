Section 2 opens attraction offers: free, discount and $1 are interchangeable ways to turn advertising into customers. It covers win your money back in depth (results, actions or both; criteria that are trackable, result-producing and promotional; store credit spread over a longer term; required check-ins as upsell points; making everyone a winner; refund-rate thresholds of about 10% claims and under 5% baseline refunds) and giveaways (grand prize as price anchor, promotional consolation offer, eligibility questions, 3 to 7 day deadlines, refer-a-winner double prizes, urgency to enter, claim and use, and downsells for those who decline). Origin stories: a gym client who proposed a money-back deal and a fitness certification's scholarship giveaway.
