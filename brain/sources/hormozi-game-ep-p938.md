A long solo lesson on selling high ticket by going where the money is. Hormozi shows how wealth and profit concentrate (top 1% hold more than the bottom 90%; nested 80/20), then gives pricing rules: tiers 5-10x apart with ~20% uptake, close-rate bands that reveal underpricing (30-40% is about right), price bands for consumer and B2B services, and lead scoring by buying power ($17 leads worth $189 vs $5 leads worth $20). Tactics include saying "it's expensive" before the price, and stories of his first $60k high-ticket day and a 50% price rise that lifted close rates.
