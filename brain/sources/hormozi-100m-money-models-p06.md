Section 4 on downsells: change how they pay or what they get, never the same thing for less. The payment-plan downsell runs seven steps from a paid-in-full reward through financing, half now, a 1-to-10 temperature check, thirds, even payments and a free trial, with seesaw selling, payday billing, decline retries and ProfitWell churn data by billing cadence. Trial with penalty lets refusers start free if they meet success criteria (card on file, sell staying first, explain fees after, required check-ins) with follow-ups for users who like, hate or ignore it. Feature downsells remove quantity, quality or components (removing a guarantee lifted one seller's close rate from 25% to 75%), with package naming, current-customer downsells, free orientations and bartering discounts for reviews and referrals.
