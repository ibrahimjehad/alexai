Chapter on cold outreach, told through Gym Launch building an outbound team during COVID (zero sales at first, almost a year to scale, eventually over half of revenue). Strangers create three problems solved in order: build a targeted list (software, brokers, manual scraping), say the right thing (personalisation plus big fast value), and get enough volume (automated delivery and distribution, multi-channel follow-up, recycling lists after 3-6 months). It gives call/email/DM benchmarks, a cost model, the 3x lifetime-profit rule, rep lead allotments, seven benefits of cold outreach, using outreach to recruit affiliates for low-ticket products, and a daily checklist of 100 reach-outs for 100 days.
