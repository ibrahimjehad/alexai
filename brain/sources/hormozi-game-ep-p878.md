A live Q&A where several owners ask whether their bottleneck is lead generation, closing or focus. Hormozi advises mechanising a founder's working outbound into many repeatable pieces, doubling down on a winning model rather than adjacent opportunities, decommoditising by removing everything that makes competitors bad, and using a one-step-of-many lead magnet scored by home value for a lighting company whose real problem was leads. He closes with his cost-of-change rule: every process change costs ~20% performance, so only act on risk-adjusted 20%+ improvements.
