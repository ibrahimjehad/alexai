Chapters 8-10, the core of offer creation. Hormozi contrasts convergent with divergent thinking (the brick exercise) and then lays out the five-step Grand Slam Offer process: identify the dream outcome, list every problem in the order the customer meets it (using the four value drivers), turn problems into "how to" solutions, brainstorm delivery vehicles (with the delivery cube and the 10x / one-tenth test), then trim and stack into one high-value bundle. Along the way he covers the sales-to-fulfillment continuum ("create flow, monetize flow, then add friction"), why you must solve every perceived problem (the eating-out guide story), and why one-to-many assets have the best value-to-cost ratio. Worked examples are his gym weight-loss bundle ($4,351 of stated value for $599) and Brooke Castillo's relationship course.
