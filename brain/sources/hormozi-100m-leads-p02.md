Section two defines a lead (a person you can contact) and an engaged lead (one who shows interest), the true output of advertising. Through the story of a failed webinar replaced by a simple case-study video, it introduces lead magnets: complete solutions to narrow problems that reveal the next problem your core offer solves. It gives the seven-step lead magnet process: problem-solution cycle, three magnet types, four delivery mechanisms, headline testing, ease of consumption, giving away the secrets, and CTAs built on scarcity, urgency and reasons. It closes with the math showing a lead magnet can cut acquisition cost 3x.
