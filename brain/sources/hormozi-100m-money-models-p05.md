Section 3 on upsells: whatever you offer next, solving the problem the last offer revealed (burger math shows profit rising from $0.25 to $3). It covers the classic upsell ('you can't have X without Y') with tactics like 'you don't want anything else, do you?', Bam Fam, the magnetic middle and selling guarantees; the menu upsell (unsell, prescribe, A/B, card on file) learned through supplement sales, plus the Economist bundle play; the anchor upsell (premium 5 to 10x first, get the gasp, come to the rescue) from a $16,000 suit; and the rollover upsell (credit prior spend toward an offer at least 4x the credit) including win-back campaigns ($1.9M from 200 videos) and the 90%-off gift card play.
