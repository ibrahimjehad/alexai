Opening of Section 4 (Enhancing the Offer) plus chapters 11 (Scarcity) and 12 (Urgency). Hormozi uses the Arnold Schwarzenegger charity fundraiser, where cutting ticket supply and raising price added about $1M, to show how presentation changes demand without changing the product. He frames all enhancers as shifting the supply-demand curve, argues demand is fractal (about one-fifth will pay 5x) and that leaving demand unmet compounds desire ("the longer you delay the ask, the bigger the ask"). He explains why scarce experts command huge fees, then gives ethical scarcity tactics (limited releases, business/growth-rate/cohort caps, honest capacity, extreme one-on-one access, no re-entry) and four urgency tactics (rolling cohorts, seasonal promotions, pricing/bonus urgency, exploding opportunities), including cleaning the pipeline before a price rise.
