A short lesson on the belief continuum: belief in what you sell is a scale, not a yes/no, and sales is a transfer of belief. Hormozi gives two equations (close rate = skill x belief, where belief can reach 100 while skill tops out near 10; units = close rate x work ethic) and argues belief counts twice because it also drives follow-up. He urges managers to train reps on the prospect's problems rather than the product, and to spend more effort raising belief than drilling technique.
