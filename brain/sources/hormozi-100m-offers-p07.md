Final part: chapter on Naming, Section 5 (Execution: "Your first $100,000") and closing. Hormozi explains why the name decides who responds, why offers fatigue (fastest in local markets with small reachable audiences), and gives the MAGIC naming formula (Magnet/reason why, Avatar, Goal, Interval, Container) with rhyme and alliteration tips and named examples by industry. He sets the order in which to vary marketing when results fade (creative, body copy, headline/wrapper, duration, enhancer, then the whole money model) and contrasts local and national marketing. He closes with the story of reaching $100K in personal savings, an 11-point recap of the whole book, and the claim that a first true Grand Slam Offer should get you to your first $100K.
