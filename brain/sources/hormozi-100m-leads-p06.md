Run Paid Ads, parts one and two. Hormozi explains paid ads as renting reach to cold audiences, where the game is efficiency rather than reach: pick a platform, target with lookalikes and filters, build every ad from a call-out, value (the what-who-when framework built on the value equation) and a call to action, then capture contact info with a simple matching landing page. The money half covers the three phases of ad spend (track, lose, print), a 2x 30-day-cash testing rule, reversing budget from a customer goal, the LTGP:CAC ratio with its 3:1 threshold and 3x-industry-CAC diagnostic, and client-financed acquisition via 30-day payback. Lessons include not confusing sales problems with ad problems and repurposing winning free content and UGC as ads. Opens with the all-caps six-week-challenge ad story.
