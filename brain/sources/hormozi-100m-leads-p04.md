Two chapters on posting free content. Part one argues the audience, not the content, is the compounding asset, and breaks all content into units of hook, retain and reward, with topic sources, news-derived headline components, platform formatting, and lists/steps/stories for retention. Part two covers monetisation: give-to-ask ratios, 'give until they ask', integrated vs intermittent asks with lead magnet or core offer, depth-vs-width scaling, seven content lessons, monthly growth benchmarks and a daily checklist. Stories include 10x content volume driving 10x growth and the finding that 78% of Gym Launch clients had consumed content before booking.
